<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Acquisition">Acquisition</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Class_Hall_set?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Acquisition"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

</p>
