'''Dream Wardens''' is the sixth and final Major Faction of ''[[World of Warcraft: Dragonflight]]''.

==Faction description==
:A coalition of various races ready to defend the Emerald Dream against any and all threats.

==Reputation sources==
There are numerous methods of gaining reputation with the Dream Wardens:
*[[Guardians of the Dream campaign]] chapters (2500 per chapter)
*World quests (50 each)
*[[A Worthy Ally: Dream Wardens]] (500)
*[[Superbloom]] (1000 for first of the week)
*One-time treasures (200 each)
*Six rare-elites: [[Envoy of Winter]], [[Firebrand Fystia]], [[Greedy Gessie]], [[Reefbreaker Moruud]], [[Nuoberon]], [[Raszageth's Last Breath]] (50 each)
*One-off quests (250-1500 rep each)
