<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Tribe?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="War_of_the_Ancients">War of the Ancients</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Tribe?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: War of the Ancients"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Highmountain are descendants of the hero <a href="/wiki/Huln_Highmountain" title="Huln Highmountain">Huln Highmountain</a>, also known as "Huln of the Eagle Spear" and the members of the tribes he united. During the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ancients" title="War of the Ancients">War of the Ancients</a>, 10,000 years ago, he united the warring <a href="/wiki/Rivermane_tribe" title="Rivermane tribe">Rivermane</a>, <a href="/wiki/Bloodtotem_tribe" title="Bloodtotem tribe">Bloodtotem</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Skyhorn_tribe" title="Skyhorn tribe">Skyhorn</a> tribes of his homeland under the banner of the Highmountain.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Huln was chosen by <a href="/wiki/Korialstrasz" title="Korialstrasz">Krasus</a> to lead his people against the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> alongside <a href="/wiki/Unng_Ak" title="Unng Ak">Unng Ak</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Furbolg" title="Furbolg">furbolgs</a> and <a href="/wiki/Dungard_Ironcutter" title="Dungard Ironcutter">Dungard Ironcutter</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Earthen" title="Earthen">earthen</a>. For his bravery during the war, Huln received a blessing from the <a href="/wiki/Wild_God" title="Wild God">Wild God</a> <a href="/wiki/Cenarius" title="Cenarius">Cenarius</a>, so that his people would forever have the demigod's favor: the <a href="/wiki/Horns_of_Eche%27ro" class="mw-redirect" title="Horns of Eche&#39;ro">Horns of Eche'ro</a>, which transformed the horns of Huln into moose-like antlers.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the war, Huln returned to his homeland and used a <a href="/wiki/Titan" title="Titan">titan</a> artifact known as the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_mace_1h_titanpillar_b_01.png/16px-Inv_mace_1h_titanpillar_b_01.png?be4cfc" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Hammer_of_Khaz%27goroth" title="Hammer of Khaz&#39;goroth"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Hammer of Khaz&#39;goroth is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Hammer of Khaz'goroth<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> to banish the traitorous <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Aspect" class="mw-redirect" title="Dragon Aspect">Dragon Aspect</a> <a href="/wiki/Deathwing" title="Deathwing">Deathwing</a> and free his <a href="/wiki/Drogbar" title="Drogbar">drogbar</a> slaves as well as <a href="/wiki/Ebyssian" title="Ebyssian">a single uncorrupted black dragon egg</a>. In his honor, the four tribes named the region "<a href="/wiki/Highmountain" title="Highmountain">Highmountain</a>".<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legion">Legion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Tribe?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Legion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:202px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Thunder_totem_concept.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Thunder_totem_concept.jpg/200px-Thunder_totem_concept.jpg?7229c4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="200" height="219" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="751" data-file-height="823" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Thunder_totem_concept.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Thunder Totem</div></div></div>
<p>When the <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elves</a> who founded the <a href="/wiki/Unseen_Path" title="Unseen Path">Unseen Path</a> left <a href="/wiki/Mount_Hyjal" title="Mount Hyjal">Mount Hyjal</a> to return to the Broken Isles, the Highmountain <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_tauren" title="Highmountain tauren">tauren</a> recognized the nobility of their cause and offered them friendship. It was the descendants of <a href="/wiki/Huln_Highmountain" title="Huln Highmountain">Huln Highmountain</a> who led the Unseen Path to <a href="/wiki/Talon_Peak" class="mw-redirect" title="Talon Peak">Talon Peak</a> where they built their base known as <a href="/wiki/Trueshot_Lodge" title="Trueshot Lodge">Trueshot Lodge</a>. Since the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>'s return, the Highmountain have helped restore the lodge, such as replacing two damaged watch towers with tauren structures.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Millennia later, Huln's descendants continue to live in <a href="/wiki/Thunder_Totem" title="Thunder Totem">Thunder Totem</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>. With the death of <a href="/wiki/Ulan_Highmountain" title="Ulan Highmountain">High Chieftain Ulan</a>, his daughter <a href="/wiki/Mayla_Highmountain" title="Mayla Highmountain">Mayla</a> has become the High Chieftain of the accord between Highmountain, Rivermane, Skyhorn and Bloodtotem. Unfortunately, the theft of the Hammer of Khaz'goroth by <a href="/wiki/Dargrul" title="Dargrul">Dargrul the Underking</a> and his drogbar has shattered the unity of the four tribes. Despite the attempts at a reunion of the tribes by an <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurer</a>, the Bloodtotem betrayed the other tribes by joining the Burning Legion. In their place, the drogbar <a href="/wiki/Navarrogg" title="Navarrogg">Navarrogg</a> and his <a href="/wiki/Stonedark_clan" title="Stonedark clan">Stonedark clan</a> joined the Highmountain's cause instead.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Joining_the_Horde">Joining the Horde</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Tribe?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Joining the Horde"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>After corresponding with Chieftain <a href="/wiki/Baine_Bloodhoof" title="Baine Bloodhoof">Baine Bloodhoof</a> in the aftermath of the <a href="/wiki/Argus_Campaign" title="Argus Campaign">Argus Campaign</a>, <a href="/wiki/Mayla_Highmountain" title="Mayla Highmountain">Mayla Highmountain</a> accepted an invitation to attend a feast in <a href="/wiki/Thunder_Bluff" title="Thunder Bluff">Thunder Bluff</a>. Yet, when Baine and the <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">champion</a> greeted their Highmountain tauren guests, <a href="/wiki/Spiritwalker_Ebonhorn" class="mw-redirect" title="Spiritwalker Ebonhorn">Spiritwalker Ebonhorn</a> was tormented by the <a href="/wiki/Old_Gods%27_forces" title="Old Gods&#39; forces">Old Gods' forces</a> of <a href="/wiki/Uul%27gyneth" title="Uul&#39;gyneth">Uul'gyneth</a> attempting to corrupt him,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and he and Mayla were forced to retreat back to <a href="/wiki/Thunder_Totem" title="Thunder Totem">Thunder Totem</a> while Baine and the <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> champion defended Thunder Bluff from Uul'gyneth's invasion force.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Apparently, the wards Ebonhorn had been maintaining in <a href="/wiki/Highmountain" title="Highmountain">Highmountain</a> weakened just enough to let the Darkness seep through.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-QCotN&#95;9-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QCotN-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> 
</p><p>Later on, Baine and the Horde champion traveled to Thunder Totem to offer their aid to mend Ebonhorn,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> who has been fighting unconsciously to keep a darkness from consuming him. Mayla sent the Horde champion to <a href="/wiki/Spiritwalker_Graysky" title="Spiritwalker Graysky">Spiritwalker Graysky</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> who has the Horde champion undergo <a href="/wiki/Spiritwalker" title="Spiritwalker">Spiritwalker</a> rituals to walk in the memories of <a href="/wiki/Igrul_the_Scalebane" title="Igrul the Scalebane">Igrul the Scalebane</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Oenia_Skyhorn" title="Oenia Skyhorn">Oenia Skyhorn</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Huln_Highmountain" title="Huln Highmountain">Huln Highmountain</a> in order to restore the wards that kept the dark corrupting forces at bay.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After restoring the wards, Ebonhorn regained consciousness<sup id="cite&#95;ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and revealed Uul'gyneth sought to return and conquer Highmountain. Baine, Mayla, the Horde champion, and Ebonhorn left to join <a href="/wiki/Lasan_Skyhorn" title="Lasan Skyhorn">Lasan Skyhorn</a>, <a href="/wiki/Aviash" title="Aviash">Aviash</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Jale_Rivermane" title="Jale Rivermane">Jale Rivermane</a> at <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Summit" title="Highmountain Summit">Highmountain Summit</a> to thwart the <a href="/wiki/Necrodark" title="Necrodark">Necrodark</a>'s ritual to incarnate Uul'gyneth<sup id="cite&#95;ref-QCotN&#95;9-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-QCotN-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and restore the final ward. They failed however, and Uul'gyneth was summoned into the physical realm,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> leaving the Horde champion little choice but to vanquish Uul'gyneth the Darkness. After the battle, Mayla expressed her gratitude for rescuing her people from the <a href="/wiki/Old_Gods" class="mw-redirect" title="Old Gods">Old Gods</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Impressed by the strength and courage of the Horde, Mayla and Baine traveled to <a href="/wiki/Orgrimmar" title="Orgrimmar">Orgrimmar</a> to pledge the Highmountain tauren to the Horde.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
