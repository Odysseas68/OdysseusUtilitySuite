<p><b>Skyhold</b> is a portion of the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a> which are located high up in the clouds above <a href="/wiki/Helheim" title="Helheim">Helheim</a>. During the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion of the Burning Legion</a>, Skyhold was set aside by <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Keeper Odyn</a> as the <a href="/wiki/Warrior" title="Warrior">warrior</a> <a href="/wiki/Order_Halls" class="mw-redirect" title="Order Halls">order hall</a>, granting the warrior the title of the <a href="/wiki/Battlelord_(title)" title="Battlelord (title)">Battlelord</a> and commander of the <a href="/wiki/Valarjar" title="Valarjar">Valarjar</a> forces in the fight against the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Geography">Geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Skyhold?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Geography"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Skyhold is entered via a central area, which leads to four sections - Eye of Odyn, Forge of Odyn, Arena of Glory and The Great Mead Hall.
<a href="/wiki/Finna_Bjornsdottir" title="Finna Bjornsdottir">Finna Bjornsdottir</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ragnvald_Drakeborn" title="Ragnvald Drakeborn">Ragnvald Drakeborn</a>, <a href="/wiki/Valarjar_Aspirant" title="Valarjar Aspirant">Valarjar Aspirants</a> and <a href="/wiki/Valkyra_Shieldmaiden" title="Valkyra Shieldmaiden">Valkyra Shieldmaidens</a> are standing around the entrance to the Skyhold, while <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr_of_Odyn" title="Val&#39;kyr of Odyn">Val'kyr of Odyn</a> can be seen flying above.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Eye_of_Odyn">Eye of Odyn</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Skyhold?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Eye of Odyn"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Eye of Odyn area is where the class hall leader can recruit troops, send champions and troops on missions, place work order for champion armaments and equipment, purchase items from the quartermaster and travel to other zones in the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Aerylia" title="Aerylia">Aerylia</a> can be found at the entrance to the area, talking to her will give the player the options to travel to one of the Broken Isles zones. <a href="/wiki/Quartermaster_Durnolf" title="Quartermaster Durnolf">Quartermaster Durnolf</a> and a replica of the <a href="/wiki/Battlelord%27s_Armor?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Battlelord&#39;s Armor (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Battlelord's Armor</a> can be found on the near left side. Durnolf sells the armor set and armor upgrades. <a href="/wiki/Haklang_Ulfsson" title="Haklang Ulfsson">Haklang Ulfsson</a> can be found on the near right, allowing the player to place work orders for champion armaments and equipment. Haklang Ulfsson will only be available if the player choose the 3rd tier Order Advancement option <a href="/wiki/Heavenly_Forge?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Heavenly Forge (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Heavenly Forge</a>.
</p><p>Troop recruiters <a href="/wiki/Savyn_Valorborn" title="Savyn Valorborn">Savyn Valorborn</a> and <a href="/wiki/Matthew_Glensorrow" title="Matthew Glensorrow">Matthew Glensorrow</a> (for <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a> players) or <a href="/wiki/Sharak_Tor" title="Sharak Tor">Sharak Tor</a> (for <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> players) can be found on the far left side. Savyn Valorborn offers to recruit <a href="/wiki/Shieldmaiden_Warband" title="Shieldmaiden Warband">Shieldmaiden Warbands</a> or <a href="/wiki/Valkyra_Shieldmaiden" title="Valkyra Shieldmaiden">Valkyra Shieldmaidens</a> if the player choose the 4th tier Order Advancement option <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_monk_ascension.png/16px-Ability_monk_ascension.png?286f96" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Ascension" title="Ascension"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Ascension<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>; Matthew Glensorrow and Sharak Tor will only be available if the player choose 7th tier Order Advancement option <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon att-factionicon"><img alt="B" src="/images/Both_15.png?e25508" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="15" height="15" data-file-width="15" data-file-height="15" />&#160;</span><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Achievement_bg_killflagcarriers_grabflag_capit.png/16px-Achievement_bg_killflagcarriers_grabflag_capit.png?87c9d6" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Strike_Hard" title="Strike Hard"><span class="ajaxttlink achievementlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Strike Hard<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. Glensorrow offers to recruit <a href="/wiki/7th_Legion_Shock_Force" title="7th Legion Shock Force">7th Legion Shock Force</a>, while Tor offers to recruit <a href="/wiki/Kor%27kron_Shock_Force" title="Kor&#39;kron Shock Force">Kor'kron Shock Force</a>. Troop recruiter <a href="/wiki/Captain_Hjalmar_Stahlstrom" title="Captain Hjalmar Stahlstrom">Captain Hjalmar Stahlstrom</a> can be found on the far right side, offering to recruit <a href="/wiki/Valarjar_Aspirant" title="Valarjar Aspirant">Valarjar Aspirants</a> or <a href="/wiki/Stormforged_Valarjar" title="Stormforged Valarjar">Stormforged Valarjar</a> if the player choose the 4th tier Order Advancement option <a href="/wiki/Trial_by_Fire?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Trial by Fire (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Trial by Fire</a>.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Skyseer_Ghrent" title="Skyseer Ghrent">Skyseer Ghrent</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Eye_of_Odyn" class="mw-redirect" title="Eye of Odyn">Eye of Odyn</a> can be found at the far end. The player can send champions and troops on mission by interacting with the Eye of Odyn, while Ghrent will be handling most of the quest turn-ins.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Forge_of_Odyn">Forge of Odyn</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Skyhold?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Forge of Odyn"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Forge of Odyn area is where the class hall leader can research their <a href="/wiki/Artifact" title="Artifact">Artifact Weapon</a> to gain more <a href="/wiki/Artifact_Knowledge" title="Artifact Knowledge">Artifact Knowledge</a>, unlocking traits in their weapon by spending <a href="/wiki/Artifact_Power" title="Artifact Power">Artifact Power</a>, changing weapon visuals and upgrading their class hall.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Einar_the_Runecaster" title="Einar the Runecaster">Einar the Runecaster</a>, <a href="/wiki/Fjornson_Stonecarver" title="Fjornson Stonecarver">Fjornson Stonecarver</a> and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_jewelcrafting_taladitecrystal.png/16px-Inv_jewelcrafting_taladitecrystal.png?fdad6c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Light%27s_Heart" title="Light&#39;s Heart"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Light&#39;s Heart is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Light's Heart<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> can be found at the north side of the entrance in front of the three stone tables: <a href="/wiki/Saga_of_the_Valarjar" title="Saga of the Valarjar">Saga of the Valarjar</a>, <a href="/wiki/The_Favored_of_Odyn" title="The Favored of Odyn">The Favored of Odyn</a> and <a href="/wiki/The_Legend_of_Odyn" title="The Legend of Odyn">The Legend of Odyn</a>.
Einar will offer upgrades to the class hall, while Fjornson will help research the weapons. On the opposite side, <a href="/wiki/Keeper" title="Keeper">Keeper</a> <a href="/wiki/Thorim" title="Thorim">Thorim</a> is standing in front five pedestals with the unlocked appearances of the artifact weapon appearing above each. At the far end, <a href="/wiki/Master_Smith_Helgar" title="Master Smith Helgar">Master Smith Helgar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Dvalen_Ironrune" title="Dvalen Ironrune">Dvalen Ironrune</a> are located besides the <a href="/wiki/Forge_of_Odyn" title="Forge of Odyn">Forge of Odyn</a>. Interacting with the Forge of Odyn will allow the player to spend Artifact Power to unlock traits for their weapon and to change their weapon appearance.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Arena_of_Glory">Arena of Glory</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Skyhold?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Arena of Glory"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Arena of Glory area is where the class hall leader can place a work order to complete one non-elite <a href="/wiki/World_Quest" class="mw-redirect" title="World Quest">World Quest</a>, use the <a href="/wiki/Training_Dummy" title="Training Dummy">Training Dummys</a> and to fight for a chance to win the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Garrison_silverchest.png/16px-Garrison_silverchest.png?98596c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Skyhold_Chest_of_Riches" title="Skyhold Chest of Riches"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-rare" title="Skyhold Chest of Riches is a rare quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Skyhold Chest of Riches<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>.
</p><p>Once every 3 hours Odyn will place the Skyhold Chest of Riches in the center of the arena, warriors entering the arena will be flagged for PvP and must fight to win the chest. If the chest is not looted in 10 minutes, <a href="/wiki/Hargal_the_Bladestormer" title="Hargal the Bladestormer">Hargal the Bladestormer</a> will jump into the area and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_warrior_bladestorm.png/16px-Ability_warrior_bladestorm.png?d92ca9" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Bladestorm" title="Bladestorm"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Bladestorm<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> until the chest is looted or he is defeated, which happens when he reaches ~10% health, at which point he will retreat and drop his <a href="/wiki/Hargal%27s_Axe_of_Bladestorming?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Hargal&#39;s Axe of Bladestorming (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">axe</a>, picking up the axe puts you into a permanent bladestorm, with a huge damage boost. A player will only be able to see and loot the chest if the they choose the 2nd tier Order Advancement option <a href="/wiki/For_Honor_and_Glory?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="For Honor and Glory (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">For Honor and Glory</a>. 
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Matilda_Skoptidottir" title="Matilda Skoptidottir">Matilda Skoptidottir</a> can be found on the left side of the arena, offering use of <a href="/wiki/Blessing_of_Mjolnir?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Blessing of Mjolnir (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Blessing of Mjolnir</a> to gain the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_weapon_shortblade_70.png/16px-Inv_weapon_shortblade_70.png?58d6a1" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Horn_of_War" title="Horn of War"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-rare" title="Horn of War is a rare quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Horn of War<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> to unleash the power of the Val'kyr to help complete a non-elite <a href="/wiki/World_Quest" class="mw-redirect" title="World Quest">World Quest</a> once every 18 hours. Matilda Skoptidottir is only available if a player choose the 5th tier Order Advancement option <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr_Call?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Val&#39;kyr Call (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Val'kyr Call</a>. 
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Valarjar_Aspirant" title="Valarjar Aspirant">Valarjar Aspirant</a> spectators, tables of food and drinks and weapon and armor racks can be found around the arena, with <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr_Arena_Master" title="Val&#39;kyr Arena Master">Val'kyr Arena Master</a> located at the 2 entrances to the arena itself.
<a href="/wiki/Weaponmaster_Asvard" title="Weaponmaster Asvard">Weaponmaster Asvard</a>, <a href="/wiki/King_Ymiron" title="King Ymiron">King Ymiron</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hodir" title="Hodir">Keeper Hodir</a> can be found at the entrance, the left and right sides of the arena, respectively.
<a href="/wiki/Lesser_Sparring_Partner" title="Lesser Sparring Partner">Lesser Sparring Partner</a> and <a href="/wiki/Greater_Sparring_Partner" title="Greater Sparring Partner">Greater Sparring Partner</a> Raider's Training Dummy can be found at the far end, while on the right side of the arena Tanking <a href="/wiki/Lesser_Sparring_Partner" title="Lesser Sparring Partner">Lesser Sparring Partner</a> and <a href="/wiki/Greater_Sparring_Partner" title="Greater Sparring Partner">Greater Sparring Partner</a> can be found.
</p><p>Armor racks featuring warrior tier sets are located at the both the left and right sides of the entrance. Once a player collects all items from a set, the set will appear on the armor rack.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Great_Mead_Hall">The Great Mead Hall</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Skyhold?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: The Great Mead Hall"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Great Mead Hall is full of <a href="/wiki/Valarjar_Aspirant" title="Valarjar Aspirant">Valarjar Aspirants</a> and <a href="/wiki/Valarjar_Shieldmaiden" title="Valarjar Shieldmaiden">Shieldmaidens</a> eating and drinking while <a href="/wiki/Stormforged_Valarjar" title="Stormforged Valarjar">Stormforged Valarjar</a> guard the hall. At the hall's center sits <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Keeper Odyn</a>, with <a href="/wiki/Hymdall" title="Hymdall">Hymdall</a> and <a href="/wiki/Lord_Darius_Crowley" class="mw-redirect" title="Lord Darius Crowley">Lord Darius Crowley</a> (for Alliance players) or <a href="/wiki/Eitrigg" title="Eitrigg">Eitrigg</a> (for Horde players) at his right and left sides, respectively.
<a href="/wiki/Hruthnir" title="Hruthnir">Hruthnir</a> and <a href="/wiki/Magnar_Icebreaker" title="Magnar Icebreaker">Magnar Icebreaker</a> can be found on the far left side, while <a href="/wiki/Renfield" title="Renfield">Renfield</a> and <a href="/wiki/Svergan_Stormcloak" title="Svergan Stormcloak">Svergan Stormcloak</a> can be found on the far right side. The <a href="/wiki/Grand_Challenger%27s_Bounty" title="Grand Challenger&#39;s Bounty">Grand Challenger's Bounty</a> can be located at the entrance to the hall.
</p>

<p>Einar the Runecaster handles the Warrior's <a href="/wiki/Order_Advancement" title="Order Advancement">Order Hall advancement</a> upgrades. Once upgraded, a player can change to the other upgrade by paying another Order Resources cost and waiting the required research time.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Artifact_Weapons">Artifact Weapons</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Skyhold?action=edit&amp;section=16" title="Edit section: Artifact Weapons"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Odyn supplies the Battlelord with weapons of great power, each fitting a different specialization. The quest to obtain the first weapon is available at level 98 and the warrior must decide which weapon to choose, however this limitation is short as the other two other weapon quests are available at level 102, enabling the warrior to have all 3 weapons. Each weapon initially came with four unlockable appearances and one hidden appearance. Each appearance has 4 color tints which can be unlocked by achieving a certain requirement. A new appearance was made available with <a href="/wiki/Patch_7.2" class="mw-redirect" title="Patch 7.2">patch 7.2</a>, requiring the player to complete a difficult solo challenge to unlock it. In addition, each weapon has a hidden trait unique to that weapon.
</p><p>In order to attempt the challenge, your character must be level 100, have 35 <a href="/wiki/Artifact_Trait?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Artifact Trait (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Artifact Traits</a> and complete the new Empowered Trait questline. This must be done for each weapon in order to unlock its challenge. In addition, your region must have constructed the <a href="/wiki/Mage_Tower_(Broken_Shore)" title="Mage Tower (Broken Shore)">Mage Tower</a> in <a href="/wiki/Deliverance_Point" title="Deliverance Point">Deliverance Point</a>, <a href="/wiki/Broken_Shore" title="Broken Shore">Broken Shore</a>. Speak to <a href="/wiki/War_Councilor_Victoria" title="War Councilor Victoria">War Councilor Victoria</a> once the Mage Tower is constructed to attempt the challenge. The first attempt is free, with subsequent attempts costing <span class="nobreak ajaxoutertt">100 <a href="/wiki/Nethershard" title="Nethershard"><img alt="Nethershard" src="/images/thumb/Inv_datacrystal01.png/16px-Inv_datacrystal01.png?465534" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" /></a></span>.
</p>
<h3><span id="Arms.2C_Strom.27kar.2C_the_Warbreaker"></span><span class="mw-headline" id="Arms,_Strom'kar,_the_Warbreaker">Arms, Strom'kar, the Warbreaker</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Skyhold?action=edit&amp;section=17" title="Edit section: Arms, Strom&#039;kar, the Warbreaker"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Strom%27kar,_the_Warbreaker" title="Strom&#39;kar, the Warbreaker">Strom'kar, the Warbreaker</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<dl><dd><i>This greatsword was borne into combat by the barbarian warlord who first united humanity under a single banner, founding the nation of <a href="/wiki/Arathor" title="Arathor">Arathor</a>. A master tactician and strategist, <a href="/wiki/Thoradin" title="Thoradin">King Thoradin</a> went on to play a critical role in ending the <a href="/wiki/Troll_Wars" title="Troll Wars">Troll Wars</a>. <p>In the alpha, spirits of famous <a href="/wiki/Warrior" title="Warrior">warriors</a> were in Skyhold, but they were removed because it didn't feel appropriate to <a href="/wiki/Blizzard_Entertainment" title="Blizzard Entertainment">Blizzard</a> for these <a href="/wiki/Human" title="Human">human</a> and <a href="/wiki/Orc" title="Orc">orc</a> heroes to appear in this <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a> place.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The identity of these removed heroes is still unknown. However, checking the NPC IDs on <a href="/wiki/Wowhead" title="Wowhead">Wowhead</a>, starting with the version of <a href="/wiki/Danath_Trollbane" title="Danath Trollbane">Danath Trollbane</a> used during the <span class="questlink"><span class="questlong-prefix linkicon"><img alt="A" src="/images/Alliance_15.png?165d42" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="15" height="15" data-file-width="15" data-file-height="15" />&#160;<img alt="Warrior" src="/images/thumb/Ui-charactercreate-classes_warrior.png/16px-Ui-charactercreate-classes_warrior.png?c63406" decoding="async" loading="lazy" title="Warrior" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;[10-45]&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Return_to_the_Broken_Shore_(Alliance)" title="Return to the Broken Shore (Alliance)"><span class="ajaxttlink questlink">Return to the Broken Shore</span></a></span> warrior class quest (<a rel="nofollow" class="external autonumber" href="http://www.wowhead.com/npc=96183/danath-trollbane">[1]</a>) and increasing incrementally, the IDs lead to several consecutive unused warrior NPCs that were possibly meant to appear in Skyhold.

<p>Considering some of these are still living to this day, it is possible that other living warriors were meant to be accepted into Skyhold like the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Battlelord</a>. The presence of <a href="/wiki/Nazgrim" title="Nazgrim">Nazgrim</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thoras_Trollbane" title="Thoras Trollbane">Thoras Trollbane</a> may also indicate that they were not maybe always meant to be part of the reborn <a href="/wiki/Four_Horsemen#Original_Legion_intent" title="Four Horsemen">Horsemen</a>. Several of them have a placeholder model, though others such as Anduin Lothar had never appeared in <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft" title="World of Warcraft">World of Warcraft</a></i> before.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Cairne_Bloodhoof" title="Cairne Bloodhoof">Cairne Bloodhoof</a> might have been originally intended intended to be a Valarjar's sub-leader, according to <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_groupneedmore.png/16px-Inv_misc_groupneedmore.png?ab3389" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/New_Recruit_(warrior)" title="New Recruit (warrior)"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="New Recruit is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>New Recruit<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, which had similar variants given by the other class halls' sub-leaders.
</p>
