'''Natal'hakata''' is a [[Zandalari troll]] located at the [[Great Seal]] in [[Dazar'alor]]. He is the Voice of the King, deliverer of the royal will, keeper of the grand schedule, and representative toward the citizens wanting an audience with the royalty.<ref>[[Speaker of the Horde]]</ref> As such, he deals with various problems of the empire. He received word from [[Warbeast Kraal]] about a missing handler<ref>[[The Missing Handler]]</ref> and learned about problems in Dol'watha. He sent the [[Adventurer|Speaker of the Horde]] for aid to those locations.<ref>[[Sandscar Breach (quest)]]</ref>

When the time of [[Talanji]]'s ascension as queen of Zandalar, she directed Natal'hakata to send the Speaker to [[Zanchul]] to accompany Talanji for the ceremony.<ref>[[A Royal Occasion]]</ref><ref>[[A Queen's Entourage]]</ref> He later traveled to [[Orgrimmar]] to see young Zandalari off and commented that the area is less fertile than Zandalar.<ref>[[Stranger in a Strange Land (Zandalari troll)]]</ref>

{{Novel-section}}
When the [[Widow's Bite]] threatened the rule of [[Talanji]] and planned to kill [[Bwonsamdi]], Natal'hakata was present in a meeting of the [[Zanchuli Council]]. He said that they should assemble a force and send it to the [[Necropolis (Nazmir)|Necropolis]]. He was then impatient with [[Zekhan]] who also wanted to voice his opinion.<ref name="SR">''[[Shadows Rising]]'', pg. 149</ref>
