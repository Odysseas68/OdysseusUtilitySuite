The '''Goldtusk Inn''' is an [[inn]] owned by [[Rhan'ka]]<ref name="Gozda'kun">[[Gozda'kun the Slaver (quest)]]</ref> and staffed by the [[Goldtusk Gang]]<ref name="Gozda'kun"/><ref name="Meet">[[Meet the Goldtusk Gang]]</ref> inside a cave on the northeast side of [[Whistlebloom Oasis]] in [[Vol'dun]]. According to Rhan'ka, "All exiles are welcome to rest at de Goldtusk Inn, de only luxury lodge in dese harsh dunes".<ref name="Meet"/> Guests at the inn need to earn their keep. The inn offers several excursion activities and packages, such as the "Warlord Package" (which is required for access to excursions to [[Dead Man's Pass]]), the "Scorpid King Package" (unlimited all-day access to Rhan'ka's famous [[scorpid]] blood drinks),<ref>[[Dirty Work for Dirty Drinks]]</ref> and the "Giant Deadly Unstoppable Killer Cobra Experience" (an activity which challenges guests to travel to the dunes north of the inn and try not to be killed by [[Sevriss]]).<ref>[[WANTED: Cobra Excursion Participants]]</ref>

When [[adventurer]]s first arrive at the inn, it offers no commodities and the visitors are only able to see the seemingly insane Rhan'ka talking to skulls.<ref name="Meet"/> After Rhan'ka sends the adventurers to [[Dead Man's Pass]], [[Farraki tribe|Sandfury]] raiders led by [[Gozda'kun the Slaver]] thrash the inn and kidnap the Goldtusk Gang (leading Rhan'ka to officially ban the tribe from the inn).<ref name="Gozda'kun"/><ref>[[The Great Cranium Caper]]</ref> After adventurers rescue the skulls, Rhan'ka conducts a ritual to initiate them into the Goldtusk Gang and allow them to see what the gang sees, revealing that most of the Goldtusk Gang members are actually ghosts that were previously invisible.<ref>[[Powerful Spirits]]</ref> After adventurers help the various gang members with tasks around the inn — specifically by helping [[Man'zul]] create and plant a "gold tusk" above the inn's entrance,<ref>[[Creative Marketing]]</ref> clearing the oasis of predators for [[Volni]],<ref>[[The Best Kill is Overkill]]</ref> and gathering fruit for [[Zulsan]]'s all-you-can-eat breakfast buffet<ref>[[Restocking the Buffet]]</ref> — the place receives its first guest in the form of the injured [[Bladeguard Tarkaj]], who is offered food, drink, bandages, and bedding by the adventurer. Afterward, Rhan'ka declares that the inn is officially open.<ref>[[A Goldtusk Greeting]]</ref>

==Notes==
*The inn contains an interactable [[Drum (Vol'dun)|Drum]] and a Cooking Cauldron in which Rhan'ka hides during part of the questline.
*The commodities at the inn are not initially available and instead have to be unlocked through quests. Volni's flight path unlocks after [[The Best Kill is Overkill]], and Grenja, Man'zul, and Rhan'ka only offer their services after completion of [[A Goldtusk Greeting]]. A Goldtusk Greeting also adds an [[anvil]], [[forge]], and [[mailbox|Goldtusk Inn Mailbox]] outside the inn's entrance.
