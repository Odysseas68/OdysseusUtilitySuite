'''Ornamm''' is a [[tauren]] from the [[Highmountain tribe (Legends)|Highmountain tribe]] that tried to kill the [[undead]] tauren [[Trag Highmountain]].<ref name=fallen>''[[Warcraft: Legends Volume 1]]'', [[Fallen]]</ref>

==Biography==
When [[Sulamm]] put Trag in a state of trance at the [[Henge of the Earth Mother]], Ornamm and his fellow tauren arrived to kill Trag. As he was in trance, he didn't realize about Ornamm's trap and was thrown to a fire. However Trag managed to awaken and defeated the party, however he didn't kill anyone and just took the weapon of Ornamm.<ref name=fallen/>
