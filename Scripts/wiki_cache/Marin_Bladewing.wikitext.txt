'''Marin Bladewing''' is a [[night elf]] [[warden]] serving under [[Maiev Shadowsong]]. She took part in the [[Firelands Invasion]] as part of the [[Shadow Wardens]]. She later joined the [[The Wardens|Wardens]] in resisting the [[Third invasion of the Burning Legion|third invasion]] of the [[Burning Legion]] from [[Azsuna]], under the command of [[Sira Moonwarden]] and Maiev. At the time of the [[Fourth War]], Marin and her fellow wardens committed themselves to the [[Alliance]] cause, and fought alongside their leader in [[Battle for Darkshore|the campaign]] to drive the [[Horde]] from [[Darkshore]].

==Background==
===Cataclysm===
{{cata-section}}
[[File:Marin Bladewing.jpg|thumb|upright|Marin on the [[Molten Front]].]]
Following the [[Cataclysm (event)|Cataclysm]], Marin originally appeared as a [[Shadow Wardens|Shadow Warden]] at the [[Forlorn Spire]] in the [[Molten Front]]. There, she took part in the [[Firelands Invasion|campaign]] against the Fire Lord, [[Ragnaros]], and his minions, in order to save all of [[Mount Hyjal]] from destruction. She was accompanied in this quest by a number of other wardens and [[adventurer]]s, and appeared to be in command of her fellow sisters.<ref name="The Wardens are Watching">[[The Wardens are Watching]]</ref> After slaying [[fire elemental]]s, they took the [[Forlorn Spire]], after which she sensed [[Druids of the Flame]] nearby.<ref>[[The Forlorn Spire (quest)]]</ref> Therefore, she requested to strike at and destroy the druids' [[Solar Core]] that had the potential of causing massive damage to Azeroth<ref>[[Solar Core Destruction]]</ref> and ordered the capture of one of the druids for questioning in order to learn of their plans, and sent a [[Shadow Warden|warden]] and the adventurer to do it.<ref name="The Wardens are Watching"/> Afterward, she asked the adventurer with assisting [[Shalis Darkhunter]] and [[Theresa Barkskin]] in their mission in the [[Igneous Depths]] and specifically tasked them to disrupt the [[Druids of the Flame]]'s ritual by destroying their [[Flame Protection Rune|summoning runes]].<ref>[[Enduring the Heat]]</ref>

===Warlords of Draenor===
{{wod-section}}
During the [[war in Draenor]], Marin was part of a contingent of [[warden]]s sent by [[Maiev Shadowsong]] to protect [[Archmage Khadgar]] as he worked against the [[Burning Legion]] on [[alternate Draenor]]. Led by [[Cordana Felsong]], the group was stationed across the continent, with Marin herself being found in [[Starfall Outpost]] in {{Au|Shadowmoon Valley}} and [[Yrel's Watch]] in {{Au|Nagrand}}.

===Legion===
{{legion-section}}
By the time of the [[Third invasion of the Burning Legion|third invasion]] of the [[Burning Legion]], Marin and her [[The Wardens|fellow Wardens]] had reestablished themselves on the [[Broken Isles]], where they guarded the [[Vault of the Wardens]] against all threats. The [[demon]]s' attack on the facility, aided by [[Cordana Felsong]] herself, succeeded in breaching the Vault. Marin would act as [[quartermaster]] for the organization from [[Wardens' Redoubt]] in [[Azsuna]], from which she and her sisters fought desperately to drive back the Legion's forces.

===Battle for Azeroth===
{{Bfa-section}}
[[File:Wardenlineup alternate.jpg|thumb|Marin Bladewing and the Wardens in [[Darkshore]].]]
Warden Bladewing and her sisters would answer the call of [[Maiev Shadowsong]] and [[Tyrande Whisperwind]], as the [[kaldorei]] prepared to retake [[Darkshore]] from its [[Horde]] occupiers during the [[Fourth War]]. They would venture with Maiev to [[Bashal'Aran]], where they defended High Priestess Tyrande during her ritual to become the [[Night Warrior]], and subsequently witnessed her transformation.<ref>[[In Darkest Night]]</ref>

Later, Marin and her sisters took part in the [[Battle for Darkshore]] alongside other [[Alliance]] forces.

===Dragonflight===
{{Df-section}}
Years later, Marin and her fellow wardens attended the gathering at [[Amirdrassil]] when it bloomed on the [[Dragon Isles]] after [[Fyrakk]]'s death. Afterward, she can be seen speaking with [[Arko'narin Starshade]] in [[Arlithrien Lodge]].
