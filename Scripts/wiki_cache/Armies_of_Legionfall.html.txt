<p>The <b>Armies of Legionfall</b><sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are a coalition of all <a href="/wiki/Class_Order" title="Class Order">orders</a> united to defeat the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Tomb_of_Sargeras" title="Tomb of Sargeras">Tomb of Sargeras</a>. Their base of operations, <a href="/wiki/Deliverance_Point" title="Deliverance Point">Deliverance Point</a>, is located south-west of the island of <a href="/wiki/Thal%27dranath" class="mw-redirect" title="Thal&#39;dranath">Thal'dranath</a>.
</p><p>In addition to the orders, the Armies of Legionfall are also allied with the <a href="/wiki/Court_of_Farondis" title="Court of Farondis">Court of Farondis</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Azurewing" title="Azurewing">Azurewing</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_(faction)" title="Nightfallen (faction)">Nightfallen</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Tribe" title="Highmountain Tribe">Highmountain Tribe</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the <a href="/wiki/Wardens" class="mw-redirect" title="Wardens">Wardens</a>, as evidenced by their assistance to the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Legionfall Commander</a>.
</p><p>Following the end of the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion of the Burning Legion</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Argus_Campaign" title="Argus Campaign">Argus Campaign</a>, it is unknown if the Armies of Legionfall remains active, however, it is most probably that they dissolved after reaching their goals.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes_and_trivia">Notes and trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Armies_of_Legionfall?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Notes and trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Legionfall_champions.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Legionfall_champions.jpg/320px-Legionfall_champions.jpg?99dbd2" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="192" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1340" data-file-height="803" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Legionfall_champions.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Khadgar" title="Khadgar">Khadgar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Velen" title="Velen">Velen</a> with the champions of the orders</div></div></div>
<ul><li>The <a href="/wiki/Class_Order" title="Class Order">orders</a> armies are accompanied by <a href="/wiki/Khadgar" title="Khadgar">Archmage Khadgar</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Mage" title="Mage">mages</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Kirin_Tor" title="Kirin Tor">Kirin Tor</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maiev_Shadowsong" title="Maiev Shadowsong">Maiev Shadowsong</a> and her <a href="/wiki/The_Wardens" class="mw-redirect" title="The Wardens">Wardens</a>, <a href="/wiki/Prophet_Velen" class="mw-redirect" title="Prophet Velen">Prophet Velen</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Illidan_Stormrage" title="Illidan Stormrage">Illidan Stormrage</a>.</li>
<li>The Armies of Legionfall's banner features, from left to right and top to bottom, the crests of the <a href="/wiki/Valarjar" title="Valarjar">Valarjar</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ebon_Blade" class="mw-redirect" title="Ebon Blade">Ebon Blade</a>, <a href="/wiki/Earthen_Ring" title="Earthen Ring">Earthen Ring</a>, <a href="/wiki/Order_of_the_Broken_Temple" title="Order of the Broken Temple">Order of the Broken Temple</a>, <a href="/wiki/Unseen_Path" title="Unseen Path">Unseen Path</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tirisgarde" title="Tirisgarde">Tirisgarde</a>, <a href="/wiki/Illidari" title="Illidari">Illidari</a>, <a href="/wiki/Silver_Hand" class="mw-redirect" title="Silver Hand">Silver Hand</a>, <a href="/wiki/Black_Harvest" class="mw-redirect" title="Black Harvest">Black Harvest</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cenarion_Circle" title="Cenarion Circle">Cenarion Circle</a>, <a href="/wiki/Conclave" title="Conclave">Conclave</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/The_Uncrowned" class="mw-redirect" title="The Uncrowned">Uncrowned</a>.</li>
<li>During the initial meeting of the Armies of Legionfall, several important delegates from the class orders met at <a href="/wiki/Krasus%27_Landing" title="Krasus&#39; Landing">Krasus' Landing</a> with <a href="/wiki/Archmage_Khadgar" class="mw-redirect" title="Archmage Khadgar">Archmage Khadgar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Prophet_Velen" class="mw-redirect" title="Prophet Velen">Prophet Velen</a> to discuss <a href="/wiki/Gul%27dan_(alternate_universe)" title="Gul&#39;dan (alternate universe)">Gul'dan's</a> <a href="/wiki/Nighthold" title="Nighthold">failure</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Pillars_of_Creation" title="Pillars of Creation">Pillars of Creation</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Kil%27jaeden" title="Kil&#39;jaeden">Kil'jaeden</a>. These included:
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Alonsus_Faol" title="Alonsus Faol">Alonsus Faol</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Conclave" title="Conclave">Conclave</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Emmarel_Shadewarden" title="Emmarel Shadewarden">Emmarel Shadewarden</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Unseen_Path" title="Unseen Path">Unseen Path</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Ritssyn_Flamescowl" title="Ritssyn Flamescowl">Ritssyn Flamescowl</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Council_of_the_Black_Harvest" title="Council of the Black Harvest">Council of the Black Harvest</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Havi" class="mw-redirect" title="Havi">Havi</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Valarjar" title="Valarjar">Valarjar</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Iron-Body_Ponshu" title="Iron-Body Ponshu">Iron-Body Ponshu</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Order_of_the_Broken_Temple" title="Order of the Broken Temple">Order of the Broken Temple</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Kor%27vas_Bloodthorn" title="Kor&#39;vas Bloodthorn">Kor'vas Bloodthorn</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Illidari" title="Illidari">Illidari</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Lady_Liadrin" title="Lady Liadrin">Lady Liadrin</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Knights_of_the_Silver_Hand" title="Knights of the Silver Hand">Knights of the Silver Hand</a> (although it appears that <a href="/wiki/Gryan_Stoutmantle" title="Gryan Stoutmantle">Gryan Stoutmantle</a> is present in the cinematic).</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Farseer_Nobundo" title="Farseer Nobundo">Farseer Nobundo</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Earthen_Ring" title="Earthen Ring">Earthen Ring</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Meryl_Felstorm" title="Meryl Felstorm">Meryl Felstorm</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Tirisgarde" title="Tirisgarde">Tirisgarde</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Darion_Mograine" title="Darion Mograine">Darion Mograine</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Knights_of_the_Ebon_Blade" title="Knights of the Ebon Blade">Knights of the Ebon Blade</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Rensar_Greathoof" title="Rensar Greathoof">Rensar Greathoof</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Cenarion_Circle" title="Cenarion Circle">Cenarion Circle</a>.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Lord_Jorach_Ravenholdt" title="Lord Jorach Ravenholdt">Lord Jorach Ravenholdt</a> representing the <a href="/wiki/Uncrowned" title="Uncrowned">Uncrowned</a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
