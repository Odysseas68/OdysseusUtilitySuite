'''Plunder Harbor''' is a town in eastern [[Tiragarde Sound]] under the control of the [[Fogsail Freebooters]]. The [[Maiden's Lament]] inn is located here.

During the [[Fourth War]], the Fogsail Freebooters allowed the [[Honorbound]] [[Horde]] to use the harbor as a safe foothold in Kul Tiras after they helped [[Owings]] pull a mutiny on the previous captain [[Captain Rhenik|Rhenik]] for wanting to sell the crew out to the [[Ashvane Trading Company]].<ref>[[I'm the Captain Now]]</ref> The Horde's actions led to the Proudmoore fleet attacking the harbor, sending the ''[[Siren's Daughter]]'' and ''[[Valorous Rose]]'' battleships to get [[Derek Proudmoore]]'s body back.
