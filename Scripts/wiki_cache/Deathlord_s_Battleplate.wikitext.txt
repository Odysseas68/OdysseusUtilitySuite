'''Deathlord's Battlegear''' is the [[Class Hall]] set for [[Death Knights]].  Visually it's a recolor of {{mop-inline}}[[Golden Chest of the Lich Lord|Lich Lord Armor]], and is meant as an entry level set as [[Tier 19]] is item level 850 in normal mode. A Full set can be seen standing behind [[Quartermaster Ozorg]].

== Notes ==
*All pieces are available from your Quartermaster for repurchase if you happen to get rid of them at some point, for the cost of {{cost|500}}.
*Shoulder and Gloves are affected by reputation discount when bought from [[First Arcanist Thalyssra]].
*Earning all 8 pieces of your Class Order Hall set rewards you with [[A Classy Outfit]].
*Every item can be upgraded to item level {{text|epic|840}} with the help of [[Deathlord's Lesser Armor Kit]], [[Deathlord's Armor Kit]] and [[Deathlord's Greater Armor Kit]]
