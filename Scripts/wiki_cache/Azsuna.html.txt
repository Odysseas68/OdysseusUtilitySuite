<dl><dd><i>Azsuna was once a gem of the night elf empire. Now much of it lies in ruins, a home for vengeful elven spirits and hidden enclaves of blue dragons. What remains of this haunted land has come under threat from conflict among Azeroth's defenders, an army of serpentine naga, and the Burning Legion.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></dd></dl>
<p><b>Azsuna</b> (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Phonetic_Alphabet" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:International Phonetic Alphabet"><span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)" class="IPA">/æzˈsuːnə/</span></a> <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Help:Pronunciation respelling key"><span title="English Pronunciation Respelling" class="IPA">az-SOO-nə</span></a></i>) is an island<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Zone" title="Zone">zone</a> in the southwestern part of the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>. It holds the ruins of the <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elf</a> civlization that existed before the <a href="/wiki/Great_Sundering" title="Great Sundering">Great Sundering</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The ghosts of ancient night elves roam this area, along with remnants of the <a href="/wiki/Blue_dragonflight" title="Blue dragonflight">blue dragonflight</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Nightfallen" title="Nightfallen">Nightfallen</a> maintain a presence here. Azsuna was not destroyed in the Great Sundering, but was actually devastated several years before that by <a href="/wiki/Queen_Azshara" title="Queen Azshara">Queen Azshara</a> herself.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>This is where one of the <a href="/wiki/Pillars_of_Creation" title="Pillars of Creation">Pillars of Creation</a>, the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_enchantedpearl.png/16px-Inv_misc_enchantedpearl.png?3dc5d1" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Tidestone_of_Golganneth" title="Tidestone of Golganneth"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Tidestone of Golganneth is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Tidestone of Golganneth<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, was contested by the <a href="/wiki/Hero" title="Hero">heroes</a> of <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a> and the minions of Azshara.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Azsuna?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Azsuna was originally populated by more than ten thousand elves.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Queen_Azshara" title="Queen Azshara">Queen Azshara</a>'s pact with the demon lord <a href="/wiki/Sargeras" title="Sargeras">Sargeras</a> went mostly uncontested by her subjects. One of the few that dared to defy her was <a href="/wiki/Prince_Farondis" title="Prince Farondis">Prince Farondis</a>.
</p><p>As one of the queen's trusted advisors, Farondis was in regular contact with the <a href="/wiki/Highborne" title="Highborne">Highborne</a> of <a href="/wiki/Zin-Azshari" title="Zin-Azshari">Zin-Azshari</a>, but his palace in Azsuna was several leagues away from the elven capital. As such, he was able to witness Sargeras' influence on the Highborne court without falling sway to the Legion lord's temptation.
</p><p>Farondis' plan was bold, but simple: destroy the <a href="/wiki/Well_of_Eternity" title="Well of Eternity">Well of Eternity</a> in Zin-Azshari. Doing so would close the portal to Sargeras' realm and stem the tide of evil before it even started. In order to accomplish this task, he would use the power of an artifact stored deep within <a href="/wiki/Nar%27thalas_Academy" title="Nar&#39;thalas Academy">Nar'thalas Academy</a>: the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_enchantedpearl.png/16px-Inv_misc_enchantedpearl.png?3dc5d1" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Tidestone_of_Golganneth" title="Tidestone of Golganneth"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Tidestone of Golganneth is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Tidestone of Golganneth<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. 
</p><p>Unfortunately, the queen's influence in Farondis' palace was already deep-seated. <a href="/wiki/Vandros" class="mw-redirect" title="Vandros">Vandros</a>, a young noble in Farondis' court, caught word of Farondis' plans to defy the Queen, and reported the insurrection to Queen Azshara herself.
</p><p>Azshara wasted no time in punishing Farondis and his people. In a frightening display of arcane power, she destroyed the Tidestone of Golganneth, and in doing so, released a wave of dark energy over all of Azsuna and its inhabitants.
</p><p>Since that day, the elves of Azsuna have been unable to experience the release of death, and their spirits wander the land indefinitely.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point during the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">invasion</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Knights_of_the_Silver_Hand" title="Knights of the Silver Hand">Order of the Silver Hand</a> sent their troops into the region after discovering a small <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demon</a> force in the east, and hearing rumors that they report directly to <a href="/wiki/Balnazzar" title="Balnazzar">Balnazzar</a>. However, they were lead by <a href="/wiki/Pitlord_Argamemm" title="Pitlord Argamemm">Pitlord Argamemm</a> who was killed by the paladins.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes_and_trivia">Notes and trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Azsuna?action=edit&amp;section=12" title="Edit section: Notes and trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Some quests seem to name it "Aszuna".<sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>The <a href="/wiki/Eye_of_Azshara" title="Eye of Azshara">Eye of Azshara</a> island was originally called "Aszuna Isle".<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>In the Legion World Panel during <a href="/wiki/BlizzCon_2015" title="BlizzCon 2015">BlizzCon 2015</a>, the devs mentioned that Azsuna had a "Greek isle theme, with stylized olive and cypress trees".</li></ul>
