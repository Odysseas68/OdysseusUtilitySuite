'''Valarjar Aspirants''' are [[vrykul]] located in the [[Halls of Valor]] and in [[Skyhold]].

==Abilities==
*'''Blast of Light''' - The caster blasts enemies with a beam of light, inflicting 1576452 to 1586202 Holy damage.
*'''Valkyra's Advance''' - The caster leaps towards an enemy, inflicting 1089677 to 1121877 Physical damage to all enemies within 5 yds. of the impact.
