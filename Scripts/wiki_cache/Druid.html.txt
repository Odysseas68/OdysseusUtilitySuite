<p>The <b>druid</b> (occasionally <b>druidess</b> for females)<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> is a <a href="/wiki/Shapeshifting" class="mw-redirect" title="Shapeshifting">shapeshifting</a> <a href="/wiki/Hybrid_class" title="Hybrid class">hybrid class</a> available to the <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elf</a>, <a href="/wiki/Worgen" title="Worgen">worgen</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tauren" title="Tauren">tauren</a> (including <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_tauren_(playable)" title="Highmountain tauren (playable)">Highmountain</a>), <a href="/wiki/Troll" title="Troll">troll</a> (including <a href="/wiki/Zandalari_troll_(playable)" title="Zandalari troll (playable)">Zandalari</a>), and <a href="/wiki/Kul_Tiran_(playable)" title="Kul Tiran (playable)">Kul Tiran</a> races. Druids are able to perform each of the three group <a href="/wiki/Role" class="mw-redirect" title="Role">roles</a> (<a href="/wiki/Tank" title="Tank">tanking</a>, <a href="/wiki/Healer_(role)" title="Healer (role)">healing</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Damage_(role)" title="Damage (role)">dealing damage</a>) by utilizing their <a href="/wiki/Shapeshift" class="mw-redirect" title="Shapeshift">shapeshift</a> forms. While a druid is most effective in the role determined by their active <a href="/wiki/Specialization" title="Specialization">specialization</a> and equipment, they can also use their shapeshifting abilities to temporarily switch to a different role in combat. In addition to combat forms, druids can shapeshift into forms that increases movement speed while <a href="/wiki/Travel_Form" title="Travel Form">running</a>, <a href="/wiki/Aquatic_Form" title="Aquatic Form">swimming</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Flight_Form" title="Flight Form">flying</a>. The ability to switch roles out of combat - and temporarily perform a different role in combat - makes the druid class one of the most versatile in <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft" title="World of Warcraft">World of Warcraft</a></i>.
</p><p>Druids have as many faces to them as nature itself: destruction, animal fury, transformation, healing. Everything about them is rooted in cycles and transience, and the <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Dream" title="Emerald Dream">Emerald Dream</a> of what <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a> would have been with nature unfettered.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Nature" title="Nature">Nature</a> magic serves as the source of power for druids, as they are guardians of nature who seek to preserve balance and protect life. It is no secret that <a href="/wiki/Tauren" title="Tauren">tauren</a> and <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elf</a> alike can call upon <a href="/wiki/Elune" title="Elune">Elune</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Moonlight" title="Moonlight">moonlight</a> magic, or Mu'sha as she is called in <a href="/wiki/Tauren_mythology" title="Tauren mythology">tauren mythology</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They also used <a href="/wiki/Solar_magic" title="Solar magic">solar magic</a>, a form of <a href="/wiki/Nature" title="Nature">nature</a> magic practiced through the use of solar energy, the radiant energy emitted by the <a href="/wiki/Sun" title="Sun">suns</a>, or <a href="/wiki/An%27she" title="An&#39;she">An'she</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Class_overview">Class overview</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Class overview"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><i>Guardians of nature who seek to preserve balance and protect life, Druids have unmatched versatility on the field of battle. This is in part because druidism is much more than a fighting discipline—it’s a way of life steeped in traditions so ancient that even the origin of their kind is preserved largely in mythology passed on through millennia.</i>
</p><p><i>Druids harness nature’s raw energy for an incredible breadth of offensive and defensive abilities, as well as to restore life to the wounded. Through communion with nature and the demigod Cenarius, Lord of the Forest, Druids are supernaturally endowed with the gift of shapeshifting, allowing them to take the form of all manner of nature’s creature and access powers as distinct as they are diverse.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Class&#95;Preview&#95;6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Class_Preview-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Game_Guide">Game Guide</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Game Guide"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><i>Druids harness the vast powers of nature to preserve balance and protect life. With experience, druids can unleash nature’s raw energy against their enemies, raining celestial fury on them from a great distance, binding them with enchanted vines, or ensnaring them in unrelenting cyclones. Druids can also direct this power to heal wounds and restore life to fallen allies. They are deeply in tune with the animal spirits of Azeroth. As master shapeshifters, druids can take on the forms of a variety of beasts, morphing into a bear, cat, storm crow, or sea lion with ease. This flexibility allows them to fill different roles during their adventures, tearing enemies to shreds one minute and surveying the battlefield from the sky the next. These keepers of the natural order are among the most versatile heroes in Azeroth, and they must be prepared to adjust to new challenges on a moment’s notice.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Creation_screen_description">Creation screen description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Creation screen description"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><i>Druids live in a state of unparalleled union with nature. Tightly bound to the plant and animal kingdoms, they are natural shapeshifters, and so they know firsthand the abuse visited on their wild brethren. In consequence, despite their numbers, druids tend to be wary, reclusive, and difficult to spot. Few outsiders have plumbed the depths of their secrets.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<dl><dd><i>Druids are shape-shifters with an affinity for the plants and animal kingdoms. Druids can specialize in healing, casting spells at range, or taking the form of a cat or bear to fight in melee.</i></dd></dl>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Classic_guide">Classic guide</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Classic guide"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p><i>Druids call upon the versatility of nature to empower themselves, assuming the forms of primal beasts, summoning celestial energy, and channeling the restorative properties of the wilds to aid their allies.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Background"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>See also: <a href="/wiki/Druid_races" title="Druid races">Druid races</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Cenarius1.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Cenarius1.jpg/320px-Cenarius1.jpg?2d43f2" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="408" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1338" data-file-height="1706" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Cenarius1.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Cenarius" title="Cenarius">Cenarius</a> instructs <a href="/wiki/Malfurion" class="mw-redirect" title="Malfurion">Malfurion</a>, <a href="/wiki/Tyrande" class="mw-redirect" title="Tyrande">Tyrande</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Illidan" class="mw-redirect" title="Illidan">Illidan</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>The demigod <a href="/wiki/Cenarius" title="Cenarius">Cenarius</a> originally gave guidance to and lived amongst the nomadic <a href="/wiki/Yaungol" title="Yaungol">yaungol</a> in the grassy plains of central Kalimdor between the 12,000 and 11,900 <a href="/wiki/BDP" title="BDP">BDP</a>. To them he taught the secrets of the wilds and of druidic magic.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This later resulted in <a href="/wiki/Tauren" title="Tauren">tauren</a> <a href="/wiki/Tauren_mythology" title="Tauren mythology">mythology</a> telling how they had been instructed themselves as the first druids by Cenarius,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> thought that was only a reinterpretation of what had happened to their ancestors.
</p><p>With the rise of the <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elves</a>, a few of them learned in turn of Cenarius, and those left touched and changed, some even becoming so transformed by his teachings that they instead chose to join the demigod in the protection of his realm. Those were no longer elves, but woodland guardians physically altered forever. But of all these followers, none of them truly followed the path Cenarius had for them until <a href="/wiki/Malfurion_Stormrage" title="Malfurion Stormrage">Malfurion Stormrage</a>. The young prodigy revealed himself to be the first with the possible aptitude and will to truly understand how to wield the forces inherent in all nature,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Well4&#95;12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Well4-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> to become the first real mortal druid on Azeroth<sup id="cite&#95;ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and to walk the <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Dream" title="Emerald Dream">Emerald Dream</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Well4&#95;12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Well4-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Others such as <a href="/wiki/Valewalker_Farodin" title="Valewalker Farodin">Valewalker Farodin</a> had dabbled in nature magic before, but without being true druids themselves.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Malfurion would lead the night elves through the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ancients" title="War of the Ancients">War of the Ancients</a> and the long years that followed. With the creation of the <a href="/wiki/World_Tree" title="World Tree">World Tree</a> <a href="/wiki/Nordrassil" title="Nordrassil">Nordrassil</a>, the kaldorei druids were bound to <a href="/wiki/Ysera" title="Ysera">Ysera</a> and her realm, the <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Dream" title="Emerald Dream">Emerald Dream</a>, in which they spent centuries of their immortal lives. The <a href="/wiki/Cenarion_Circle" title="Cenarion Circle">Cenarion Circle</a> was eventually formed as the governing society of druids, based in sacred <a href="/wiki/Moonglade" title="Moonglade">Moonglade</a>.
</p><p>Locked in slumber in their <a href="/wiki/Barrow_den" title="Barrow den">barrow dens</a> for generations, the druids awoke to meet the threat of the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> during its recent invasion. The Legion's attack left a terrible scar on the natural order and, after <a href="/wiki/Archimonde" title="Archimonde">Archimonde</a>'s defeat, the druids chose to remain in the waking world and help rebuild their shattered lands.
</p><p>The night elf druids were recently joined in the <a href="/wiki/Cenarion_Circle" title="Cenarion Circle">Cenarion Circle</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Tauren" title="Tauren">tauren</a> druids. It is not clear from the current game content if the tauren learned druidism separately from the night elves or if they were taught by the night elves. Conflicting information in the <a href="/wiki/Warcraft_universe" title="Warcraft universe">Warcraft universe</a> further exacerbates the confusion. Either way, the tauren and the night elf druids do not participate in the Alliance/Horde conflict, but coexist peacefully within the Cenarion Circle.
</p><p>Early <a href="/wiki/Troll" title="Troll">troll</a> tribes, though varying wildly in customs and traditions, all shared a common religion: the worship and reverence for the Wild Gods whom they called <a href="/wiki/Loa" title="Loa">Loa</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Those gods granted their followers various boons, many of whom resembeled those abilities that the druids possess, such as shapeshifting into animal forms, resembling those of the loa they worshiped<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Zen&#95;16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Zen-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They even knew how to control the forces of nature, the <a href="/wiki/Haruspex" title="Haruspex">haruspices</a> shaped it by their need, not the other way around, like how druids do it. But when <a href="/wiki/Zalazane" title="Zalazane">Zalazane</a> trapped the loa inside the Emerald Dream, <a href="/wiki/Gonk" title="Gonk">Gonk</a> showed his followers how to connect with all the spirits of nature, to work with them, and not serve only one loa at the time, even teaching them how to reach into the Emerald Dream, which allowed them to become proper druids, an act that wasn't well accepted by most of the other loa.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Zen&#95;16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Zen-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In Zandalar, Gonk has pushed for a progressive movement in society to allow the worship of many loa, championed in voice by <a href="/wiki/Loti" class="mw-redirect" title="Loti">Loti</a>, leading to a resurgence of druidism in Zandalar.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Gilnean druids were called <a href="/wiki/Harvest-witch" title="Harvest-witch">harvest-witches</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Celestine_of_the_Harvest" title="Celestine of the Harvest">Celestine</a> also refers to her druidic order as "keepers of the old ways". The harvest-witches would use their primitive druidic skills to cultivate crops but almost "died out" as Gilneas developed, though, when the nation fell into famine, it was the druids who, as Celestine claims, "called upon the earth's blessing and restored the harvest".<sup id="cite&#95;ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When <a href="/wiki/Kul_Tiras_(kingdom)" title="Kul Tiras (kingdom)">Kul Tiras</a> was founded by Gilnean sailors, it is possible that some harvest-witches brought some of their teachings, but modern Kul Tiran druids claim only the <a href="/wiki/Thornspeakers" title="Thornspeakers">Thornspeakers</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Drust" title="Drust">Drust</a> <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a> <a href="/wiki/Ulfar" title="Ulfar">Ulfar</a> as the source of their druidry, who says some of his people became part of human society and their descendants hear the "call of the wild".<sup id="cite&#95;ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Druids belong to many different <a href="/wiki/Druid_organizations" title="Druid organizations">organizations</a> or sects such as the <a href="/wiki/Cenarion_Circle" title="Cenarion Circle">Cenarion Circle</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cenarion_Expedition" title="Cenarion Expedition">Cenarion Expedition</a>, <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Claw" title="Druids of the Claw">Druids of the Claw</a>, <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Talon" title="Druids of the Talon">Druids of the Talon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Fang" title="Druids of the Fang">Druids of the Fang</a>, <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Grove" class="mw-redirect" title="Druids of the Grove">Druids of the Grove</a>, <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Antler" title="Druids of the Antler">Druids of the Antler</a> and <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Scythe" title="Druids of the Scythe">Druids of the Scythe</a>.
</p><p>It usually takes most druids a brief time to shapeshift, and it can rarely be done in a few seconds.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Druids generally cannot speak while in their animal forms, but there are some exceptions.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> For example, <a href="/wiki/Gaivan_Shadewalker" title="Gaivan Shadewalker">Gaivan Shadewalker</a>, <a href="/wiki/Archdruid_Glaidalis" title="Archdruid Glaidalis">Archdruid Glaidalis</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Arthur_Tradewind" title="Arthur Tradewind">Arthur Tradewind</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are all able to speak while shapeshifted. This may be possible through alternative, magical means of communication, similar to how jawless undead are capable of speech.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Warcraft_Encyclopedia">Warcraft Encyclopedia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Warcraft Encyclopedia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:267px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Lunar_Barrage.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Lunar_Barrage.jpg/265px-Lunar_Barrage.jpg?7fcfd3" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="265" height="208" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="1253" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Lunar_Barrage.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>A moonkin casting a lunar spell.</div></div></div>
<p>Druids come and go as they please, and their goals typically have little to do with the "civilized" world. Unaffiliated with any specific government, the primary druidic organization on Azeroth, the Cenarion Circle, answers to no one save itself. The highest rank that a druid can have is that of '<a href="/wiki/Archdruid" title="Archdruid">archdruid</a>', and there are only a few archdruids on Azeroth. At the moment the Cenarion Circle's leader is Archdruid Malfurion Stormrage, also the first mortal druid on Azeroth.
</p><p>Through their deep connection to life and nature, druids are able to take on an unusually large variety of roles. Probably a druid's best-known role is that of a healer. Druids are justly famed for their ability to restore life, cure poisonous wounds, and remove curses. Indeed, restoring and protecting the planet is one of the top priorities for all druids, however, restoring life comes with a price. Even the newest druid understands that life cannot simply be created from nothing<sup id="cite&#95;ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and must ask nature to sacrifice their life essence in order to transfer that life essence to the people and plants being healed.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, to regard a druid as merely a healer is a dangerously simplistic assumption that has led to the defeat of many an unwary foe.
</p><p>A druid's empathy with the creatures of the wild also allows him to shapeshift into the forms of other animals. For example, a wounded druid whose magical energies are running low might abruptly take on the form of a bear in order to better withstand further injury. Alternatively, the druid might shift into the form of some large cat, sneak up behind an enemy, then pounce and deliver a fierce flurry of melee attacks. Even the deepest oceans can be explored by a druid, who can simply shift into an aquatic creature, allowing him to stay underwater as long as needed.
</p><p>Until the end of the Third War, druids periodically visited the Emerald Dream to monitor the ebb and flow of life on Azeroth. Today such a visit has become more difficult due to Nordrassil's poor health. Druids possess a deep understanding of the way in which all living things depend upon one another. Whenever this delicate equilibrium is disrupted, the druid works to restore the balance. To that end, a druid can use his connection with the Emerald Dream to exert a calming influence over animals, even forcing some into a temporary state of hibernation. Just as the druid can wield peace and somnolence, the druid can tap into the fury of nature itself: terrible storms have responded to a druid's call; thorns have sprouted from the druid's skin to wound his attackers; even previously harmless roots have grown up out of the soil at greatly accelerated speeds in order to entangle a foe.
</p><p>For obvious reasons, druids thrive in the wild outdoors. Inevitably, however, they lose some of their effectiveness when they are forced to endure separation from the natural environment. What good is a cleansing rain when a druid cannot even see the sky? How are roots to capture a foe who is standing atop a high tower? Such cases clearly require a druid with the imagination to consider new strategies and the flexibility to carry them out.
</p><p>Yet this flexibility, too, comes with an underlying negative corollary. The druid tends to be a jack-of-all-trades, but a master of none. An unusual mixture of priest, rogue, and warrior, the druid is therefore not equal to any of these three classes in their particular specialties. Even so, the druids' incredible adaptability serves them well in confronting the challenges that Azeroth has to offer.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Note_about_races">Note about races</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=11" title="Edit section: Note about races"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Druids utilize various forms for each specialization, and each race has a different-looking skin for their forms (<a href="/wiki/Bear_Form" title="Bear Form">Bear</a>, <a href="/wiki/Cat_Form" title="Cat Form">Cat</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Moonkin_Form" title="Moonkin Form">Moonkin</a>). Beginning in Shadowlands, Druids can now change the skin and coloration of each of their forms at the Barbershop, including using Legion Artifact forms without transmogrifying the weapon itself, although there are still race-specific skins that cannot be used by other races. Prior to this, the Bear and Cat forms were colored based on hair color when new models were introduced in Wrath of the Lich King, so in Classic versions of the game, it would be wise to research what hair color corresponds with each form's color.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Racial_abilities">Racial abilities</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=12" title="Edit section: Racial abilities"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Below is a list of every race that can be a Druid, their <a href="/wiki/Racial_trait" title="Racial trait">racial traits</a> and how they can be relevant (or not) to druids:

</p>
<ul><li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_ambush.png/16px-Ability_ambush.png?a8904b" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Shadowmeld" title="Shadowmeld"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Shadowmeld<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> is a <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_vanish.png/16px-Ability_vanish.png?82cf9f" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Vanish" title="Vanish"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Vanish<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>-like ability. It can be useful in <a href="/wiki/Aggro" title="Aggro">aggro</a> management for <a href="/wiki/DPS" class="mw-redirect" title="DPS">DPS</a> in <a href="/wiki/PvE" class="mw-redirect" title="PvE">PvE</a>, although the aggro drop is only temporary. <li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_spiritarmor.png/16px-Spell_nature_spiritarmor.png?244f3c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Nature_Resistance_(racial)" title="Nature Resistance (racial)"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Nature Resistance<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> grants them a 1% reduction to all Nature damage taken.</li>
<li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_racial_shadowmeld.png/16px-Ability_racial_shadowmeld.png?2dac42" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Quickness" title="Quickness"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Quickness<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> increases the Druid's dodge change by 2% for both melee and ranged attacks, as well as increasing their movement speed by 2%.</li>
<li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_holy_elunesgrace.png/16px-Spell_holy_elunesgrace.png?9cbac9" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Touch_of_Elune" title="Touch of Elune"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Touch of Elune<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> increases the Druid's haste by 1% during the night, and their Critical Strike by 1% during the day.</li>
<li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_wispsplode.png/16px-Spell_nature_wispsplode.png?3728af" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Wisp_Spirit" title="Wisp Spirit"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Wisp Spirit<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> changes the player into a wisp upon death, with 75% more speed, allowing faster death recovery.</li></ul>

</p>
<ul><li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_racial_darkflight.png/16px-Ability_racial_darkflight.png?348a2a" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Darkflight" title="Darkflight"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Darkflight<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> is a <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_dash.png/16px-Ability_druid_dash.png?6de64a" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Dash_(druid_ability)" title="Dash (druid ability)"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Dash<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>-like ability with a 2 minute cooldown that can be useful in PvP or PvE. <h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Specializations">Specializations</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=13" title="Edit section: Specializations"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<p>Druids are the only class that has four <a href="/wiki/Specialization" title="Specialization">specializations</a>, which are:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Druid_abilities#Balance" title="Druid abilities">Balance</a> - <a href="/wiki/Caster" title="Caster">Caster</a> <a href="/wiki/Damage_(role)" title="Damage (role)">DPS</a></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Druid_abilities#Feral" title="Druid abilities">Feral</a> - <a href="/wiki/Melee" title="Melee">Melee</a> <a href="/wiki/Damage_(role)" title="Damage (role)">DPS</a></li>

<li><a href="/wiki/Druid_abilities#Restoration" title="Druid abilities">Restoration</a> - <a href="/wiki/Healer_(role)" title="Healer (role)">Healer</a></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Balance">Balance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=14" title="Edit section: Balance"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="floatright"><a href="/wiki/File:Druid_preview_Balance.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Druid preview Balance.jpg" src="/images/thumb/Druid_preview_Balance.jpg/300px-Druid_preview_Balance.jpg?5b804e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="150" data-file-width="960" data-file-height="480" /></a></div>
<div class="floatleft"><a href="/wiki/Druid_abilities#Specializations" title="Druid abilities"><img alt="Spell nature starfall.png" src="/images/Spell_nature_starfall.png?6bfb68" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="64" height="64" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" /></a></div> <p><i><b>Can take on the form of a powerful Moonkin, balancing the power of Arcane and Nature magic to destroy enemies at a distance.</b></i>
</p><p>Balance druids focus on dealing <a href="/wiki/Caster" title="Caster">ranged magical</a> <a href="/wiki/Damage_(role)" title="Damage (role)">damage</a>, with an array of signature abilities such as <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_arcane_arcane03.png/16px-Spell_arcane_arcane03.png?9e12b9" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Starsurge" title="Starsurge"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Starsurge<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_mage_firestarter.png/16px-Ability_mage_firestarter.png?cd2e73" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Sunfire" title="Sunfire"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Sunfire<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_vehicle_sonicshockwave.png/16px-Ability_vehicle_sonicshockwave.png?84fb20" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Solar_Beam" title="Solar Beam"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Solar Beam<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_starfall.png/16px-Ability_druid_starfall.png?e6000d" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Starfall" title="Starfall"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Starfall<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. Balance druids use <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_forceofnature.png/16px-Spell_nature_forceofnature.png?732d46" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Moonkin_Form" title="Moonkin Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Moonkin Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> to increase their damage dealt, protect them from harm and grant spell <a href="/wiki/Haste" title="Haste">haste</a> to themselves and their party. As well as granting Balance druids a distinctive <a href="/wiki/Owlkin" class="mw-redirect" title="Owlkin">owlkin</a> appearance (although this can be changed with <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_glyph_minordruid.png/16px-Inv_glyph_minordruid.png?8c8d3b" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Glyph_of_Stars" title="Glyph of Stars"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Glyph of Stars is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Glyph of Stars<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>), this form has also gained Balance druids nicknames such as "Boomkin", reflecting their potential for extremely high damage output.
</p><p>As of the <i>Legion</i> expansion, Balance druids no longer primarily use mana as a resource. Instead, using their spells such as <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_starfall.png/16px-Spell_nature_starfall.png?6bfb68" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Moonfire" title="Moonfire"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Moonfire<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_wrathv2.png/16px-Spell_nature_wrathv2.png?42be28" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Solar_Wrath" title="Solar Wrath"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Solar Wrath<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, they generate <a href="/wiki/Astral_Power" title="Astral Power">Astral Power</a>, which is used to cast the abilities <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_arcane_arcane03.png/16px-Spell_arcane_arcane03.png?9e12b9" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Starsurge" title="Starsurge"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Starsurge<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_starfall.png/16px-Ability_druid_starfall.png?e6000d" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Starfall" title="Starfall"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Starfall<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>.
</p><p><i>The elements that carve form into the universe are fluid forces of nature. Some beings seek to bend the power of these natural elements to their will. Druids, however, worship the protecting spirits of nature. Long ago, nature’s equilibrium was thrown out of balance, leaving the world vulnerable to catastrophic events, including the first invasion of the Burning Legion.</i>
</p><p><i>By leveraging the sacred powers of the moon, the sun, and the stars, balance druids access arcane and nature magics—made more potent still through shapeshifting, when the spellcaster takes the form of the moonkin—to aid in the fight against imbalance that threatens the natural order of all things.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Class&#95;Preview&#95;6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Class_Preview-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Feral">Feral</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=15" title="Edit section: Feral"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="floatright"><a href="/wiki/File:Druid_preview_Feral.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Druid preview Feral.jpg" src="/images/thumb/Druid_preview_Feral.jpg/300px-Druid_preview_Feral.jpg?618d08" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="150" data-file-width="960" data-file-height="480" /></a></div>
<div class="floatleft"><a href="/wiki/Druid_abilities#Specializations" title="Druid abilities"><img alt="Ability druid catform.png" src="/images/Ability_druid_catform.png?a8895e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="64" height="64" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" /></a></div> <p><i><b>Takes on the form of a great cat to deal damage with bleeds and bites.</b></i>
</p><p>Feral druids are powerful feline <a href="/wiki/Melee" title="Melee">melee</a> <a href="/wiki/Damage_(role)" title="Damage (role)">damage-dealers</a>, using <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_catform.png/16px-Ability_druid_catform.png?a8895e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Cat_Form" title="Cat Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Cat Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> to deal swift and savage damage to their enemies. They have much in common with <a href="/wiki/Rogue" title="Rogue">rogues</a>, using <a href="/wiki/Energy" title="Energy">energy</a> to fuel their attacks and <a href="/wiki/Combo_point" title="Combo point">combo points</a> to build to deadly <a href="/wiki/Finishing_move" title="Finishing move">finishing moves</a>. Like rogues, Feral druids are extremely agile, and can use <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_prowl.png/16px-Ability_druid_prowl.png?a09842" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Prowl" title="Prowl"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Prowl<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> to sneak around and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_supriseattack.png/16px-Ability_druid_supriseattack.png?9ae555" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Pounce" title="Pounce"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Pounce<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> on enemies when they least expect it. Feral druids are often called "Cat", "Kitten" or "Kitty", these affectionate nicknames belying their formidable ferocity.
</p><p><i>Feral druids also seek to curb imbalance in nature. They observe the fantastic intricacies of the physical world and the delicate fabric in which all living creatures are given purpose. Whether on land or in the sea, in a lush jungle or an uncultivated desert, death is part of the cycle which sustains life. Nature is an eternal dance between predator and prey.</i>
</p><p><i>As a shapeshifter, the feral druid endlessly pursues a greater understanding of this truth. They seek a visceral connection to the wild, and in combat take the form of a deadly feline predator. Feral druids become ferocious, agile stalkers—ripping, biting, and bleeding their enemies dry. The forces of chaos are tamed by the druid’s primal fury.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Class&#95;Preview&#95;6-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Class_Preview-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Guardian">Guardian</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=16" title="Edit section: Guardian"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="floatright"><a href="/wiki/File:Druid_preview_Guardian.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Druid preview Guardian.jpg" src="/images/thumb/Druid_preview_Guardian.jpg/300px-Druid_preview_Guardian.jpg?ab7c29" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="150" data-file-width="960" data-file-height="480" /></a></div>
<div class="floatleft"><a href="/wiki/Druid_abilities#Specializations" title="Druid abilities"><img alt="Ability racial bearform.png" src="/images/Ability_racial_bearform.png?ef2632" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="64" height="64" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" /></a></div> <p><i><b>Takes on the form of a mighty bear to absorb damage and protect allies.</b></i>
</p><p>Guardian druids are indomitable <a href="/wiki/Tank" title="Tank">tanks</a>, using the strength of <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_racial_bearform.png/16px-Ability_racial_bearform.png?ef2632" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Bear_Form" title="Bear Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Bear Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> to become a towering, snarling wall between themselves and their allies. Similar to Protection <a href="/wiki/Warrior" title="Warrior">warriors</a>, Guardian druids use <a href="/wiki/Rage" title="Rage">rage</a> to fuel their abilities, mauling and mangling their enemies and transforming their rage into health with <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_bullrush.png/16px-Ability_bullrush.png?249264" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Frenzied_Regeneration" title="Frenzied Regeneration"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Frenzied Regeneration<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. Guardian druids' Bear Form is improved with <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_pelt_bear_03.png/16px-Inv_misc_pelt_bear_03.png?939da0" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Thick_Hide_(druid_talent)" title="Thick Hide (druid talent)"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Thick Hide<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, allowing them to square up to the most formidable of foes. Guardian druids are often known simply as "Bears".
</p><p><i>Like their feral brothers and sisters, guardian druids attune themselves to nature through the animal kingdom. They commune with the wild to understand how life perseveres through adversity. They know that the creatures who survive are often those built to best protect themselves and their kin. In this, the guardian druid finds a deep and harmonious value in the steadfast—all the while recognizing that sturdiness requires an aggressive stance when danger comes near.</i>
</p><p><i>Taking the form of a great bear, the guardian druid becomes a massive wall of fur, claw, tooth, and rage, aided by the forces of nature, standing between allies and any opposing threats.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Class&#95;Preview&#95;6-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Class_Preview-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Restoration">Restoration</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=17" title="Edit section: Restoration"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="floatright"><a href="/wiki/File:Druid_preview_Restoration.jpg" class="image"><img alt="Druid preview Restoration.jpg" src="/images/thumb/Druid_preview_Restoration.jpg/300px-Druid_preview_Restoration.jpg?ce5ef4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="150" data-file-width="960" data-file-height="480" /></a></div>
<div class="floatleft"><a href="/wiki/Druid_abilities#Specializations" title="Druid abilities"><img alt="Spell nature healingtouch.png" src="/images/Spell_nature_healingtouch.png?6ef21f" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="64" height="64" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" /></a></div> <p><i><b>Uses heal-over-time Nature spells to keep allies alive.</b></i>
</p><p>Restoration druids are capable <a href="/wiki/Healer_(role)" title="Healer (role)">healers</a>, specializing in restoring their allies' health through <a href="/wiki/Heal_over_time" title="Heal over time">heal over time</a> effects. Restoration druids possess a range of healing options, from direct healing to more sophisticated buffs such as <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_giftoftheearthmother.png/16px-Ability_druid_giftoftheearthmother.png?ba215b" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Living_Seed" title="Living Seed"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Living Seed<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, with abilities such as <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_nourish.png/16px-Ability_druid_nourish.png?918bd6" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Nourish" title="Nourish"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Nourish<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> offering synergistic effects, and can also dispel with <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_shaman_cleansespirit.png/16px-Ability_shaman_cleansespirit.png?a38f2e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Nature%27s_Cure" title="Nature&#39;s Cure"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Nature's Cure<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. Restoration druids are capable of healing allies without the use of a special shapeshift form, but with the talent <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_druid_incarnation.png/16px-Spell_druid_incarnation.png?cf1691" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Incarnation" title="Incarnation"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Incarnation<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> can take on the form of the Tree of Life.
</p><p><i>The many gifts provided by nature must sometimes be reciprocated. Restoration druids seek order in the world by tending directly to its many life forms. Friend to flora and fauna alike, restoration druids celebrate birth and growth. Where there is decay, they bring rejuvenation. Where there is abatement, they summon regrowth. Life not only needs protection—it needs nourishment.</i>
</p><p><i>To foster this harmony, the restoration druid builds a bond with the things that grow, gaining inspiration from the flower’s bloom, the seed’s sprout, the mushroom’s spores, and the tree’s growth. Like nature, the restoration druid perseveres through patience and persistence, the foundation upon which all life is built and sustained. They use this power to mend wounds and provide persistent remedies that keep their allies from falling.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Class&#95;Preview&#95;6-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Class_Preview-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<dl><dd><i>For a full list of druid abilities, see <a href="/wiki/Druid_abilities" title="Druid abilities">Druid abilities</a></i></dd></dl>
<p>Druids have a range of magical and physical abilities. While each specialization focuses on strengthening a particular area of a druid's repertoire, all druids are capable of using <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_catform.png/16px-Ability_druid_catform.png?a8895e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Cat_Form" title="Cat Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Cat Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_racial_bearform.png/16px-Ability_racial_bearform.png?ef2632" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Bear_Form" title="Bear Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Bear Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, dealing physical and ranged magical damage, healing targets and using numerous other abilities.

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Shapeshifting">Shapeshifting</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=24" title="Edit section: Shapeshifting"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Druid_shapeshift_forms" title="Druid shapeshift forms">Druid shapeshift forms</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<p>The druid's most notable ability is shapeshifting into different forms. These following forms can be obtained when reaching a certain level: <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_forceofnature.png/16px-Spell_nature_forceofnature.png?732d46" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Moonkin_Form" title="Moonkin Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Moonkin Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> (Balance specialization only), <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_catform.png/16px-Ability_druid_catform.png?a8895e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Cat_Form" title="Cat Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Cat Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_racial_bearform.png/16px-Ability_racial_bearform.png?ef2632" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Bear_Form" title="Bear Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Bear Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_travelform.png/16px-Ability_druid_travelform.png?cf78ce" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Travel_Form" title="Travel Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Travel Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> (which increases speed in a form appropriate to the location used, whether it be land, water, or flying allowed).
</p><p>Many abilities are restricted to a certain form. Others have different forms or effects depending on the druid's form when it is used. Because Cat Form and Bear Form use energy and rage, respectively, rather than mana, druid abilities that use mana cannot be used by druids when they are in these forms. However, druids can quickly shift between forms to gain access to desired abilities.
</p><p>For all forms, the following applies:
</p>
<ul><li>Immune to <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_polymorph.png/16px-Spell_nature_polymorph.png?639e38" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Polymorph" title="Polymorph"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Polymorph<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> in all forms except the default, humanoid form.</li>
<li>You can't switch from polymorphed humanoid to another form.</li>
<li>Shapeshifting breaks all <a href="/wiki/Snare" title="Snare">movement-impairing effects</a> (like <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_slow.png/16px-Spell_nature_slow.png?f9257f" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Slow" title="Slow"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Slow<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> or <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_shockwave.png/16px-Ability_shockwave.png?fb0098" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Hamstring" title="Hamstring"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Hamstring<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>) and <a href="/wiki/Root" title="Root">roots</a> (<span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_frost_frostnova.png/16px-Spell_frost_frostnova.png?988a26" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Frost_Nova" title="Frost Nova"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Frost Nova<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> or <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_stranglevines.png/16px-Spell_nature_stranglevines.png?d0bd35" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Entangling_Roots" title="Entangling Roots"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Entangling Roots<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>), except the 3-second daze that can proc when one is attacked from behind by a melee attack.</li>
<li>Depending on the form, the druid is considered either Humanoid (Caster and Moonkin), Beast (Travel, Aquatic, Bear, and Cat), or Elemental (Tree of Life); and thus is vulnerable (or immune) to different spells. This also applies to being tracked.</li>
<li>Shapeshifts are not buffs or magical effects, thus they may not be dispelled.</li>
<li>Mana regeneration still continues at the same speed as out of the form.</li>
<li>Weapons and armor that have the "Chance on hit" effect or have gained the said <a href="/wiki/Enchantment" title="Enchantment">enchantments</a> can still <a href="/wiki/Proc" title="Proc">proc</a> regardless of form.</li>
<li>Items with an "Equip: When hit ..." do work in forms. Some examples of these items are the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_jewelry_ring_05.png/16px-Inv_jewelry_ring_05.png?c88725" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Naglering" title="Naglering"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-rare" title="Naglering is a rare quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Naglering<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_ticket_tarot_wrath.png/16px-Inv_misc_ticket_tarot_wrath.png?2ab8c0" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Darkmoon_Card:_Wrath" title="Darkmoon Card: Wrath"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-epic" title="Darkmoon Card: Wrath is an epic quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Darkmoon Card: Wrath<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>'s damage, <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_chest_chain_09.png/16px-Inv_chest_chain_09.png?cff158" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Green_Whelp_Armor" title="Green Whelp Armor"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-uncommon" title="Green Whelp Armor is an uncommon quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Green Whelp Armor<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> sleep.</li></ul>
<p>If you shapeshift while wearing any <a href="/wiki/Disguise" class="mw-redirect" title="Disguise">disguise</a>, your form buff will remove the disguise buff. This is because it has a blanket effect and can cause unexpected bugs when two buffs are stacked. Items like the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_potion_83.png/16px-Inv_potion_83.png?1273da" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Noggenfogger_Elixir" title="Noggenfogger Elixir"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Noggenfogger Elixir is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Noggenfogger Elixir<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> were changed in 2007 to not stack with Druid forms so that it would not cause any future problems. Subsequent issues have arisen, such as the jet-pack required for <a href="/wiki/Gunship_Battle" class="mw-redirect" title="Gunship Battle">Gunship Battle</a>, giving the message "Cannot use whilst shape-shifted". This can make completing some achievements more challenging for feral and balance druids, such as <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Achievement_worldevent_littlehelper.png/16px-Achievement_worldevent_littlehelper.png?2f2cb9" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/With_a_Little_Helper_from_My_Friends" title="With a Little Helper from My Friends"><span class="ajaxttlink achievementlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>With a Little Helper from My Friends<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, requiring creative thinking such as spending the battles as a gunner in <a href="/wiki/Wintergrasp" title="Wintergrasp">Wintergrasp</a>.
</p><p>As of <a href="/wiki/5.0.4" class="mw-redirect" title="5.0.4">5.0.4</a>, druids can mount while in <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_forceofnature.png/16px-Spell_nature_forceofnature.png?732d46" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Moonkin_Form" title="Moonkin Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Moonkin Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Gear">Gear</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=25" title="Edit section: Gear"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>See also: <a href="/wiki/Druid_sets" title="Druid sets">Druid sets</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<p>Druids are able to wear <a href="/wiki/Cloth" title="Cloth">cloth</a> and <a href="/wiki/Leather" title="Leather">leather</a> <a href="/wiki/Armor" title="Armor">armor</a> only, and gain additional benefits from wearing completely leather. 
</p><p>Druids can equip <a href="/wiki/Staves" class="mw-redirect" title="Staves">staves</a>, <a href="/wiki/Dagger" title="Dagger">daggers</a>, one-handed or two-handed <a href="/wiki/Mace" title="Mace">maces</a>, <a href="/wiki/Fist_weapon" title="Fist weapon">fist weapons</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Polearm" title="Polearm">polearms</a>. "Held in off-hand" items can still be used, but they cannot <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_dualwield.png/16px-Ability_dualwield.png?43476f" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Dual_wield" title="Dual wield"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Dual Wield<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. 
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="In_the_RPG">In the RPG</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=26" title="Edit section: In the RPG"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/Warcraft_RPG" title="Warcraft RPG"><img alt="Icon-RPG.png" src="/images/Icon-RPG.png?d76c0c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="48" height="20" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="48" data-file-height="20" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section contains information from the <i><a href="/wiki/Warcraft_RPG" title="Warcraft RPG">Warcraft RPG</a></i> which is considered <a href="/wiki/Non-canon" class="mw-redirect" title="Non-canon">non-canon</a></b>.</span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>Druids are keepers of the world who walk the path of nature following the wisdom of the Ancients and Cenarius, healing and nurturing the world. To a druid, nature is a delicate balance of actions in which even the smallest imbalance can create storming turmoil from peaceful skies. Druids draw their power from this wild energy using it to change their shapes and command the forces of nature.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Traditionally, druids chose the path of a specific animal totem, however, since the invasion of the Burning Legion, most druids have undergone a number of reforms including encouraging the study of magic from all totems. Druids who do this are known as <a href="/wiki/Druid_of_the_Wild" class="mw-redirect" title="Druid of the Wild">druids of the wild</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes_and_trivia">Notes and trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=27" title="Edit section: Notes and trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The <a href="/wiki/Touch_of_nature" title="Touch of nature">Touch of nature</a> refers to ones ability to interact with nature itself, animals, plants, trees and generally all druidic characteristics.</li>
<li>There is a relation to the spiritual magic of <a href="/wiki/Shaman" title="Shaman">shaman</a> and the natural magic of druids, which is why the shaman were able to hear the spirits of <a href="/wiki/Stonetalon_Mountains" title="Stonetalon Mountains">Stonetalon Mountains</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Prior to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Mists_of_Pandaria" title="World of Warcraft: Mists of Pandaria">World of Warcraft: Mists of Pandaria</a></i>, druids had only three talent trees, with Feral being called Feral Combat originally. In these versions, many talents provided benefits to both Tanking and DPS capabilities, based upon which form the Druid was in (either <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_racial_bearform.png/16px-Ability_racial_bearform.png?ef2632" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Bear_Form" title="Bear Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Bear Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> or <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_catform.png/16px-Ability_druid_catform.png?a8895e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Cat_Form" title="Cat Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Cat Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>). Currently, they are the only class in the game with four talent trees.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos" title="Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos">Warcraft III</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Druid_of_the_Claw_(Warcraft_III)" title="Druid of the Claw (Warcraft III)">Druids of the Claw</a> wear bear pelts on their back and their shapeshift animation depicts them pulling the pelt's head over their face as they enter Bear Form, while <a href="/wiki/Druid_of_the_Talon_(Warcraft_III)" title="Druid of the Talon (Warcraft III)">Druids of the Talon</a> wear feathered cloaks which they sweep around themselves when they enter Crow Form. Similarly, during development, the writers played with the idea of having <a href="/wiki/Medivh" title="Medivh">Medivh</a>'s cloak be a night elf artifact that allows the wearer to turn into a raven.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Altogether, this indicates that the original idea behind druid shapeshifting was that they turn into animals by literally putting on the enchanted skins of the creatures in question. This concept was evidently abandoned after <i>Warcraft III</i>, as it has never been mentioned in <i>World of Warcraft</i> or any other sources.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Druid_of_the_wild" title="Druid of the wild">Druids of the wild</a> are mentioned in the <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Game_Manual" title="World of Warcraft: Game Manual">World of Warcraft: Game Manual</a></i> as having been trained by <a href="/wiki/Fandral_Staghelm" title="Fandral Staghelm">Fandral Staghelm</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This presumably refers to the druid <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a> that can shift between many animal shapes.</li>
<li>Druids replaced <a href="/wiki/Runemaster" title="Runemaster">runemasters</a> during the early development of <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft" title="World of Warcraft">World of Warcraft</a></i>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><i><a href="/wiki/Hearthstone_(game)" title="Hearthstone (game)">Hearthstone</a></i> has a number of different shapeshifted forms not seen in <i>World of Warcraft</i>, as can be seen with <a href="https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Druid_of_the_Swarm" class="extiw" title="hswiki:Druid of the Swarm">Druid of the Swarm</a> and <a href="https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Shellshifter" class="extiw" title="hswiki:Shellshifter">Shellshifter</a>.</li>
<li>The <i>Hearthstone</i> token card <a href="https://hearthstone.wiki.gg/wiki/Small_Repairs" class="extiw" title="hswiki:Small Repairs">Small Repairs</a> seems to feature a <a href="/wiki/Dwarf" title="Dwarf">dwarf</a> druid.</li>
<li>The description of the Wildstalker hero talent tree was changed slightly during the development of <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_The_War_Within" title="World of Warcraft: The War Within">The War Within</a></i>. When first announced, it read: <i>Wildstalkers are druids who feel such an affinity for the remote wilds of the world that they live amongst them, mostly in cat form, hunting to perpetuate the cycle of life and death and destroy those who would despoil nature. They use their healing powers to restore life to barren spaces and the creatures who live there.</i><sup id="cite&#95;ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
<ul><li>This early conception of Wildstalkers resembles that of the <a href="/wiki/Savagekin" title="Savagekin">savagekin</a> druids from the non-canon <i><a href="/wiki/Alliance_Player%27s_Guide" title="Alliance Player&#39;s Guide">Alliance Player's Guide</a></i>.</li></ul></li>
<li>The druid class inspired the design of <a rel="nofollow" class="external text" href="https://overwatch.fandom.com/wiki/Lifeweaver">Lifeweaver</a>, a hero in <i><a href="/wiki/Overwatch_franchise" title="Overwatch franchise">Overwatch 2</a></i>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Patch_changes">Patch changes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Druid?action=edit&amp;section=29" title="Edit section: Patch changes"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 50px; text-align:center"><span class="icon sl-inline"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands" title="Shadowlands"><img alt="Shadowlands" src="/images/thumb/Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png?d84a63" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="48" height="17" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span></span> <b><a href="/wiki/Patch_9.0.1" title="Patch 9.0.1">Patch 9.0.1</a> (2020-10-13):</b> Druid forms can now be customized in the Barbershop.   <i></i><br /></li>
<li><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 50px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Mists_of_Pandaria" title="Mists of Pandaria"><img alt="Mists of Pandaria" src="/images/Mists-Logo-Small.png?f96b23" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="46" height="14" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="46" data-file-height="14" /></a></span> <b><a href="/wiki/Patch_5.0.4" title="Patch 5.0.4">Patch 5.0.4</a> (2012-08-28):</b>
<ul><li>Feral Combat splits into two different specs: Feral (melee) and Guardian (tank).</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Relic_(slot)" class="mw-redirect" title="Relic (slot)">Relics</a> have been removed from the game.</li>
<li>Druids can now mount while in <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_forceofnature.png/16px-Spell_nature_forceofnature.png?732d46" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Moonkin_Form" title="Moonkin Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Moonkin Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>.   <i></i><br /></li></ul></li>
<li><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 50px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm" title="Cataclysm"><img alt="Cataclysm" src="/images/Cata-Logo-Small.png?cd16d8" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="39" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="39" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <b><a href="/wiki/Patch_4.2.0" title="Patch 4.2.0">Patch 4.2.0</a> (2011-06-28):</b> Druids now gain 1 <a href="/wiki/Attack_power" title="Attack power">attack power</a> per point of <a href="/wiki/Strength" class="mw-redirect" title="Strength">Strength</a>, down from 2. They continue to gain 2 attack power per point of <a href="/wiki/Agility" class="mw-redirect" title="Agility">Agility</a> while in <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_catform.png/16px-Ability_druid_catform.png?a8895e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Cat_Form" title="Cat Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Cat Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> or <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_racial_bearform.png/16px-Ability_racial_bearform.png?ef2632" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Bear_Form" title="Bear Form"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Bear Form<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. In addition, Cat Form's scaling rate from gear upgrades was slower than other classes, which was causing them to fall behind in damage with higher item levels. To counter the Strength change and improve scaling, several abilities had their coefficients modified.   <i></i><br /></li>
<li><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 50px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm" title="Cataclysm"><img alt="Cataclysm" src="/images/Cata-Logo-Small.png?cd16d8" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="39" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="39" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <b><a href="/wiki/Patch_4.0.1" title="Patch 4.0.1">Patch 4.0.1</a> (2010-10-12):</b>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Worgen_(playable)" title="Worgen (playable)">Worgen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Troll_(playable)" title="Troll (playable)">trolls</a> become the third and fourth races capable of becoming druids.</li>
<li>Duration of Restoration HoTs reduced from, e.g. 27 to 6 seconds for <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_resistnature.png/16px-Spell_nature_resistnature.png?1f6716" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Regrowth" title="Regrowth"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Regrowth<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>.</li>
<li><span id="Ability:Class&#95;specific&#95;quest&#95;chains&#95;removed"><b>Class specific quest chains removed </b>&#160;&#8212;&#160; druids who completed the Epic Flight Form quest chain before the patch now have a <a href="/wiki/Feat_of_Strength" class="mw-redirect" title="Feat of Strength">Feat of Strength</a>.</span></li>
<li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_eclipseorange.png/16px-Ability_druid_eclipseorange.png?6199a5" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Eclipse" title="Eclipse"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Eclipse<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> has been reworked and the <a href="/wiki/Eclipse_Bar" title="Eclipse Bar">Eclipse Bar</a> added.   <i></i><br /></li></ul></li>
<li><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 50px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King" title="Wrath of the Lich King"><img alt="Wrath of the Lich King" src="/images/Wrath-Logo-Small.png?6705fd" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="36" height="19" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="36" data-file-height="19" /></a></span> <b><a href="/wiki/Patch_3.2.0" title="Patch 3.2.0">Patch 3.2.0</a> (2009-08-04):</b>
<ul><li>If you already possess a 310% speed mount, your Swift Flight Form's speed will increase to 310%.</li>
<li>Druids are able to customize the appearance of their Feral forms.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>   <i></i><br /></li></ul></li>
<li><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 50px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King" title="Wrath of the Lich King"><img alt="Wrath of the Lich King" src="/images/Wrath-Logo-Small.png?6705fd" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="36" height="19" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="36" data-file-height="19" /></a></span> <b><a href="/wiki/Patch_3.0.2" title="Patch 3.0.2">Patch 3.0.2</a> (2008-10-14):</b> Druids received the regular resurrection spell <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Ability_druid_lunarguidance.png/16px-Ability_druid_lunarguidance.png?7ee323" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Revive" title="Revive"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Revive<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>.   <i></i><br /></li>
<li><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 50px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/Public_Test_Realm" title="Test"><img alt="Test" src="/images/Test-inline.png?3b2330" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="32" height="18" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="32" data-file-height="18" /></a></span> <b><a href="/wiki/Patch_0.6" title="Patch 0.6">Patch 0.6</a> (2004-04-13):</b> Added.   <i></i><br /></li></ul>
