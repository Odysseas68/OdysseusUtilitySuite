'''The Wardens''' are [[Night elf|night elven]] [[warden]]s loyal to [[Maiev Shadowsong]] operating in the [[Broken Isles]]. They have worked from their base of operations, the [[Isle of the Watchers]] in [[Azsuna]], to hunt down and neutralize major threats to [[Azeroth]]'s inhabitants, through imprisonment or summary execution. Captured foes were interned in the [[Vault of the Wardens]]. 

They reward the heroes who help them deal with dangerous threats on the [[Broken Isles]]. The faction is known to possess [[Warden Towers]] across the Broken Isles, although in recent times they have been forced to abandon many of them. There are two in most ''Legion'' zones that serve as PvP targets.

==History==
{{Main|Watchers}}
At the outset of the [[third invasion]] of Azeroth by the [[Burning Legion]], the [[demon]]s targeted the Vault and, with the aid of the traitor warden [[Cordana Felsong]], they succeeded in overrunning the prison complex and retrieving the corpse of [[Illidan Stormrage]]. [[Maiev Shadowsong]] was captured during the conflict and taken to [[Black Rook Hold]]. The Wardens worked with the [[Illidari]] to fight back the invading demons.<ref name="Working With the Wardens"/>

In Shadowsong's absence, {{NPC||Sira Moonwarden}} took up the mantle of leadership.<ref name=DealWithIt/> The Wardens continued their duties of upholding justice and safeguarding the world's inhabitants, directing [[Adventurer|order leaders]] to eliminate various threats on the [[Broken Isles]]. It is implied that the vast majority of the Wardens' ranks have been decimated by recent events,<ref>[[Vault of the Wardens: A Grim Matter]]</ref> making it necessary for them to [[Warden Trainee|train new recruits]], and to directly ask [[adventurer]]s to hunt down prisoners who have escaped from the [[Vault of the Wardens]].<ref>[[:Category:The Wardens quests|Warden quests]]</ref>

Following the creation of the [[Armies of Legionfall]], members of the Wardens, including the now freed Maiev and several [[Warden Vigilant]]s, participated in the [[Assault on Broken Shore]].
