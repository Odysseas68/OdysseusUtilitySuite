They will be formed from the vrykul, and their bravery shall preserve their brethren as <a href="/wiki/Valarjar" title="Valarjar">Valarjar</a> for all time. Like life, they shall be powerful. <p><b>Val'kyr</b> are a type of ascended female <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a>. The original Val'kyr are warriors of the <a href="/wiki/Light" title="Light">Light</a> created to serve the Prime Designate <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a> by bringing the spirits of the glorious dead to his <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a>. The Val'kyr of the <a href="/wiki/Scourge" title="Scourge">Scourge</a> acted as the <a href="/wiki/Lich_King" title="Lich King">Lich King</a>'s agents in the spirit world and were described by <a href="/wiki/Thoralius_the_Wise" title="Thoralius the Wise">Thoralius the Wise</a> as fearsome creatures made of pure nightmare.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Echo&#95;of&#95;Ymiron&#95;3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Echo_of_Ymiron-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the fall of <a href="/wiki/Arthas" class="mw-redirect" title="Arthas">Arthas</a>, several Val'kyr came to serve the <a href="/wiki/Forsaken" title="Forsaken">Forsaken</a> under <a href="/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner" title="Sylvanas Windrunner">Sylvanas Windrunner</a>, and following her desertion, they followed her to serve under the <a href="/wiki/Zovaal" title="Zovaal">Zovaal</a>'s forces in the <a href="/wiki/Maw" class="mw-redirect" title="Maw">Maw</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The first Val'kyr were created in ancient times by the <a href="/wiki/Keeper" title="Keeper">titanic keeper</a> <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a>, who disagreed with the other keepers' uplifting of the <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Aspects" title="Dragon Aspects">Dragon Aspects</a> and decided to form his own elite army to protect <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>. He offered <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a> warriors who died in glorious combat the opportunity to become part of his <a href="/wiki/Valarjar" title="Valarjar">Valarjar</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a>. However, Odyn first had to find a way to take the spirits of these departed vrykul from the <a href="/wiki/Shadowlands" title="Shadowlands">Shadowlands</a> to the Halls of Valor.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Chronicle&#95;48&#95;4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chronicle_48-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With the help of his adopted daughter, the <a href="/wiki/Titan-forged" title="Titan-forged">titan-forged</a> sorceress <a href="/wiki/Helya" title="Helya">Helya</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-The&#95;Legend&#95;of&#95;Odyn&#95;2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-The_Legend_of_Odyn-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Odyn contacted the powerful spirit <a href="/wiki/Mueh%27zala" title="Mueh&#39;zala">Mueh'zala</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and sacrificed <a href="/wiki/Eye_of_the_Jailer" title="Eye of the Jailer">his eye</a> to him in exchange for the ability to gaze into the Shadowlands. There he saw the <a href="/wiki/Kyrian" title="Kyrian">kyrian</a> of <a href="/wiki/Bastion" title="Bastion">Bastion</a>, winged beings responsible for ferrying the souls of the dead to the afterlife, and decided to create his own beings based on them.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-SA&#95;Gamer&#95;6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA_Gamer-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> These "Val'kyr" would travel between the Shadowlands and the physical world to guide souls to the Halls of Valor, but would also be cursed to a wraithlike existence for eternity. Since no vrykul volunteered for the task, Odyn decided to create his servants by force, a plan Helya vehemently objected to. Seeing Helya's disobedience as a threat to his plans, Odyn shattered her physical form, twisted her spirit into the first Val'kyr, and ordered her to begin transforming other unwilling vrykul into Val'kyr as well. Helya was forced to obey Odyn's commands<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Chronicle&#95;48&#95;4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chronicle_48-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> but nursed resentment against him for millennia, ultimately causing her to rebel against him with the aid of <a href="/wiki/Loken" title="Loken">Loken</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Chronicle&#95;57&#95;7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chronicle_57-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the current day, Valarjar Val'kyr are ascended warriors of the <a href="/wiki/Valkyra" title="Valkyra">Valkyra</a>, who prove their worth to <a href="/wiki/Eyir" title="Eyir">Eyir</a> in order to become Val'kyr.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As Val'kyr, they take worthy vrykul warriors who die with glory in battle to the Halls of Valor, where they will feast and battle for eternity as part of Odyn's Valarjar. Val'kyr are considered "sunborne" when they are honored in the presence of Odyn.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They are also considered warriors of the <a href="/wiki/Light" title="Light">Light</a>, which Odyn also follows.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Exploring&#95;Azeroth&#95;10-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Exploring_Azeroth-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>From the vrykul in his service, the Lich King learned of the Val'kyr and their ability to corral spirits and even delve into the Shadowlands, the realm of death. The Lich King tried to create his own Val'kyr, but the task proved difficult even for him. After missteps, he finally succeeded, and the Scourge Val'kyr were born. The Val'kyr greatly enhanced his ability to control the dark powers of undeath.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Some of his Val'kyr were also corrupted original Val'kyr, twisted and bent to his will.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Exploring&#95;Azeroth&#95;10-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Exploring_Azeroth-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The vrykul bard, <a href="/wiki/Helgus" title="Helgus">Helgus</a>, who lived in the town of <a href="/wiki/Nifflevar" title="Nifflevar">Nifflevar</a>, wrote three sagas, one of which was the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_scroll_02.png/16px-Inv_scroll_02.png?4e02ae" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Saga_of_the_Val%27kyr" title="Saga of the Val&#39;kyr"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Saga of the Val&#39;kyr is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Saga of the Val'kyr<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. This is ancient knowledge,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and it is unknown if other mentions of the Val'kyr in the <a href="/wiki/Howling_Fjord" title="Howling Fjord">Howling Fjord</a> are references to the original Val'kyr, or are direct references to the Lich King's Val'kyr forces.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Wrath_of_the_Lich_King">Wrath of the Lich King</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Wrath of the Lich King"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King" title="Wrath of the Lich King"><img alt="Wrath of the Lich King" src="/images/Wrath-Logo-Small.png?6705fd" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="36" height="19" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="36" data-file-height="19" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King" title="World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King">Wrath of the Lich King</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Geirrvif.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Geirrvif.jpg/320px-Geirrvif.jpg?95e7d7" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="295" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="982" data-file-height="904" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Geirrvif.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>A Val'kyr, original model in <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King" title="World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King">Wrath of the Lich King</a></i>.</div></div></div>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Scourge" title="Scourge">Scourge</a> Val'kyr were described as warrior maidens of old, and each was the greatest warrior of her generation. They are sisters who served the warriors of the north in life, and chose to continue [their] service in death.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-EoN&#95;14-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EoN-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> An unknown amount of them were created from the female <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a> of <a href="/wiki/Valkyrion" title="Valkyrion">Valkyrion</a>, who fought and trained to receive the Lich King's unusual gift.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At least <a href="/wiki/Svala_Sorrowgrave" title="Svala Sorrowgrave">one example</a> exists of a female vrykul being turned into a Val'kyr at another location as well. Those that were deemed worthy underwent the transformation from vrykul to Val'kyr. It is said that the Val'kyr serve to judge the fate of vrykul combatants, transforming the favored contestants to <a href="/wiki/Ymirjar" title="Ymirjar">Ymirjar</a>, and the failed contestants to the ranks of the undead <a href="/wiki/Vargul" title="Vargul">Vargul</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Echo&#95;of&#95;Ymiron&#95;3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Echo_of_Ymiron-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Val'kyr are considered "unborn" until they vanquish a creature of high nobility.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr_Battle-maiden" title="Val&#39;kyr Battle-maiden">Val'kyr Battle-maidens</a> watched the fates of the training <a href="/wiki/Death_knight" title="Death knight">death knights</a> of <a href="/wiki/Acherus" class="mw-redirect" title="Acherus">Acherus</a>, making sure the newly born warriors didn't fall in battle before their time. These Val'kyr were acting much like <a href="/wiki/Spirit_Healer" title="Spirit Healer">spirit healers</a>. If they fell in battle, the Val'kyr would return them to their unlife. During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_for_the_Ebon_Hold" title="Battle for the Ebon Hold">Battle for the Ebon Hold</a> shortly after the death knights were freed, they were aiding the remains of the <a href="/wiki/Scourge" title="Scourge">Scourge</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Upon <a href="/wiki/Ingvar_the_Plunderer" title="Ingvar the Plunderer">Ingvar the Plunderer</a>'s death during his encounter in <a href="/wiki/Utgarde_Keep" title="Utgarde Keep">Utgarde Keep</a>, the Val'kyr <a href="/wiki/Annhylde_the_Caller" title="Annhylde the Caller">Annhylde the Caller</a> judged Ingvar as unworthy and resurrected him as <a href="/wiki/Vargul" title="Vargul">Vargul</a> as a second chance.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Geirrvif" title="Geirrvif">Geirrvif</a> was appointed to oversee the trials of <a href="/wiki/Valhalas" title="Valhalas">Valhalas</a>; judging those worthy to ascend to the ranks of the <a href="/wiki/Ymirjar" title="Ymirjar">Ymirjar</a>, while those unworthy became <a href="/wiki/Vargul" title="Vargul">Varguls</a>. She is notably extremely friendly to the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a>, expressing genuine joy at seeing them return to fight in the arena and holding no ill will or threats towards them.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Fjola_Lightbane" title="Fjola Lightbane">Fjola Lightbane</a> and <a href="/wiki/Eydis_Darkbane" title="Eydis Darkbane">Eydis Darkbane</a> were two very powerful Val'kyr that were originally sent by the Lich King along with their <a href="/wiki/Vengeful_Val%27kyr" title="Vengeful Val&#39;kyr">Vengeful Val'kyr</a> army to disrupt, destroy, and terrorize the <a href="/wiki/Argent_Tournament" title="Argent Tournament">Argent Tournament</a> and its citizens. However, <a href="/wiki/Argent_Confessor_Paletress" title="Argent Confessor Paletress">Argent Confessor Paletress</a> came to battle the twins, and upon her victory, Fjola and Eydis were captured by <a href="/wiki/Paladin" title="Paladin">paladins</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Argent_Crusade" title="Argent Crusade">Argent Crusade</a>. Their bonds were maintained by <a href="/wiki/Priestess_Alorah" title="Priestess Alorah">Priestess Alorah</a> and <a href="/wiki/Priest_Grimmin" title="Priest Grimmin">Priest Grimmin</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They later appeared as the final challenge of the coliseum after the raid defeated the Faction Champions.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Edge_of_Night">Edge of Night</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Edge of Night"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels"><img alt="WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png" src="/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="62" height="16" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="62" data-file-height="16" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to the <i>Warcraft</i> <a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels">novels</a>, <a href="/wiki/Novellas" title="Novellas">novellas</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Short_stories" title="Short stories">short stories</a>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>After the events took place within the <a href="/wiki/Icecrown_Citadel" title="Icecrown Citadel">Icecrown Citadel</a>, and the death of Arthas, nine of the Scourge's Val'kyr remained at the Frozen Throne. These nine, led by <a href="/wiki/Annhylde_the_Caller" title="Annhylde the Caller">Annhylde the Caller</a>, were bound to the will of their new dormant Lich King. Imprisoned atop the <a href="/wiki/Icecrown" title="Icecrown">Icecrown</a>, possibly for eternity, they hungered for their freedom. To gain this freedom, they needed a vessel, one like them, a sister of war, strong, and who understood life and death. Someone who had seen the light and the dark. Someone worthy of power over life and death. <a href="/wiki/Sylvanas_Windrunner" title="Sylvanas Windrunner">Sylvanas Windrunner</a>, after having just committed suicide off the Frozen Throne, was shown what would happen to her people and herself if she was gone. Using this information, they convinced her to make a pact with them. In this pact, the Val'kyr would be free of the Lich King forever, but their souls would be bound to the Dark Lady. Sylvanas will walk with the living once again but through the sisterhood of the Val'kyr. As long as they live, so too would Sylvanas. All of them would have their freedom, life, and power over death. Sylvanas agreed to this pact if only to prolong her fate, which filled her with terror. Annhylde took Sylvanas's place in death as the pact was made, and the remaining eight sisters were bound to their new leader.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-EoN&#95;14-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EoN-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Part_of_the_Forsaken">Part of the Forsaken</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Part of the Forsaken"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Sylvanas_and_friends_at_the_Sepulcher.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Sylvanas_and_friends_at_the_Sepulcher.jpg/320px-Sylvanas_and_friends_at_the_Sepulcher.jpg?233f76" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="265" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1298" data-file-height="1074" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Sylvanas_and_friends_at_the_Sepulcher.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Sylvanas" class="mw-redirect" title="Sylvanas">Sylvanas</a> and her Val'kyr at the <a href="/wiki/Sepulcher" title="Sepulcher">Sepulcher</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>The Forsaken had one major flaw working against them; they could not reproduce. The Lich King was the one who created the Scourge, and now that the Lich King was gone, there were no Scourge left to turn to Forsaken. The Val'kyr were her salvation and solution to her dilemma. Since the Val'kyr could raise the undead, they were essentially the key to keeping the Forsaken "alive" as a viable race. After leaving Northrend, a few of the Val'kyr were sent to the <a href="/wiki/Deathknell_Graves" title="Deathknell Graves">Deathknell Graves</a> to "recruit" any remaining corpses there into the Forsaken, while the Dark Lady made her way to Silverpine to prevent her people from being slaughtered by <a href="/wiki/Garrosh_Hellscream" title="Garrosh Hellscream">Garrosh's</a> command. With her were the rest of the Val'kyr making their presence known.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-EoN&#95;14-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EoN-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After Garrosh backed down and allowed Sylvanas to take command of the <a href="/wiki/Invasion_of_Gilneas" title="Invasion of Gilneas">campaign for Gilneas</a>, Sylvanas had decided that her people would no longer be carelessly used or squandered away for some <a href="/wiki/Orc" title="Orc">orc</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-EoN&#95;14-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EoN-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> They would be used wisely and with a purpose, as a bulwark to safeguard her and their kingdom. A bulwark that grows stronger as the Val'kyr raises the living's dead to bolster their numbers, even with former <a href="/wiki/Archmage_Ataeric" title="Archmage Ataeric">enemies</a> joining their ranks. This is due to the fact that those raised by the Val'kyr who die in combat or under extreme stress, enter into a violent, frenzied state. Undead in this state are easily manipulated, and their rage is often directed at the foes of those who raised them. After the effects wear off, the remaining resurrected are given the option of joining the Forsaken or being returned to the grave.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Val'kyr themselves have been deployed into several battles since their conscription, notably throughout <a href="/wiki/Silverpine" class="mw-redirect" title="Silverpine">Silverpine</a> and <a href="/wiki/Andorhal" title="Andorhal">Andorhal</a>, both of which were sound Forsaken victories due to the Val'kyr's input. However, the Val'kyr were only able to reanimate <a href="/wiki/Human" title="Human">human</a> corpses as Forsaken.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The humans trapped in the conflict with the Forsaken sought to escape that fate by turning into <a href="/wiki/Worgen" title="Worgen">worgen</a>, and thus the surviving leaders and refugees of <a href="/wiki/Hillsbrad_Fields" title="Hillsbrad Fields">Hillsbrad Fields</a> and <a href="/wiki/Southshore" title="Southshore">Southshore</a> were willing to take the <a href="/wiki/Worgen_curse" title="Worgen curse">worgen curse</a> and became <a href="/wiki/Hillsbrad_Worgen" title="Hillsbrad Worgen">Hillsbrad Worgen</a> to prevent the Forsaken from raising them into undeath.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When Sylvanas was killed in Silverpine Forest, Agatha, Arthura, and Daschla sacrificed themselves to revive her once more.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Cities&#95;in&#95;Dust&#95;23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cities_in_Dust-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Then later in the battle at Andorhal, the Alliance would kill Aradne, thus leaving only four dark Val'kyr in existence. It's unknown where they currently are, but they're likely in a safe area continuing to raise more Forsaken members.
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Siege_of_Orgrimmar" title="Siege of Orgrimmar">Siege of Orgrimmar</a>, Sylvanas intended to use her Val'kyr to raise slain Alliance soldiers in the streets of Orgrimmar.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legion">Legion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Legion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>

They are tasked with bringing the souls of deceased Vrykul heroes from <a href="/wiki/Stormheim" title="Stormheim">Stormheim</a> and Northrend to these halls for eternal feasts.

However, <a href="/wiki/Genn_Greymane" title="Genn Greymane">Genn Greymane</a> followed Sylvanas into the vault and foiled her plan by smashing the lantern and freeing Eyir.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels"><img alt="WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png" src="/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="62" height="16" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="62" data-file-height="16" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to the <i>Warcraft</i> <a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels">novels</a>, <a href="/wiki/Novellas" title="Novellas">novellas</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Short_stories" title="Short stories">short stories</a>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>

There, they found that the Val'kyr <a href="/wiki/Geirrvif" title="Geirrvif">Geirrvif</a> continued her duties at <a href="/wiki/Valhalas" title="Valhalas">Valhalas</a>; though her people no longer have a <a href="/wiki/Lich_King" title="Lich King">death god</a> to serve.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Exploring&#95;Azeroth&#95;10-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Exploring_Azeroth-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Notably, this makes her the last of the known named <a href="/wiki/Scourge" title="Scourge">Scourge</a> Val'kyr; and the only one of them to have not joined with the Nine or the <a href="/wiki/Knights_of_the_Ebon_Blade" title="Knights of the Ebon Blade">Knights of the Ebon Blade</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="The_nine">The nine</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr?action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: The nine"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>After the defeat of the Lich King, only nine of his Val'kyr remained present in his citadel. These nine bound themselves to Sylvanas and in order to seal the pact, the strongest Val'kyr <a href="/wiki/Annhylde" class="mw-redirect" title="Annhylde">Annhylde</a> sacrificed herself to save the banshee's life. Later, in Silverpine, <a href="/wiki/Agatha" title="Agatha">Agatha</a>, <a href="/wiki/Daschla" title="Daschla">Daschla</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Arthura" title="Arthura">Arthura</a> sacrificed themselves to revive the Dark Lady once more after she was shot dead by <a href="/wiki/Lord_Godfrey" class="mw-redirect" title="Lord Godfrey">Lord Godfrey</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Cities&#95;in&#95;Dust&#95;23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cities_in_Dust-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In Andorhal, <a href="/wiki/Aradne" title="Aradne">Aradne</a> was killed by Alliance forces.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Multiple <a href="/wiki/Lesser_Val%27kyr" title="Lesser Val&#39;kyr">lesser Val'kyr</a> maidens were spotted in the skies during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_for_Andorhal" title="Battle for Andorhal">Battle for Andorhal</a>, and though they can raise corpses<sup id="cite&#95;ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> these lesser Val'kyr are not part of the original pact and are thus not strong enough to revive Sylvanas. How exactly they are created remains unknown, and where they dwell now is a mystery.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is known that they are bound to greater Val'kyr, as when Aradne was slain, these Val'kyr dissipated.
</p><p>The number of Val'kyr who appeared during the <a href="/wiki/Battle_for_the_Broken_Shore" class="mw-redirect" title="Battle for the Broken Shore">Battle for the Broken Shore</a> cinematic was five, though five Val'kyr had died, and thus there should be only four left. These could have been lesser Val'kyr.
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/Arathi_Highlands" title="Arathi Highlands">Arathi Highlands</a>, <a href="/wiki/Nathanos_Blightcaller" title="Nathanos Blightcaller">Nathanos Blightcaller</a> ordered Val'kyr to raise several powerful slain enemies.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In <a href="/wiki/Darkshore" title="Darkshore">Darkshore</a>, <a href="/wiki/Brynja" title="Brynja">Brynja</a> was killed by <a href="/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind" title="Tyrande Whisperwind">Tyrande Whisperwind</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Val'kyr left alive were: <a href="/wiki/Signe" title="Signe">Signe</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kyra" title="Kyra">Kyra</a>, and one other.
</p><p>The Val'kyr are able to raise corpses into undeath, which is how more Forsaken are created. The power of the Lich King previously allowed them to raise more powerful undead in the form of <a href="/wiki/Death_knight" title="Death knight">death knights</a>. They can still extremely rarely use their own powers to reshape the body of an active undead and make it stronger, though it is an arduous endeavor that they are reluctant to undertake. For this reason, Sylvanas cannot have all the Forsaken undergo the ritual, and the only known person to have done it is <a href="/wiki/Nathanos_Blightcaller" title="Nathanos Blightcaller">Nathanos Blightcaller</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the <a href="/wiki/Sanctum_of_Domination" title="Sanctum of Domination">Sanctum of Domination</a>, the final Val'kyr is revealed to be <a href="/wiki/Skyja" title="Skyja">Skyja</a>. The remaining three Val'kyr and Sylvanas raise the six who died back, so that they can block the heroes' progress and protect Sylvanas. This resurrection was seemingly incomplete, or somehow rested on Skyja, as the six are only able to take a supporting role in the ensuing battle with the Maw Walkers, and once Skyja is struck down, the fallen six vanish. When the heroes finally reached the pinnacle of the Sanctum of Domination, <a href="/wiki/Bolvar_Fordragon" title="Bolvar Fordragon">Bolvar Fordragon</a> announced to Sylvanas that all of her Val'kyr were eliminated, and that she would die here for the last time. Sylvanas was undeterred, coldly comparing the losses of her Val'kyr to that of an archer losing their arrows.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="As_a_companion_pet">As a companion pet</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr?action=edit&amp;section=10" title="Edit section: As a companion pet"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The <a href="/wiki/Unborn_Val%27kyr" title="Unborn Val&#39;kyr">Unborn Val'kyr</a> is an extremely rare wild battle pet that has a chance to appear in three specific locations in every <a href="/wiki/Northrend" title="Northrend">Northrend</a> zone.</li>
<li>The <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_valkiergoldpet.png/16px-Inv_valkiergoldpet.png?581a47" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Sunborne_Val%27kyr" title="Sunborne Val&#39;kyr"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-rare" title="Sunborne Val&#39;kyr is a rare quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Sunborne Val'kyr<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> can be purchased from <span class="raceicon"></span><a href="/wiki/Valdemar_Stormseeker" title="Valdemar Stormseeker"><span style="color:">Valdemar Stormseeker</span></a>&#160;<small>&lt;Valarjar Emissary&gt;</small> at a cost of <span class="nobreak ajaxoutertt">500<span><a href="/wiki/Money#Types_of_coins" title="g"><img alt="g" src="/images/thumb/Gold.png/14px-Gold.png?c28389" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="14" height="14" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20" /></a></span></span> upon reaching <a href="/wiki/Revered" class="mw-redirect" title="Revered"><span class="rep-revered" title="Revered">revered</span></a> reputation with the <a href="/wiki/Valarjar" title="Valarjar">Valarjar</a>.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes_and_trivia">Notes and trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr?action=edit&amp;section=11" title="Edit section: Notes and trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li><i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Chronicle_Volume_3" title="World of Warcraft: Chronicle Volume 3">World of Warcraft: Chronicle Volume 3</a></i> revealed that the Lich King created his own Val'kyr, something shown with <a href="/wiki/Svala_Sorrowgrave" title="Svala Sorrowgrave">Svala Sorrowgrave</a>. Despite this, <a href="/wiki/Alex_Afrasiabi" title="Alex Afrasiabi">Alex Afrasiabi</a> claimed that they were subjugated servants of Odyn, bound to him without a choice, rather than newly created ones.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-EoN&#95;14-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-EoN-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This was reconciled with <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Exploring_Azeroth:_Northrend" class="mw-redirect" title="World of Warcraft: Exploring Azeroth: Northrend">Exploring Azeroth: Northrend</a></i>, which clarified the Lich King both corrupted and created Val'kyr.</li>
<li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_staff_109.png/16px-Inv_staff_109.png?f54c23" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Nibelung" title="Nibelung"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-epic" title="Nibelung is an epic quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Nibelung<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> causes players' harmful spells to have a chance to summon a <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr_Guardian" title="Val&#39;kyr Guardian">Val'kyr Guardian</a> to assist them in combat. Unholy <a href="/wiki/Death_knight" title="Death knight">death knights</a> used to be able to summon a Val'kyr to their side instead of a <a href="/wiki/Gargoyle" title="Gargoyle">gargoyle</a> with the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Achievement_boss_svalasorrowgrave.png/16px-Achievement_boss_svalasorrowgrave.png?91bbf7" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Dark_Arbiter" title="Dark Arbiter"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Dark Arbiter<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> talent.</li>
<li>Early in the <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Wrath_of_the_Lich_King" title="World of Warcraft: Wrath of the Lich King">Wrath of the Lich King</a></i> beta, before the proper Val'kyr model was created, they looked like <a href="/wiki/Banshee" title="Banshee">banshees</a> instead.</li>
<li>In <i><a href="/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reign_of_Chaos" title="Warcraft III: Reign of Chaos">Warcraft III</a></i>, a sword-wielding, <a href="/wiki/Guardian_of_Ancient_Kings" title="Guardian of Ancient Kings">angelic figure</a> briefly appears when a <a href="/wiki/Paladin_(Warcraft_III)" title="Paladin (Warcraft III)">Paladin</a> uses the <a href="/wiki/Paladin_(Warcraft_III)#Resurrection_(Ultimate)" title="Paladin (Warcraft III)">Resurrection</a> spell. In <i><a href="/wiki/Warcraft_III:_Reforged" title="Warcraft III: Reforged">Warcraft III: Reforged</a></i>, it's been replaced with a golden Val'kyr.</li>
<li>"Val'kyr" are an obvious reference to the <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valkyrie" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Valkyrie">Valkyries</a> of Norse myth — minor female deities in the service of Odin. Their duty was to choose the most heroic spirits of slain warriors and take them to Valhalla, where they would serve at Odin's side in the Ragnarök.</li>
<li><i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Chronicle_Volume_1" title="World of Warcraft: Chronicle Volume 1">World of Warcraft: Chronicle Volume 1</a></i> mentions that not all Val'kyr chose to join <a href="/wiki/Helya" title="Helya">Helya</a> in <a href="/wiki/Helheim" title="Helheim">Helheim</a> after the sealing of the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a> and instead disappeared into the <a href="/wiki/Shadowlands" title="Shadowlands">Shadowlands</a> to guide the dead back to the realm of the living,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Chronicle&#95;57&#95;7-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Chronicle_57-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> implying that these rogue Val'kyr became <a href="/wiki/Spirit_Healer" title="Spirit Healer">spirit healers</a>. However, <i>Shadowlands</i> revealed that spirit healers are actually a form of <a href="/wiki/Kyrian" title="Kyrian">kyrian</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-SA&#95;Gamer&#95;6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SA_Gamer-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_7_0raid_necklace_03c.png/16px-Inv_7_0raid_necklace_03c.png?d6ea70" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Bondable_Val%27kyr_Diadem" title="Bondable Val&#39;kyr Diadem"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-rare" title="Bondable Val&#39;kyr Diadem is a rare quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Bondable Val'kyr Diadem<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> toy will summon Angelis a small Val'kyr above the player's shoulder.</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Svala_Sorrowgrave" title="Svala Sorrowgrave">Svala Sorrowgrave</a> is the only Val'kyr ascension witnessed, being directly imbued with the Lich King's powers through Frostmourne.</li></ul>
