'''Talanji's Expedition''' is an expedition of [[Zandalari troll]]s from [[Zuldazar]], backed by the [[Darkspear tribe]]. It is personally led by [[Talanji]], Princess of the [[Zandalari Empire]]. The expedition will face off against the [[blood troll]] forces.

==Faction description==
''Princess Talanji and her allies fight to protect Zandalar from the onslaught of the Blood Trolls.''

==History==
[[File:Talanji and her allies in Nazmir.jpg|thumb|The expedition and their allies before the assault on [[Zul'Nazman]]]]
Even though her [[King Rastakhan|father]] dismissed the threat of the [[blood troll]]s, Talanji sought to take the fight to them. Aided by her fellow Zandalari and backed by [[Rokhan]], a [[shadow hunter]] of the [[Darkspear tribe]], as well as a contingent of his scouts and warriors, Talanji's newly created expedition ventured into the swamps of [[Nazmir]]. At Rokhan's direction, [[Zul'jan Ruins]] was converted into the expedition's main outpost.<ref>[[Into The Darkness]]</ref> After discovering that the blood trolls sought to unleash [[Taloc]] on Zuldazar, Talanji and Rokhan sought to enlist the aid of the loa to aid them in the battle against the blood trolls, unaware that [[Torga]] had been killed, [[Krag'wa]] was under assault by the blood trolls, and [[Hir'eek]] corrupted into serving [[G'huun]].<ref>[[Isolating Zalamar]]</ref>

As they sought the aid of [[Bwonsamdi]], [[Horde]] [[adventurer]]s would encounter his worshiper, [[Hanzabu]], who brought them to the [[Necropolis (Nazmir)|Necropolis]] to meet with him. After tasking the adventurer with cleansing his temple of the [[Ghost|spirits]] who had abandoned Bwonsamdi for G'huun, Bwonsamdi agreed to pledge his powers to the fight against the blood trolls in exchange for one million [[soul]]s. The ritual to finalize their contract was interrupted by the blood trolls and their matriarch who were successfully fought off. For being attacked in his own temple, Bwonsamdi declared that the blood trolls would be mighty sorry.<ref>[[Soulbound (quest)]]</ref> Thus the expedition gained the loa of death as their ally. The expedition also recruited numerous [[tortollan]]s to their cause after saving Torga's corpse from being corrupted by G'huun's [[herald]], [[Jungo]].<ref>[[Jungo, Herald of G'huun (quest)]]</ref>

While Hir'eek was killed,<ref>[[Hir'eek, the Bat Loa]]</ref> Krag'wa would be liberated from the blood troll assault, and the loa unleash his fury on them.<ref>[[Krag'wa the Terrible]]</ref> With their new allies and Horde [[Gob Squad|reinforcements]], Talanji's expedition launched an assault on the [[Zul'Nazman|blood troll capital]] that saw to the death of [[Grand Ma'da Ateena]], the matriarch of the blood trolls, and Taloc being blasted into [[Uldir]] itself.<ref>[[Ateena's Fall]]</ref>
