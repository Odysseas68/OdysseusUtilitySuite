The '''Voldunai''' is a [[faction]] of [[vulpera]] and [[Zandalari troll|Zandalari]] exiles found in [[Vol'dun]]. "Voldunai" seems to be a general name for most non-hostile, non-[[sethrak]] inhabitants of Vol'dun, encompassing everything from vulpera [[caravan]]s and the worshippers of [[Akunda]], to individual groups of Zandalari like those at the [[Goldtusk Inn]] and the [[Scorched Sands Outpost]].

[[Reins of the Alabaster Hyena|Alabaster hyenas]] are used by the Voldunai to quickly move unseen throughout the sands.<ref>[[Reins of the Alabaster Hyena]]</ref>
