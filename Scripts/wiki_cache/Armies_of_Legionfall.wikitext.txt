The '''Armies of Legionfall'''<ref>[[Armies of Legionfall (quest)]]</ref> are a coalition of all [[Class Order|orders]] united to defeat the [[Burning Legion]] at the [[Tomb of Sargeras]]. Their base of operations, [[Deliverance Point]], is located south-west of the island of [[Thal'dranath]].

In addition to the orders, the Armies of Legionfall are also allied with the [[Court of Farondis]], the [[Azurewing]], the [[Nightfallen (faction)|Nightfallen]], the [[Highmountain Tribe]],<ref>[[Get Them Up]]</ref> and the [[Wardens]], as evidenced by their assistance to the [[Adventurer|Legionfall Commander]].

Following the end of the [[third invasion of the Burning Legion]] and the [[Argus Campaign]], it is unknown if the Armies of Legionfall remains active, however, it is most probably that they dissolved after reaching their goals.

==Notes and trivia==
[[File:Legionfall champions.jpg|thumb|[[Khadgar]] and [[Velen]] with the champions of the orders]]
*The [[Class Order|order]]s armies are accompanied by [[Khadgar|Archmage Khadgar]] and the [[mage]]s of the [[Kirin Tor]], [[Maiev Shadowsong]] and her [[The Wardens|Wardens]], [[Prophet Velen]], and [[Illidan Stormrage]].
*The Armies of Legionfall's banner features, from left to right and top to bottom, the crests of the [[Valarjar]], [[Ebon Blade]], [[Earthen Ring]], [[Order of the Broken Temple]], [[Unseen Path]], [[Tirisgarde]], [[Illidari]], [[Silver Hand]], [[Black Harvest]], [[Cenarion Circle]], [[Conclave]], and the [[The Uncrowned|Uncrowned]].
*During the initial meeting of the Armies of Legionfall, several important delegates from the class orders met at [[Krasus' Landing]] with [[Archmage Khadgar]] and [[Prophet Velen]] to discuss [[Gul'dan (alternate universe)|Gul'dan's]] [[Nighthold|failure]], the [[Pillars of Creation]], and [[Kil'jaeden]]. These included:
**[[Alonsus Faol]] representing the [[Conclave]].
**[[Emmarel Shadewarden]] representing the [[Unseen Path]].
**[[Ritssyn Flamescowl]] representing the [[Council of the Black Harvest]].
**[[Havi]] representing the [[Valarjar]].
**[[Iron-Body Ponshu]] representing the [[Order of the Broken Temple]].
**[[Kor'vas Bloodthorn]] representing the [[Illidari]].
**[[Lady Liadrin]] representing the [[Knights of the Silver Hand]] (although it appears that [[Gryan Stoutmantle]] is present in the cinematic).
**[[Farseer Nobundo]] representing the [[Earthen Ring]].
**[[Meryl Felstorm]] representing the [[Tirisgarde]].
**[[Darion Mograine]] representing the [[Knights of the Ebon Blade]].
**[[Rensar Greathoof]] representing the [[Cenarion Circle]].
**[[Lord Jorach Ravenholdt]] representing the [[Uncrowned]].
