<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Reputation_sources">Reputation sources</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Dream_Wardens?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Reputation sources"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>There are numerous methods of gaining reputation with the Dream Wardens:
</p>
<ul><li><a href="/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Dream_campaign" title="Guardians of the Dream campaign">Guardians of the Dream campaign</a> chapters (2500 per chapter)</li>
<li>World quests (50 each)</li>
<li><span class="questlink"><span class="questlong-prefix linkicon"><img alt="N" src="/images/Neutral_15.png?a79dba" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="15" height="15" data-file-width="15" data-file-height="15" />&#160;[10-80W]&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/A_Worthy_Ally:_Dream_Wardens" title="A Worthy Ally: Dream Wardens"><span class="ajaxttlink questlink">A Worthy Ally: Dream Wardens</span></a></span> (500)</li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Superbloom" title="Superbloom">Superbloom</a> (1000 for first of the week)</li>
<li>One-time treasures (200 each)</li>
<li>Six rare-elites: <a href="/wiki/Envoy_of_Winter" title="Envoy of Winter">Envoy of Winter</a>, <a href="/wiki/Firebrand_Fystia" title="Firebrand Fystia">Firebrand Fystia</a>, <a href="/wiki/Greedy_Gessie" title="Greedy Gessie">Greedy Gessie</a>, <a href="/wiki/Reefbreaker_Moruud" title="Reefbreaker Moruud">Reefbreaker Moruud</a>, <a href="/wiki/Nuoberon" title="Nuoberon">Nuoberon</a>, <a href="/wiki/Raszageth%27s_Last_Breath" title="Raszageth&#39;s Last Breath">Raszageth's Last Breath</a> (50 each)</li>
