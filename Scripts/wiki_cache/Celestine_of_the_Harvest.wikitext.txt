'''Celestine of the Harvest''' is a [[worgen]] [[druid]] of [[Gilneas (kingdom)|Gilneas]].

==History==
[[File:Celestine.jpg|thumb|upright|Celestine's human form.]]
===Cataclysm===
{{Cata-section}}
At the time of the [[Cataclysm (event)|Cataclysm]], [[worgen]] [[druid]] [[adventurer]]s first met her in [[Gilneas City (starting zone)|Gilneas City]], where she instructed them on how to use the spell [[Rejuvenation]]. At some point, she was afflicted with the [[worgen curse]] and later moved to [[Stormwind City]], residing in a house next to [[Olivia's Pond]]. Druid adventurers in Stormwind can visit her to train their skills.

During the [[Firelands Invasion]], Celestine helped patrol the [[Regrowth (subzone)|Regrowth]] in [[Mount Hyjal]].

During [[Hallow's End]], Celestine is standing outside the gates of Stormwind, where she explains the significance of burning the [[Wickerman]].

===Legion===
{{Legion-section}}
During the [[third invasion of the Burning Legion]], Celestine worked with [[Hamuul Runetotem]] and [[Zen'tabra]] to battle [[Destromath]] at the nearly-destroyed [[Well of Eternity (Hyjal)|Well of Eternity]] on Hyjal.<ref>[[Cleansing the Mother Tree]]</ref>

When [[satyr]] and the [[Burning Legion]] attacked [[Val'sharah]]'s [[Temple of Elune (Val'sharah)|Temple of Elune]], Celestine went there and aided in the defense. She was then recalled by the [[Dreamgrove]] to assist with a ritual to commune with [[Malorne]] in [[Nordrassil]] on Hyjal.<ref>[[Gathering the Dreamweavers: Celestine of the Harvest]]</ref><ref>[[Communing With Malorne]]</ref> Afterward, she was among the druids that entered [[Malorne's Nightmare]].

===Battle for Azeroth===
{{bfa-section}}
During the [[Fourth War]], Celestine participated in the [[Battle for Darkshore]]. In [[Bashal'Aran]], she is seen channeling a spell to [[Shadebough (ancient)|Shadebough]]. In Stormwind City, she moved out of her house and is standing next to a tree.

===Shadowlands===
{{Sl-section}}
Celestine was present in [[Bashal'Aran]] and witnessed Tyrande's speech about the Horde's defeat in [[Darkshore]].<ref>[[A New Hope]]</ref>

===Dragonflight===
{{Df-section}}
[[File:Celestine and Vassandra - Reclamation of Gilneas.jpg|thumb|Celestine fighting the [[Scarlet Crusade]] alongside [[Vassandra Stormclaw]].]]
Following the reappearance of the [[Dragon Isles]], she traveled to the [[Shady Sanctuary]] in the [[Ohn'ahran Plains]] and was seen speaking with [[Lyessa Bloomwatcher]].

She was later amongst those who came to [[Alexstrasza]]'s aid when she was attacked by [[Fyrakk|Fyrakk the Blazing]]. In the battle that followed, she fought side by side with [[Moira Thaurissan]] and [[Genn Greymane]] at the [[Field of Flames]].<ref>[[The Age of Mortals]]</ref> Celestine later attended the gathering in [[Amirdrassil]] when it bloomed on the [[Dragon Isles]] after Fyrakk's death.

During the [[Reclamation of Gilneas]], Celestine went with [[Genn Greymane|King Genn Greymane]] to [[Aderic's Repose]] and was sent to tell the [[Forsaken]] outside the city to wait for their signal.<ref>[[Knee-High]]</ref> She was later seen fighting the [[Scarlet Crusade]] soldiers at [[Cathedral Square]] alongside [[Vassandra Stormclaw]]. Afterward, she can be found at [[Tal'doren]] in the [[Blackwald]].

Celestine can also be seen in [[Belanaar]] speaking with Vassandra.

==Trivia==
*With the release of [[patch 7.3.5]] and the [[Stormwind Embassy]], she was moved underground, rendering her unavailable but still targetable via /tar. Guards still refer to her at her old location.
**She was later moved back overground, although guards still refer to her cottage despite her now being outdoors.
*Prior to patch 4.0.6, her worgen form was bugged and had the wrong female worgen texture applied to it.
*In Stormwind, she was in human form by day and in worgen form by night, like the worgen in the pre-''Cataclysm'' [[Pyrewood Village]].
*Celestine is voiced by [[Lauren Tom]] in ''[[World of Warcraft: Legion|Legion]]''.
