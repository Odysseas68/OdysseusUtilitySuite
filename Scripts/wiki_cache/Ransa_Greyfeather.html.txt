<p><b>Ransa Greyfeather</b> is the emissary and <a href="/wiki/Quartermaster" title="Quartermaster">quartermaster</a> for the <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Tribe" title="Highmountain Tribe">Highmountain Tribe</a>, found on the upper level of <a href="/wiki/Thunder_Totem" title="Thunder Totem">Thunder Totem</a> in <a href="/wiki/Highmountain" title="Highmountain">Highmountain</a>. She can later be found at the <a href="/wiki/Port_of_Zandalar" title="Port of Zandalar">Port of Zandalar</a> in <a href="/wiki/Dazar%27alor" title="Dazar&#39;alor">Dazar'alor</a> as the emissary for the <a href="/wiki/Honorbound" title="Honorbound">Honorbound</a>.
</p>
