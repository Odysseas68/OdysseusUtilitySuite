<p><b>The Nightfallen</b>, also called <b>Dusk Lily rebels</b>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> or simply the <b>Dusk Lily</b>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are a dissident <a href="/wiki/Nightfallen" title="Nightfallen">nightfallen</a> and <a href="/wiki/Nightborne" title="Nightborne">nightborne</a> faction that splintered from the rest of nightborne society in <a href="/wiki/Suramar_City" title="Suramar City">Suramar City</a> and led a <a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_rebellion" title="Nightfallen rebellion">rebellion</a> to overthrow <a href="/wiki/Grand_Magistrix_Elisande" class="mw-redirect" title="Grand Magistrix Elisande">Grand Magistrix Elisande</a> and her <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demonic</a> <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">allies</a>. Led by <a href="/wiki/First_Arcanist_Thalyssra" class="mw-redirect" title="First Arcanist Thalyssra">First Arcanist Thalyssra</a>, the Nightfallen waged a successful war of resistance against Elisande's loyalist forces, eventually taking control of Suramar. With their success, they liberated Suramar and its people and expelled the demons from the city. They would also continue to aid the defenders of <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a> in their <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">wider fight</a> against the Burning Legion.
</p><p>For most of the rebellion, the main headquarters of the Nightfallen remained the underground complex of <a href="/wiki/Shal%27Aran" title="Shal&#39;Aran">Shal'Aran</a>, though they maintained bases and safehouses throughout Suramar. Having come to power in the city, they are now based in the <a href="/wiki/Nighthold" title="Nighthold">Nighthold</a> itself, the traditional seat of power for Suramar's rulers. Now sole rulers of their people, Thalyssra and the Nightfallen began to forge a new future for the nightborne race. In this new role, they would be responsible for the subsequent nightborne entry into the <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> prior to the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_(faction)?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Rebellion">Rebellion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_(faction)?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Rebellion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_rebellion" title="Nightfallen rebellion">Nightfallen rebellion</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Shal%27Aran_inhabited.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Shal%27Aran_inhabited.jpg/320px-Shal%27Aran_inhabited.jpg?e94aec" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="200" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1280" data-file-height="800" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Shal%27Aran_inhabited.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The Nightfallen base at <a href="/wiki/Shal%27Aran" title="Shal&#39;Aran">Shal'Aran</a></div></div></div>
<p>The Nightfallen were founded as a direct result of the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion</a> of <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>. When the <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a> first arrived on the world in the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>, they made contact with the <a href="/wiki/Nightborne" title="Nightborne">nightborne</a> in <a href="/wiki/Suramar_City" title="Suramar City">Suramar</a>. Threatened with annihilation, <a href="/wiki/Grand_Magistrix_Elisande" class="mw-redirect" title="Grand Magistrix Elisande">Grand Magistrix Elisande</a> reached an accord with the Legion, though many citizens opposed this decision. One of these dissidents, <a href="/wiki/First_Arcanist_Thalyssra" class="mw-redirect" title="First Arcanist Thalyssra">First Arcanist Thalyssra</a> personally led a failed coup to prevent Elisande from lowering Suramar's defenses to the Legion. Despite her efforts, the attempt was betrayed and crushed, with Thalyssra herself escaping on the brink of death.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Though her city was now occupied by demonic forces, the First Arcanist refused to give up the struggle. She made her way to the subterranean ruins of <a href="/wiki/Shal%27Aran" title="Shal&#39;Aran">Shal'Aran</a> in central <a href="/wiki/Suramar" title="Suramar">Suramar</a> and made contact with outsiders also resisting the Burning Legion based from <a href="/wiki/Dalaran" title="Dalaran">Dalaran</a>. <a href="/wiki/Order_Halls" class="mw-redirect" title="Order Halls">Order Hall</a> <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">champions</a> sent by <a href="/wiki/Archmage_Khadgar" class="mw-redirect" title="Archmage Khadgar">Archmage Khadgar</a> eventually responded to Thalyssra's plea, saving her life and claiming Shal'Aran.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Her position stable, Thalyssra began to take the first steps in forming an effective resistance movement.
</p><p>Fortunately for the fledgling rebellion, many nightborne were still strongly (albeit secretly) opposed to the Legion's arrival. Before long, many key figures had been recruited, such as <a href="/wiki/Arcanist_Valtrois" title="Arcanist Valtrois">Arcanist Valtrois</a>, <a href="/wiki/Chief_Telemancer_Oculeth" title="Chief Telemancer Oculeth">Chief Telemancer Oculeth</a>, <a href="/wiki/Silgryn" title="Silgryn">Silgryn</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Ly%27leth_Lunastre" title="Ly&#39;leth Lunastre">Lady Ly'leth Lunastre</a>. Refugees and exiled dissidents from across Suramar joined the faction, which had become known as the <i>Nightfallen</i> in reference to the <a href="/wiki/Nightfallen" title="Nightfallen">devolved state</a> most of the rebels were in, cut off as they were from the energies of the <a href="/wiki/Nightwell" title="Nightwell">Nightwell</a>. They also began training <a href="/wiki/Withered" title="Withered">withered</a> to help them fight their opponents.
</p><p>With the help of their foreign allies, the Nightfallen quickly gained ground and support against Elisande and her forces. The war they waged was one of stealth, employing hit-and-run tactics and keeping to <a href="/wiki/Masquerade" title="Masquerade">disguises</a> and the shadows. The figure of First Arcanist Thalyssra in particular inspired hope and support among the populace,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while Elisande's worsening cruelty alienated many of her allies.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Dusk_lily" title="Dusk lily">dusk lily</a>, a flower associated personally with Thalyssra, would become the main symbol of both the rebellion and the Nightfallen themselves.
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Nightfallen_Nighthold_1.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Nightfallen_Nighthold_1.jpg/320px-Nightfallen_Nighthold_1.jpg?a73312" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="181" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1360" data-file-height="768" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Nightfallen_Nighthold_1.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <a href="/wiki/Nighthold" title="Nighthold">Nighthold</a> under Nightfallen control</div></div></div>
<p>As the conflict reached its climax, the Nightfallen were able to reverse their withering state through the fruit of the powerful <a href="/wiki/Arcan%27dor" title="Arcan&#39;dor">arcan'dor</a> tree, which freed them from their <a href="/wiki/Magic_addiction" title="Magic addiction">addiction to magic</a>. Coupled with the arrival of three other elven armies to support them,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the Nightfallen were ready to march on Suramar City and topple the Grand Magistrix's regime. After battling through the city and laying siege to the <a href="/wiki/Nighthold" title="Nighthold">Nighthold</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> they and their allies were finally successful; Elisande was slain and the Burning Legion firmly defeated in Suramar.
</p><p>In a closing act of the civil war, and as part of her new role in leading her people, Thalyssra made the decision to let the waning Nightwell die. She reasoned that its death would mark a new beginning awaited the nightborne, with the Nightfallen guiding them in rejoining the world.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="New_beginnings">New beginnings</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_(faction)?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: New beginnings"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="Battle for Azeroth"><img alt="Battle for Azeroth" src="/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>The Nightfallen would continue to support their allies in the ongoing war in the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a> against the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Legion</a>, which eventually proved successful. <a href="/wiki/Suramar_City" title="Suramar City">Suramar</a> itself has suffered terribly in the <a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_rebellion" title="Nightfallen rebellion">civil war</a>, and work was done to rebuild their society and their city. First Arcanist Thalyssra assumed the role of leader, while other prominent Nightfallen also took up positions of power from the <a href="/wiki/Nighthold" title="Nighthold">Nighthold</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Duskwatch" title="Duskwatch">Duskwatch</a>, once the rebellion's sworn enemy, was assimilated into the new regime. The deserter-turned-rebel <a href="/wiki/Victoire" title="Victoire">Victoire</a> was placed in charge of the security force and made First Blade under Thalyssra.
</p><p>Despite the successes they had achieved, Thalyssra was convinced that the nightborne should seek out allies as they took their new place in the world. Though she considered joining the <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a>, in the end she and the other Nightfallen would ally with the <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the lead-up to the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>, the Nightfallen would join the Horde in their preparations for war, while many new nightborne <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a> ventured out into the world for the first time.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Reputation">Reputation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_(faction)?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Reputation"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Many quests in <a href="/wiki/Suramar" title="Suramar">Suramar</a> award reputation, and completing <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Achievements_zone_suramar.png/16px-Achievements_zone_suramar.png?1e106f" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_But_Not_Forgotten" title="Nightfallen But Not Forgotten"><span class="ajaxttlink achievementlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Nightfallen But Not Forgotten<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> opens up their world quests. Progressing further into the faction's storyline unlocks more quests for additional reputation. <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_relics_runestone.png/16px-Inv_relics_runestone.png?6460a8" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Arcane_Remnant_of_Falanaar" title="Arcane Remnant of Falanaar"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-rare" title="Arcane Remnant of Falanaar is a rare quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Arcane Remnant of Falanaar<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> and <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_rune_13.png/16px-Inv_misc_rune_13.png?9f4745" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Arcane_Tablet_of_Falanaar" title="Arcane Tablet of Falanaar"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-rare" title="Arcane Tablet of Falanaar is a rare quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Arcane Tablet of Falanaar<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> both come from chests in <span class="questlink"><span class="questlong-prefix linkicon"><img alt="N" src="/images/Neutral_15.png?a79dba" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="15" height="15" data-file-width="15" data-file-height="15" />&#160;[45WQ]&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Withered_Army_Training_(quest)" title="Withered Army Training (quest)"><span class="ajaxttlink questlink">Withered Army Training</span></a></span>, and after completing the mission.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Nightborne_(playable)" title="Nightborne (playable)">Playable Nightborne</a> begin at exalted reputation by default.
</p>
<dl><dt>Faction description</dt></dl>
<p><i>These exiled <a href="/wiki/Nightborne" title="Nightborne">Nightborne</a> elves suffer withdrawals after being cut off from the <a href="/wiki/Nightwell" title="Nightwell">Nightwell</a>. They oppose their people's alliance with the Legion and fight for some kind of redemption.</i>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Feeding">Feeding</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Nightfallen_(faction)?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Feeding"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Cut off from all sources of <a href="/wiki/Arcwine" title="Arcwine">Arcwine</a>, <a href="/wiki/First_Arcanist_Thalyssra" class="mw-redirect" title="First Arcanist Thalyssra">First Arcanist Thalyssra</a> will have to be provided with <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_ancient_mana.png/16px-Inv_misc_ancient_mana.png?33a40d" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Ancient_Mana" title="Ancient Mana"><span class="currencylink ajaxttlink qc-common"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Ancient Mana<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, along with <a href="/wiki/Chief_Telemancer_Oculeth" title="Chief Telemancer Oculeth">Chief Telemancer Oculeth</a> and <a href="/wiki/Arcanist_Valtrois" title="Arcanist Valtrois">Arcanist Valtrois</a> (after they have been recruited) in order to feed them and allow them to keep functioning.
</p><p>Each one has an Arcane Thirst meter that starts at 100 and slowly depletes to 1. As part of their quests, they will need to be sated once as an example mechanic. From 100 to 1 takes about one day, thus, they each have a daily feed requirement of 50 Ancient Mana.  This will reward a unique one hour buff and 75 reputation.
</p><p>Their state will be listed as <i>Stable</i> until their Arcane Thirst reaches 1, at which point they will be listed as <i>Withering</i>.  When this happens, the quest giver will be starving, start trembling, and will not be able to do anything until fed. Once <span class="questlink"><span class="questlong-prefix linkicon"><img alt="N" src="/images/Neutral_15.png?a79dba" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="15" height="15" data-file-width="15" data-file-height="15" />&#160;[45]&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Friends_On_the_Outside" title="Friends On the Outside"><span class="ajaxttlink questlink">Friends On the Outside</span></a></span> is completed, the buffs can also be purchased at any time; altogether, it is also at a discount at 120 Ancient Mana.
</p><p>Note that for gameplay purposes, the quest givers will not actually become Withered if you do not do their daily feeding over a long period of time since such a change is said to be permanent and irreversible.
</p>
