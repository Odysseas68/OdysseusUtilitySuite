The '''Highmountain Tribe''' is the dominant tribe of the [[Highmountain tauren]] of the [[Broken Isles]], currently led by [[Mayla Highmountain|High Chieftain Mayla Highmountain]].

==History==
===War of the Ancients===
The Highmountain are descendants of the hero [[Huln Highmountain]], also known as "Huln of the Eagle Spear" and the members of the tribes he united. During the [[War of the Ancients]], 10,000 years ago, he united the warring [[Rivermane tribe|Rivermane]], [[Bloodtotem tribe|Bloodtotem]], and [[Skyhorn tribe|Skyhorn]] tribes of his homeland under the banner of the Highmountain.<ref>[[The Story of Huln]]</ref> Huln was chosen by [[Korialstrasz|Krasus]] to lead his people against the [[Burning Legion]] alongside [[Unng Ak]] of the [[furbolg]]s and [[Dungard Ironcutter]] of the [[earthen]]. For his bravery during the war, Huln received a blessing from the [[Wild God]] [[Cenarius]], so that his people would forever have the demigod's favor: the [[Horns of Eche'ro]], which transformed the horns of Huln into moose-like antlers.<ref>[[Huln's War - Stormrage]]</ref> After the war, Huln returned to his homeland and used a [[titan]] artifact known as the [[Hammer of Khaz'goroth]] to banish the traitorous [[Dragon Aspect]] [[Deathwing]] and free his [[drogbar]] slaves as well as [[Ebyssian|a single uncorrupted black dragon egg]]. In his honor, the four tribes named the region "[[Highmountain]]".<ref>[[Titanic Showdown]]</ref>

===Legion===
{{Legion-section}}
[[File:Legion zone concept 2.jpg|thumb||200px|Thunder Totem]]
When the [[Night elf|night elves]] who founded the [[Unseen Path]] left [[Mount Hyjal]] to return to the Broken Isles, the Highmountain [[Highmountain tauren|tauren]] recognized the nobility of their cause and offered them friendship. It was the descendants of [[Huln Highmountain]] who led the Unseen Path to [[Talon Peak]] where they built their base known as [[Trueshot Lodge]]. Since the [[Burning Legion]]'s return, the Highmountain have helped restore the lodge, such as replacing two damaged watch towers with tauren structures.<ref>[[The Unseen Path]]</ref>

Millennia later, Huln's descendants continue to live in [[Thunder Totem]] on the [[Broken Isles]]. With the death of [[Ulan Highmountain|High Chieftain Ulan]], his daughter [[Mayla Highmountain|Mayla]] has become the High Chieftain of the accord between Highmountain, Rivermane, Skyhorn and Bloodtotem. Unfortunately, the theft of the Hammer of Khaz'goroth by [[Dargrul|Dargrul the Underking]] and his drogbar has shattered the unity of the four tribes. Despite the attempts at a reunion of the tribes by an [[adventurer]], the Bloodtotem betrayed the other tribes by joining the Burning Legion. In their place, the drogbar [[Navarrogg]] and his [[Stonedark clan]] joined the Highmountain's cause instead.<ref>[[Unexpected Allies]]</ref>

===Joining the Horde===
After corresponding with Chieftain [[Baine Bloodhoof]] in the aftermath of the [[Argus Campaign]], [[Mayla Highmountain]] accepted an invitation to attend a feast in [[Thunder Bluff]]. Yet, when Baine and the [[Horde]] [[Adventurer|champion]] greeted their Highmountain tauren guests, [[Spiritwalker Ebonhorn]] was tormented by the [[Old Gods' forces]] of [[Uul'gyneth]] attempting to corrupt him,<ref>[[A Feast for Our Kin]]</ref> and he and Mayla were forced to retreat back to [[Thunder Totem]] while Baine and the [[Horde]] champion defended Thunder Bluff from Uul'gyneth's invasion force.<ref>[[Shadow Over Thunder Bluff]]</ref><ref>[[Dark Forces]]</ref> Apparently, the wards Ebonhorn had been maintaining in [[Highmountain]] weakened just enough to let the Darkness seep through.<ref name="QCotN">[[Curse of the Necrodark]]</ref> 

Later on, Baine and the Horde champion traveled to Thunder Totem to offer their aid to mend Ebonhorn,<ref>[[Return to Highmountain]]</ref> who has been fighting unconsciously to keep a darkness from consuming him. Mayla sent the Horde champion to [[Spiritwalker Graysky]],<ref>[[Dark Tales]]</ref> who has the Horde champion undergo [[Spiritwalker]] rituals to walk in the memories of [[Igrul the Scalebane]],<ref>[[Walking in Their Footsteps]]</ref> [[Oenia Skyhorn]],<ref>[[Minions of the Darkness]]</ref> and [[Huln Highmountain]] in order to restore the wards that kept the dark corrupting forces at bay.<ref>[[Huln's Mountain]]</ref> After restoring the wards, Ebonhorn regained consciousness<ref>[[How Fares Ebonhorn?]]</ref> and revealed Uul'gyneth sought to return and conquer Highmountain. Baine, Mayla, the Horde champion, and Ebonhorn left to join [[Lasan Skyhorn]], [[Aviash]], and [[Jale Rivermane]] at [[Highmountain Summit]] to thwart the [[Necrodark]]'s ritual to incarnate Uul'gyneth<ref name="QCotN" /> and restore the final ward. They failed however, and Uul'gyneth was summoned into the physical realm,<ref>[[The Final Ward]]</ref> leaving the Horde champion little choice but to vanquish Uul'gyneth the Darkness. After the battle, Mayla expressed her gratitude for rescuing her people from the [[Old Gods]].<ref>[[The Darkness (quest)]]</ref> Impressed by the strength and courage of the Horde, Mayla and Baine traveled to [[Orgrimmar]] to pledge the Highmountain tauren to the Horde.<ref>[[Together We Are the Horde!]]</ref>

===Battle for Azeroth===
{{Bfa-section}}
Members of the Highmountain tribe took part in the [[Fourth War]]. Notably, they set up an outpost named [[Swiftwind Post]] in [[Drustvar]] on [[Kul Tiras]].
