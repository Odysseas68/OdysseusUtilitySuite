<p><b>Natal'hakata</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Zandalari_troll" title="Zandalari troll">Zandalari troll</a> located at the <a href="/wiki/Great_Seal" title="Great Seal">Great Seal</a> in <a href="/wiki/Dazar%27alor" title="Dazar&#39;alor">Dazar'alor</a>. He is the Voice of the King, deliverer of the royal will, keeper of the grand schedule, and representative toward the citizens wanting an audience with the royalty.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As such, he deals with various problems of the empire. He received word from <a href="/wiki/Warbeast_Kraal" title="Warbeast Kraal">Warbeast Kraal</a> about a missing handler<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and learned about problems in Dol'watha. He sent the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Speaker of the Horde</a> for aid to those locations.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the time of <a href="/wiki/Talanji" title="Talanji">Talanji</a>'s ascension as queen of Zandalar, she directed Natal'hakata to send the Speaker to <a href="/wiki/Zanchul" title="Zanchul">Zanchul</a> to accompany Talanji for the ceremony.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> He later traveled to <a href="/wiki/Orgrimmar" title="Orgrimmar">Orgrimmar</a> to see young Zandalari off and commented that the area is less fertile than Zandalar.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels"><img alt="WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png" src="/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="62" height="16" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="62" data-file-height="16" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to the <i>Warcraft</i> <a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels">novels</a>, <a href="/wiki/Novellas" title="Novellas">novellas</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Short_stories" title="Short stories">short stories</a>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>When the <a href="/wiki/Widow%27s_Bite" title="Widow&#39;s Bite">Widow's Bite</a> threatened the rule of <a href="/wiki/Talanji" title="Talanji">Talanji</a> and planned to kill <a href="/wiki/Bwonsamdi" title="Bwonsamdi">Bwonsamdi</a>, Natal'hakata was present in a meeting of the <a href="/wiki/Zanchuli_Council" title="Zanchuli Council">Zanchuli Council</a>. He said that they should assemble a force and send it to the <a href="/wiki/Necropolis_(Nazmir)" title="Necropolis (Nazmir)">Necropolis</a>. He was then impatient with <a href="/wiki/Zekhan" title="Zekhan">Zekhan</a> who also wanted to voice his opinion.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-SR&#95;7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SR-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
