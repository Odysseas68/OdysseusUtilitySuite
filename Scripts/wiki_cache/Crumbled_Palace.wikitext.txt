'''Crumbled Palace''' is a subzone found in [[Azsuna]].{{coords|47|40|Azsuna}} It is the home of [[Prince Farondis]] and the main hub of the [[Court of Farondis]].

There is no flight path, but [[Illidari Stand]] is [[Patch_7.1.0_(undocumented_changes)#World_Environment|right down the hill]]. There are also skinning and archeology camps nearby.

==Trivia==
*[[A Familiar Ring to It]] refers to it as the Crumbling Palace which might be a remnant from beta.
