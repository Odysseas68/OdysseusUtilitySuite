<p><b>Val'sharah</b> (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Phonetic_Alphabet" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:International Phonetic Alphabet"><span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)" class="IPA">/ˈvæl.ʃəˌɹɑː/</span></a> <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Help:Pronunciation respelling key"><span title="English Pronunciation Respelling" class="IPA">VAL-shə-rah</span></a></i>), sometimes spelled as <b>Val'Sharah</b>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> is a <a href="/wiki/Zone" title="Zone">region</a> in the northwestern area of the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>. It is covered by a thick forest, and is the cradle of <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elven</a> <a href="/wiki/Druid" title="Druid">druidic</a> culture. It is home to the <a href="/wiki/World_Tree" title="World Tree">World Tree</a> <a href="/wiki/Shaladrassil" title="Shaladrassil">Shaladrassil</a>. The zone is currently contested by <a href="/wiki/Xavius" title="Xavius">Xavius</a> and his <a href="/wiki/Satyrs" class="mw-redirect" title="Satyrs">satyrs</a> attempting to spread the corruption of the <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Nightmare" title="Emerald Nightmare">Emerald Nightmare</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-GamesCon&#95;3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GamesCon-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Early_history">Early history</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Early history"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Originally, this ancient forest lay within the great and undivided realm of <a href="/wiki/Kalimdor_(landmass)" title="Kalimdor (landmass)">Kalimdor</a>. It was best known as the home to the <a href="/wiki/Demigod" title="Demigod">demigod</a> <a href="/wiki/Cenarius" title="Cenarius">Cenarius</a>, the Lord of the Forest. It was within this region that Cenarius taught the <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elves</a> the way of <a href="/wiki/Druid" title="Druid">druidism</a>, with <a href="/wiki/Malfurion_Stormrage" title="Malfurion Stormrage">Malfurion Stormrage</a>, who was born in <a href="/wiki/Lorlathil" title="Lorlathil">Lorlathil</a>, becoming the first druid under his tutelage.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-GamesCon&#95;3-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GamesCon-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Val'sharah can thus be regarded as the birthplace of druidism.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Tranquil&#95;4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tranquil-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Soon, the region became the pinnacle of druidism on <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-GamesCon&#95;3-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GamesCon-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Its forests were specifically molded by druids to be a reflection of the <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Dream" title="Emerald Dream">Emerald Dream</a> – no more than an echo, but as close to the Dream as the real world could ever be.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Cenarius&#95;5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cenarius-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The forest was considered to be sacred by the druids,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Tranquil&#95;4-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tranquil-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and was populated by night elves, the children of Cenarius (<a href="/wiki/Dryad" title="Dryad">dryads</a> and <a href="/wiki/Keeper_of_the_grove" title="Keeper of the grove">keepers of the grove</a>), as well as <a href="/wiki/Furbolg" title="Furbolg">furbolgs</a>, <a href="/wiki/Moonkin" class="mw-redirect" title="Moonkin">moonkin</a> and others. The elves considered these races their allies.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point thousands of years ago, the <a href="/wiki/World_Tree" title="World Tree">World Tree</a> <a href="/wiki/Shaladrassil" title="Shaladrassil">Shaladrassil</a> was planted here. Over the ages, it grew, towering over the area and pushing its roots deep into the land. It became the protector of the region.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Die&#95;7-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Die-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The druid base of <a href="/wiki/Shala%27nir" title="Shala&#39;nir">Shala'nir</a> was established at the base of the tree.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-PPV&#95;8-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-PPV-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Apart from civilian settlements, the region also became the home to one of the most famous night elven fortresses – <a href="/wiki/Black_Rook_Hold" title="Black Rook Hold">Black Rook Hold</a>. This mighty but somber keep was chiseled into the solid rock by the <a href="/wiki/House_of_Ravencrest" title="House of Ravencrest">House of Ravencrest</a> over multiple generations. It was a well-guarded and mostly impenetrable fortress. Relying on their military might, the Ravencrests rose steadily within the elven society, eventually being considered second to the royal house only. <sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The region was also home to the elven martial city of <a href="/wiki/Kal%27delar" title="Kal&#39;delar">Kal'delar</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="War_of_the_Ancients">War of the Ancients</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: War of the Ancients"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The region saw heavy fighting during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ancients" title="War of the Ancients">War of the Ancients</a>. It was within the forests of Val'sharah that the <a href="/wiki/Wild_God" title="Wild God">Wild God</a> <a href="/wiki/Ashamane" title="Ashamane">Ashamane</a> fought a delaying battle against the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> forces bound for <a href="/wiki/Suramar" title="Suramar">Suramar</a>. Although she was able to kill the <a href="/wiki/Annihilan" title="Annihilan">annihilan</a> general <a href="/wiki/Ronokon" title="Ronokon">Ronokon</a>, the being's self-destruction immolated Ashamane and burned a crater into the forest. The Wild God's actions brought a precious time for the <a href="/wiki/Suramar_City" title="Suramar City">city of Suramar</a>, allowing them to raise a protective shield. She thus saved countless lives. The elves eventually erected a <a href="/wiki/Ashamane%27s_Fall" title="Ashamane&#39;s Fall">great shrine</a> at the place of Ashamane's sacrifice.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Tome&#95;12-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tome-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Reveal&#95;13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reveal-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The War of the Ancients also saw a demonic attack on Black Rook Hold. Lord <a href="/wiki/Kur%27talos_Ravencrest" title="Kur&#39;talos Ravencrest">Kur'talos Ravencrest</a> and <a href="/wiki/Illidan_Stormrage" title="Illidan Stormrage">Illidan Stormrage</a> led the <a href="/wiki/Moon_Guard" title="Moon Guard">Moon Guard</a> in the defense of the fortress. They eventually succeeded, albeit only at the price of Illidan sacrificing the lives of the other Moon Guard members to absorb their energy. Famous combatants in the battle included <a href="/wiki/Broxigar_the_Red" class="mw-redirect" title="Broxigar the Red">Broxigar the Red</a>, <a href="/wiki/Jarod_Shadowsong" title="Jarod Shadowsong">Jarod Shadowsong</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Rhonin" title="Rhonin">Rhonin</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Legion also tried, and failed, to destroy the <a href="/wiki/Temple_of_Elune_(Val%27sharah)" title="Temple of Elune (Val&#39;sharah)">Temple of Elune</a> located within the <a href="/wiki/Crescent_Vale" title="Crescent Vale">Crescent Vale</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Lyanis&#95;15-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lyanis-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The war ended with the <a href="/wiki/Great_Sundering" title="Great Sundering">Great Sundering</a>, which blasted Kalimdor into pieces. Val'sharah broke off from the mainland and mostly survived as part of the isle group known as the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>, along with <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>, Suramar, and others. Suramar City cut itself off from the surrounding land behind a protective barrier, further isolating Val'sharah from the outside world. In essence, the ancient forest was forgotten by the outside world with the exception of druids.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-GamesCon&#95;3-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-GamesCon-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the war, the surviving <a href="/wiki/Satyr" title="Satyr">satyrs</a> of the forest were assembled together and placed into a deep hibernation under the roots of <a href="/wiki/Shaladrassil" title="Shaladrassil">Shaladrassil</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Thaon&#95;16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thaon-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Druid_refuge">Druid refuge</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Druid refuge"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>Even after its isolation, Val'sharah has remained central to druidic culture. The main druidic organization is the order of the <a href="/wiki/Dreamweavers" title="Dreamweavers">Dreamweavers</a> (also called the Druids of Val'sharah), who have protected the Emerald Dream since time immemorial.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Many of its members are ancient enough to remember <a href="/wiki/Shaladrassil" title="Shaladrassil">Shaladrassil</a> taking root.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After the Great Sundering, they trained under Cenarius's tutelage, and acquired unparalleled knowledge in the ways of the druids.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-TTD&#95;19-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTD-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Their main base of operation is the <a href="/wiki/Dreamgrove" title="Dreamgrove">Dreamgrove</a>, which is directly linked to the <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Dreamway" title="Emerald Dreamway">Emerald Dreamway</a>, a portion of the Dream that serves as a travel hub to key locations across Azeroth. The location is protected by ancient magic, creating a sanctuary here. The leader of the Dreamweavers is archdruid <a href="/wiki/Rensar_Greathoof" title="Rensar Greathoof">Rensar Greathoof</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-TTD&#95;19-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-TTD-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Val'sharah is also the home of several of the strongest druidic individuals alive. The demi-god Cenarius, the Lord of the Forest, lives within the Grove of Cenarius.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Cenarius&#95;5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Cenarius-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Elothir" title="Elothir">Elothir</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ancient_of_lore" class="mw-redirect" title="Ancient of lore">ancient of lore</a>, resides with his acolytes in the ancient grove of <a href="/wiki/Thas%27talah" title="Thas&#39;talah">Thas'talah</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A group of druids live within the <a href="/wiki/Moonclaw_Vale" title="Moonclaw Vale">Moonclaw Vale</a>, charged with the protection of Val'sharah.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Claw" title="Druids of the Claw">Druids of the Claw</a> make their home in <a href="/wiki/Steelclaw_Vale" title="Steelclaw Vale">Steelclaw Vale</a>, dedicating themselves to observing the Emerald Dream.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Fin" title="Druids of the Fin">Druids of the Fin</a> patrol <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Bay" title="Emerald Bay">Emerald Bay</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Ashen" title="Ashen">Ashen</a> druids guard the legacy of Ashamane in the <a href="/wiki/Mistvale" title="Mistvale">Mistvale</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> There are several <a href="/wiki/Barrow_den" title="Barrow den">barrow dens</a> located here, including the <a href="/wiki/Sleeper%27s_Barrow" title="Sleeper&#39;s Barrow">Sleeper's Barrow</a> in Steelclaw Vale and the <a href="/wiki/Tel%27Andu_Barrow_Den" title="Tel&#39;Andu Barrow Den">Tel'Andu Barrow Den</a> within the Dreamgrove.
</p><p>The druids had several key artifacts in their possession. The Ashen guarded the <a href="/wiki/Fangs_of_Ashamane" title="Fangs of Ashamane">Fangs of Ashamane</a> within their shrine.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Reveal&#95;13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Reveal-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Tome&#95;12-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tome-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Meanwhile a branch of <a href="/wiki/G%27Hanir" title="G&#39;Hanir">G'Hanir</a>, the ancient Mother Tree, was kept in a shrine beneath the roots of Shaladrassil called the <a href="/wiki/Circle_of_Spirits" title="Circle of Spirits">Circle of Spirits</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Malorne%27s_Idol" class="mw-redirect" title="Malorne&#39;s Idol">Malorne's Idol</a>, a gift by the Wild God herself, was held at the shrine called <a href="/wiki/Malorne%27s_Refuge" title="Malorne&#39;s Refuge">Malorne's Refuge</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Sisterhood_of_Elune" title="Sisterhood of Elune">Sisterhood of Elune</a> is also represented within the region. Their main base of operation is the <a href="/wiki/Temple_of_Elune_(Val%27sharah)" title="Temple of Elune (Val&#39;sharah)">Temple of Elune</a> within the <a href="/wiki/Crescent_Vale" title="Crescent Vale">Crescent Vale</a>, which predates the War of the Ancients.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Lyanis&#95;15-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Lyanis-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This location also served as the hiding place for the <a href="/wiki/Tears_of_Elune" title="Tears of Elune">Tears of Elune</a>, an artifact that is part of the group of relics known as the <a href="/wiki/Pillars_of_Creation" title="Pillars of Creation">Pillars of Creation</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Tears&#95;30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tears-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The night elves have several villages scattered throughout the area. These include the travel hub of <a href="/wiki/Lorlathil" title="Lorlathil">Lorlathil</a>, <a href="/wiki/Ash%27theran" title="Ash&#39;theran">Ash'theran</a>, <a href="/wiki/Lightsong" title="Lightsong">Lightsong</a>, <a href="/wiki/Shadowfen" title="Shadowfen">Shadowfen</a>, the enclave of <a href="/wiki/Shala%27nir" title="Shala&#39;nir">Shala'nir</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and others. Several other sentient races also inhabit the forest, including the <a href="/wiki/Cenarius%27s_children" class="mw-redirect" title="Cenarius&#39;s children">Cenarius's children</a>, <a href="/wiki/Furbolg" title="Furbolg">furbolg</a>, <a href="/wiki/Moonkin" class="mw-redirect" title="Moonkin">moonkin</a>, <a href="/wiki/Harpy" title="Harpy">harpies</a> and <a href="/wiki/Grell" title="Grell">grell</a>. Recently a new village, <a href="/wiki/Bradensbrook" title="Bradensbrook">Bradensbrook</a>, was founded on the west coast by uncorrupted humans from <a href="/wiki/Gilneas_(kingdom)" title="Gilneas (kingdom)">Gilneas</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Val'sharah has a very vivid fauna and flora.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legion">Legion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Legion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>

He was able to wake up the <a href="/wiki/Satyr" title="Satyr">satyrs</a> slumbering below <a href="/wiki/Shaladrassil" title="Shaladrassil">Shaladrassil</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Thaon&#95;16-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Thaon-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and corrupt the World Tree itself. However, Xavius got to the artifact first,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Tears&#95;30-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tears-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and managed to corrupt Ysera herself with it.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-47" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-47"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>47<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind" title="Tyrande Whisperwind">Tyrande Whisperwind</a> tracked down the Nightmare Lord, although not before losing the archdruid Elothir to the corrupting influence.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-48" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-48"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>48<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In the climactic battle at the Temple of Elune, Tyrande and her companions were forced to kill Ysera, reclaiming and cleansing the Tears of Elune in the process.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-49" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-49"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>49<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>These dramatic events caused heavy damage to the forest and its inhabitants. Shala'nir was completely lost to the Nightmare, its luckier inhabitants evacuating the village.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-FF&#95;50-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FF-50"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A similar fate befell Shadowfen, which was hastily abandoned.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-51" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-51"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>51<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Whole sections of the forest, such as the area of Dreadwood and Moonrest, were lost to the affliction.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Purge&#95;52-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Purge-52"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-53" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-53"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>53<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Crazed creatures began appearing throughout the land, and brazen satyrs and their allies attacked several locations. Many elves escaped to places such as <a href="/wiki/Starsong_Refuge" title="Starsong Refuge">Starsong Refuge</a>, or even crossed the border into Suramar. The corrupted tendrils of Shaladrassil followed them even there.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-FF&#95;50-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-FF-50"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>50<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Burning Legion also assaulted the region directly. The heroes of Azeroth helped the Dreamweavers and other locals repel the assault.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-54" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-54"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">Demons</a> attacked several key locations, including the <a href="/wiki/Ashamane%27s_Fall" title="Ashamane&#39;s Fall">Shrine of Ashamane</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-55" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-55"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>55<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Sundersong_Glade" title="Sundersong Glade">Sundersong Glade</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-56" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-56"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>A wholly different crisis emerged around <a href="/wiki/Black_Rook_Hold" title="Black Rook Hold">Black Rook Hold</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Warlock" title="Warlock">warlock</a> <a href="/wiki/Gul%27dan_(alternate_universe)" title="Gul&#39;dan (alternate universe)">Gul'dan</a> reanimated the spirits of Lord <a href="/wiki/Kur%27talos_Ravencrest" title="Kur&#39;talos Ravencrest">Kur'talos Ravencrest</a> and his soldiers,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-57" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-57"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>57<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-58" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-58"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>58<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> who started assaulting <a href="/wiki/Bradensbrook" title="Bradensbrook">Bradensbrook</a>. With the help of <a href="/wiki/Jarod_Shadowsong" title="Jarod Shadowsong">Jarod Shadowsong</a>, the villagers were eventually able to repulse this threat. Shadowsong and adventurers then pushed into Black Rook Hold itself. After heavy fighting, they were able to slay the <a href="/wiki/Dreadlord" class="mw-redirect" title="Dreadlord">dreadlord</a> <a href="/wiki/Dantalionax" title="Dantalionax">Dantalionax</a>, and finally put the spirits of Ravencrest's soldiers to rest.
</p><p>Adventurers eventually followed Xavius into the corrupted <a href="/wiki/Darkheart_Thicket" title="Darkheart Thicket">Darkheart Thicket</a>, managing to free Malfurion Stormrage.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-59" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-59"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>59<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Eventually, the adventurers entered the Emerald Nightmare itself, freeing Cenarius and slaying Xavius. With the immediate danger gone, the druids began the work to cleanse the Nightmare from the land, hoping to reclaim Shala'nir and safeguard the roots of the World Tree.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-60" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-60"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>60<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, it remains to be seen if the corruption can ever be fully cleansed.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Purge&#95;52-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Purge-52"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>52<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Battle_for_Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Battle for Azeroth"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="Battle for Azeroth"><img alt="Battle for Azeroth" src="/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>As of the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>, and after the <a href="/wiki/Burning_of_Teldrassil" class="mw-redirect" title="Burning of Teldrassil">Burning of Teldrassil</a>, Val'sharah and its <a href="/wiki/Temple_of_Elune_(Val%27sharah)" title="Temple of Elune (Val&#39;sharah)">Temple of Elune</a> are among the few remaining night elf communities on Azeroth.
</p><p>An Alliance adventurer was sent to <a href="/wiki/Mevris_Ghostdancer" title="Mevris Ghostdancer">Mevris Ghostdancer</a> for assistance in seeking out raw materials to create the <a href="/wiki/Drums_of_Primal_Might" class="mw-redirect" title="Drums of Primal Might">Drums of Primal Might</a> by his wife <a href="/wiki/Daniss_Ghostdancer" title="Daniss Ghostdancer">Daniss Ghostdancer</a>, who sought a means of vengeance against the Horde for the lives they destroyed.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-61" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-61"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>61<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The night elves of the Temple have established active trade ties with the neighboring <a href="/wiki/Gilnean" class="mw-redirect" title="Gilnean">Gilnean</a> village of <a href="/wiki/Bradensbrook" title="Bradensbrook">Bradensbrook</a>, sending and receiving supplies to and from the villagers via ships.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-62" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-62"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>62<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Quests">Quests</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah?action=edit&amp;section=13" title="Edit section: Quests"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah_storyline" title="Val&#39;sharah storyline">Val'sharah storyline</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<p><a href="/wiki/Archmage_Khadgar" class="mw-redirect" title="Archmage Khadgar">Archmage Khadgar</a> is trying to recover one of the <a href="/wiki/Pillars_of_Creation" title="Pillars of Creation">Pillars of Creation</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Tears_of_Elune" title="Tears of Elune">Tears of Elune</a>. At the same time <a href="/wiki/Malfurion_Stormrage" title="Malfurion Stormrage">Malfurion Stormrage</a> is on a mission to figure out what happened to his mentor, the demigod <a href="/wiki/Cenarius" title="Cenarius">Cenarius</a>. The Nightmare is starting to take hold and the satyr <a href="/wiki/Xavius" title="Xavius">Xavius</a> has designs on the Tears and <a href="/wiki/Ysera" title="Ysera">Ysera</a>. Can adventurers sort everything out in time?
</p>
