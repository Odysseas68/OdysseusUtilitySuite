The term '''quartermaster''' has a number of meanings in ''World of Warcraft'', mainly associated with ''faction quartermasters''.  These are [[NPC]]s who will sell items to players that have accumulated a sufficient [[reputation]] level with a particular [[faction]].  They also sell [[championing]] tabards, which allow the player to gain reputation with a faction in level-relevant dungeons. Many vendors added in ''[[World of Warcraft: Cataclysm]]'', and accepting [[currencies]] other than gold, are also called quartermasters.

The term quartermaster has been increasingly used since ''[[The Burning Crusade]]'' expansion when each of the dungeon reputation factions had an NPC identified by name as the quartermaster.  The term is commonly applied retroactively to classic vendors who served a similar purpose.

Typically the items available at {{rep|Exalted}} are of a high quality, and may include rare cosmetic rewards such as [[Mounts]] or [[Companions]], or unique [[profession]] recipes.
