<p><b>Thunder Totem</b> (or <b>Thundertotem</b>)<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> is the capital city of the <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_tauren" title="Highmountain tauren">Highmountain tauren</a>, located in the center of <a href="/wiki/Highmountain" title="Highmountain">Highmountain</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>. The city resembles a massive totem, towering into the sky. The totem overlooks the expansive and awe-insipring landscape of Highmountain. Much of the city stretches out beyond the totem, camps fabricated on cliff faces and bluffs are all connected by wooden bridges to the city center. Established hundreds of years ago, the city ties a coalition between the major factions of Highmountain. <a href="/wiki/Mayla_Highmountain" title="Mayla Highmountain">Mayla Highmountain</a> just recently became leader of the Highmountain tauren due to her father's unexpected death when <a href="/wiki/Dargrul" title="Dargrul">Dargrul</a> struck him down with the mighty Hammer of Khaz'goroth and stole it.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Thunder_Totem?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Thunder_Totem_AMD.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Thunder_Totem_AMD.jpg/320px-Thunder_Totem_AMD.jpg?557ead" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="137" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1672" data-file-height="714" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Thunder_Totem_AMD.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Thunder Totem as seen in <i><a href="/wiki/Highmountain:_A_Mountain_Divided" title="Highmountain: A Mountain Divided">Highmountain: A Mountain Divided</a></i>.</div></div></div>
<p>Centuries ago, Highmountain was the home of four large tribes. The <a href="/wiki/Rivermane_tribe" title="Rivermane tribe">Rivermane</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Skyhorn_tribe" title="Skyhorn tribe">Skyhorn</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Bloodtotem_tribe" title="Bloodtotem tribe">Bloodtotem</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Tribe" title="Highmountain Tribe">Highmountain</a> tribes. For ages they lived independently, often with a quarrelsome relationship. However, when the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> invaded during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ancients" title="War of the Ancients">War of the Ancients</a>, <a href="/wiki/Huln_Highmountain" title="Huln Highmountain">Huln Highmountain</a> united the tribes to fight their common enemy.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This coalition would last for centuries, and for a long time Thunder Totem was the heart of the Highmountain tauren. All three tribes have lived in unity, each sharing a path into the <a href="/wiki/Hall_of_Chieftains" title="Hall of Chieftains">Hall of Chieftains</a> and each sharing an animal on the totem. 
</p><p>However, the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion of the Burning Legion</a> has severed the salient pact between the Highmountain tauren. <a href="/wiki/Dargrul" title="Dargrul">Dargrul</a>, king of the <a href="/wiki/Drogbar" title="Drogbar">Drogbar</a> has stolen the <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_mace_1h_titanpillar_b_01.png/16px-Inv_mace_1h_titanpillar_b_01.png?be4cfc" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Hammer_of_Khaz%27goroth" title="Hammer of Khaz&#39;goroth"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Hammer of Khaz&#39;goroth is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Hammer of Khaz'goroth<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> from the <a href="/wiki/Hammer%27s_Cradle" title="Hammer&#39;s Cradle">Hammer's Cradle</a> in Thunder Totem and killed the Highmountain leader.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Bloodtotem tribe has joined the Burning Legion, and the Rivermane tribe has been driven from their home.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the end of the third invasion of the Burning Legion, parts of the city were turned into a small Horde capital, including <a href="/wiki/Pandaren" title="Pandaren">pandaren</a> <a href="/wiki/Monk_trainers" title="Monk trainers">monk trainers</a>. Survivors of the Bloodtotem tribe who didn't chose to join the Legion also settled at Thunder Totem.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Geography">Geography</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Thunder_Totem?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Geography"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Thunder Totem is nestled against a large cliff face in the central valley of Highmountain. It is a large tent with a platform perched atop a great bluff, the totem being the crown jewel. The weather here is usually crisp and breezy, clouds are never in sight, even at night.
</p><p>The entirety of Thunder Totem is comprised of many small camps and settlements all centered around the main bluff. A settlement to the north provides access to Thunder Totem from <a href="/wiki/Pinerock_Basin" title="Pinerock Basin">Pinerock Basin</a>. A large path to the east connects directly to <a href="/wiki/Snowblind_Mesa" title="Snowblind Mesa">Snowblind Mesa</a>. And a path to the south connects to the <a href="/wiki/Path_of_Huln" title="Path of Huln">Path of Huln</a>. The main platform is a large circle coverd by a tent. This is where most of the merchants set up shop and sell their goods.
Thunder Totem, the bluff itself contains two central chambers, the <a href="/wiki/Hammer%27s_Cradle" title="Hammer&#39;s Cradle">Hammer's Cradle</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Hall_of_Chieftains" title="Hall of Chieftains">Hall of Chieftains</a>. Two elevators travel down to the Hall of Cheftains from the platform and three halls link the Hall of Chieftains to the outside. The <a href="/wiki/Path_of_Earth" title="Path of Earth">Path of Earth</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Path_of_Sky" title="Path of Sky">Path of Sky</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Path_of_Water" title="Path of Water">Path of Water</a>. 
</p>
