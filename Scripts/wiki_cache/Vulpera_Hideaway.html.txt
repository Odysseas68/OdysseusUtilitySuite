<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vulpera_Hideaway?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Battle_for_Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vulpera_Hideaway?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Battle for Azeroth"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="Battle for Azeroth"><img alt="Battle for Azeroth" src="/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>After the destruction of the <a href="/wiki/Abandoned_Burrows" title="Abandoned Burrows">Abandoned Burrows</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Voldunai" title="Voldunai">Voldunai</a> <a href="/wiki/Vulpera" title="Vulpera">vulpera</a> fled to what would become the Vulpera Hideaway.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The refuge was not intended as a permanent one, as the vulpera caravans were ready to leave at moment's notice should the hideaway be discovered by the <a href="/wiki/Faithless" title="Faithless">Faithless</a> <a href="/wiki/Sethrak" title="Sethrak">sethrak</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Aided by adventurers from the <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a>, <a href="/wiki/Kiro" title="Kiro">Kiro</a> rallied numerous vulpera to this region as a safehaven, serving as the main base for the Voldunai.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>From here, <a href="/wiki/Nisha" title="Nisha">Nisha</a> and Kiro devised a plan to strike back at the Faithless for their crimes against their people, working with the Horde and the Zandalari that they had found, such as <a href="/wiki/Bladeguard_Kaja" title="Bladeguard Kaja">Bladeguard Kaja</a> and <a href="/wiki/Rakera" title="Rakera">Rakera</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the downfall of the Faithless and the winding down of the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>, the vulpera joined the Horde. Vulpera heroes who proved their worth to the Horde and in the name of their people were called back to the Hideaway and granted special equipment by <a href="/wiki/Hagashi" title="Hagashi">Hagashi</a>. It was put together by Kiro and the rest of the Voldunai in appreciation for their hard work.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
