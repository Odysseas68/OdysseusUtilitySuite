<p><b>Warmage Kath'leen</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Human" title="Human">human</a> located near the <a href="/wiki/Violet_Citadel" title="Violet Citadel">Violet Citadel</a> in <a href="/wiki/Dalaran" title="Dalaran">Dalaran</a>. She can provide directions for various locations in the <a href="/wiki/Dalaran" title="Dalaran">Dalaran</a>.
</p><p>It is currently unknown if she survived Dalaran's destruction.
</p>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>With the invasion of the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>, she joined the effort of the <a href="/wiki/Armies_of_Legionfall" title="Armies of Legionfall">Armies of Legionfall</a> as one of their artificers.
</p>
