<p><b>The Dreamgrove</b> is the <a href="/wiki/Order_Halls" class="mw-redirect" title="Order Halls">order hall</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Druid" title="Druid">druids</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Dreamweavers" title="Dreamweavers">Dreamweavers</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Cenarion_Circle" title="Cenarion Circle">Cenarion Circle</a>. Located in <a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah" title="Val&#39;sharah">Val'sharah</a>, the Dreamgrove is directly linked to the <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Dreamway" title="Emerald Dreamway">Emerald Dreamway</a>, a section of the <a href="/wiki/Emerald_Dream" title="Emerald Dream">Emerald Dream</a> connecting several locations powerful in Nature magic across <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>, such as the <a href="/wiki/Grizzly_Hills" title="Grizzly Hills">Grizzly Hills</a> and <a href="/wiki/Twilight_Grove" title="Twilight Grove">Twilight Grove</a>.
</p><p>When asked what the Dreamgrove meant to them, the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Archdruid</a> replied among the followings:
</p>
<ul><li>Balance - Like the turn of every season, so do they change, adapt, survive, and endure. They ensure the promise of the Dreamgrove: that all things return to balance.</li>
<li>Life - The Dreamgrove is the warm light by which death's shadow is cast. That is worth protecting.</li>
<li>Nature - They are the vast and varied nature of all living things. The freedom of the world that was, that is, and that will ever be. Good and evil may wax and wane, but nature is eternal.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Map">Map</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Dreamgrove?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Map"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/File:WorldMap-TheDreamgrove.jpg" class="image" title="map of the Dreamgrove"><img alt="map of the Dreamgrove" src="/images/thumb/WorldMap-TheDreamgrove.jpg/400px-WorldMap-TheDreamgrove.jpg?65d254" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="400" height="267" data-file-width="1002" data-file-height="668" /></a>
</p>
<dl><dt><a href="/wiki/Tome_of_the_Ancients" title="Tome of the Ancients">Tome of the Ancients</a></dt></dl>
<p>A large book located north of the Circle of Spirits. This tome starts blank but will grow to hold the lore, history, and powers of the druid artifacts as the player funnels order hall resources into it. Every two work orders provide a new page in the tome. <a href="/wiki/Celadine_the_Fatekeeper" title="Celadine the Fatekeeper">Celadine the Fatekeeper</a> is the artifact researcher.
</p>
<dl><dt>Seed of Ages</dt></dl>
<p>The Artifact Forge is located west of the Circle of Spirits. It allows players to upgrade their artifact as well as change its appearance. <a href="/wiki/Tender_Daranelle" title="Tender Daranelle">Tender Daranelle</a> can refund artifact power.
</p>
<dl><dt>Sub-zones</dt></dl>
<ul><li>The Retreat: Located in the eastern part of the grove, it houses armor racks for raid tier sets as well as <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_jewelcrafting_taladitecrystal.png/16px-Inv_jewelcrafting_taladitecrystal.png?fdad6c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/Light%27s_Heart" title="Light&#39;s Heart"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="Light&#39;s Heart is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Light's Heart<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>.</li>
<li>Tel'Andu Barrow Den: Located in the northern part of the grove, it is where the <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Claw" title="Druids of the Claw">Druids of the Claw</a> sleep.</li>
<li>Circle of Spirits: Located at the center of the grove, it contains four statues that represent each of the druid specializations and the mythic cache.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Accessing_the_Dreamgrove">Accessing the Dreamgrove</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Dreamgrove?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Accessing the Dreamgrove"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<dl><dt>On foot</dt></dl>
<p>The Dreamgrove can be accessed on foot via a dirt path running north from the <a href="/wiki/Temple_of_Elune_(Val%27sharah)" title="Temple of Elune (Val&#39;sharah)">Temple of Elune</a> along the shoreline. Non-druids who attempt to enter are put to sleep by the <a href="/wiki/Ancient_protector" title="Ancient protector">ancient protectors</a> at the border and teleported out.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Order_Hall_Advancement">Order Hall Advancement</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Dreamgrove?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Order Hall Advancement"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Leafbeard_the_Storied" title="Leafbeard the Storied">Leafbeard the Storied</a> handles <a href="/wiki/Order_Advancement" title="Order Advancement">Order Hall advancement</a> upgrades. There are 8 tiers with each tier having two available upgrades to choose from, except two tiers which only have one upgrade. Each upgrade will require <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_orderhall_orderresources.png/16px-Inv_orderhall_orderresources.png?19cd4a" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Order_Resources" title="Order Resources"><span class="currencylink ajaxttlink qc-common"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Order Resources<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span> and a research time before their buff will be available. Once upgraded, a player can change to the other upgrade by paying another Order Resources cost and waiting the required research time.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Dreamgrove?action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The Dreamgrove is not an <a href="/wiki/Instance" title="Instance">instance</a>, but trying to enter it as a non-druid tints the screen green and gives you the debuff "Drowsy", which teleports you out if you do not leave in five seconds.</li>
<li>The Dreamgrove is one of the few <a href="/wiki/Order_Hall" class="mw-redirect" title="Order Hall">Order Halls</a> with a mailbox, which is located across from the Artifact Researcher.</li>
<li>The <a href="/wiki/Tradewind_%26_Crow:_Dreamgrove_Detectives" title="Tradewind &amp; Crow: Dreamgrove Detectives">Tradewind &amp; Crow: Dreamgrove Detectives</a> scenario was planned to take place here, featuring <a href="/wiki/Arthur_Tradewind" title="Arthur Tradewind">Arthur Tradewind</a> and <a href="/wiki/Thisalee_Crow" title="Thisalee Crow">Thisalee Crow</a> as a buddy cop duo in a murder mystery.</li></ul>
