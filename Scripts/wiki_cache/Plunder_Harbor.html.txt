<p><b>Plunder Harbor</b> is a town in eastern <a href="/wiki/Tiragarde_Sound" title="Tiragarde Sound">Tiragarde Sound</a> under the control of the <a href="/wiki/Fogsail_Freebooters" title="Fogsail Freebooters">Fogsail Freebooters</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Maiden%27s_Lament" title="Maiden&#39;s Lament">Maiden's Lament</a> inn is located here.
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>, the Fogsail Freebooters allowed the <a href="/wiki/Honorbound" title="Honorbound">Honorbound</a> <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> to use the harbor as a safe foothold in Kul Tiras after they helped <a href="/wiki/Owings" title="Owings">Owings</a> pull a mutiny on the previous captain <a href="/wiki/Captain_Rhenik" title="Captain Rhenik">Rhenik</a> for wanting to sell the crew out to the <a href="/wiki/Ashvane_Trading_Company" title="Ashvane Trading Company">Ashvane Trading Company</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Horde's actions led to the Proudmoore fleet attacking the harbor, sending the <i><a href="/wiki/Siren%27s_Daughter" title="Siren&#39;s Daughter">Siren's Daughter</a></i> and <i><a href="/wiki/Valorous_Rose" title="Valorous Rose">Valorous Rose</a></i> battleships to get <a href="/wiki/Derek_Proudmoore" title="Derek Proudmoore">Derek Proudmoore</a>'s body back.
</p>
