<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Objectives">Objectives</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/The_Wardens_are_Watching?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Objectives"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Attack a <a href="/wiki/Druid_of_the_Flame" class="mw-redirect" title="Druid of the Flame">Druid of the Flame</a> to weaken it, then lure it into your <a href="/wiki/Shadow_Warden" title="Shadow Warden">Shadow Warden</a>'s trap.
</p>
<ul><li>Druid of the Flame captured</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Description">Description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/The_Wardens_are_Watching?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Description"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>We must tread carefully, &lt;class&gt;.  <a href="/wiki/Fandral_Staghelm" title="Fandral Staghelm">Fandral's</a> Druids of the Flame practice their fiery magic beyond the pillars to the north.
</p><p>I need you to capture one of those druids alive for questioning.  We must seize every opportunity to learn of their plans.
</p><p>One of my wardens will accompany you.  Follow her instructions if you wish to survive.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Rewards">Rewards</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/The_Wardens_are_Watching?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Rewards"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>You will receive:
</p>
<ul><li>2x <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_markoftheworldtree.png/16px-Inv_misc_markoftheworldtree.png?4073be" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Mark_of_the_World_Tree" title="Mark of the World Tree"><span class="currencylink ajaxttlink qc-common"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Mark of the World Tree<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span></li>
<li><span class="nobreak ajaxoutertt">16<span><a href="/wiki/Money#Types_of_coins" title="g"><img alt="g" src="/images/thumb/Gold.png/14px-Gold.png?c28389" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="14" height="14" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20" /></a></span>&#32;54<span><a href="/wiki/Money#Types_of_coins" title="s"><img alt="s" src="/images/thumb/Silver.png/14px-Silver.png?af780d" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="14" height="14" data-file-width="20" data-file-height="20" /></a></span></span></li>
<li>+250 <a href="/wiki/Reputation" title="Reputation">reputation</a> with the <a href="/wiki/Guardians_of_Hyjal" title="Guardians of Hyjal">Guardians of Hyjal</a></li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Progress">Progress</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/The_Wardens_are_Watching?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Progress"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>The Druids of the Flame have gone beyond redemption.  Now they will answer to us for their crimes.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Completion">Completion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/The_Wardens_are_Watching?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Completion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes">Notes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/The_Wardens_are_Watching?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Notes"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

</p>
<ul><li><span class="text-say">Call for aid and the Shadow Wardens will answer. I am ready for anything.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">Elune greet you, champion. I will watch your back.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">Reporting for duty. There is no time to lose.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">My blade is yours, champion.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">My blade thirsts for battle. Do not keep me waiting.</span></li></ul>
<p>The Shadow Warden attacking:
</p>
<ul><li><span class="text-say">Ask not for mercy. My blade will not grant it.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">Fire burns many things, but it cannot touch a shadow.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">I promise this won't hurt...too much.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">I will cleanse your sins with the edge of my dagger.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">I will deliver my enemies to their ancestors!</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">I will show no mercy to the defiled!</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">Justice will be served, heathen!</span></li></ul>
<dl><dt>Shield</dt>
<dd><span class="text-say"><b>Druid of the Flame&#160;says:</b> Master, fill me with your glorious radiance!</span></dd>
<dd><span class="text-say"><b>Shadow Warden&#160;says:</b> It's no use! Their shield is too strong!</span></dd></dl>
<dl><dt>Trap set</dt>
<dd><span class="text-say">Quickly! Lure the enemy onto my trap!</span></dd></dl>
<p><a href="/wiki/Druid_of_the_Flame_(mob)" title="Druid of the Flame (mob)">Druid of the Flame</a> trapped:
</p>
<ul><li><span class="text-say">I am impressed, hero. Thank you for helping me subdue this...druid. I can take this <a href="/wiki/Heretic" title="Heretic">heretic</a> back on my own.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">Your skills in combat are admirable, hero. Keep yourself useful on the battlefield. I will take this criminal back for...questioning.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">Not bad, champion! What they say about you is true. However, we part ways here. I must go deal with this prisoner...personally.</span></li>
<li><span class="text-say">Good work, champion. I will take this foul creature back with me. This druid has much to answer to.</span></li></ul>
