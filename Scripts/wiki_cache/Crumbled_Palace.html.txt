<p><b>Crumbled Palace</b> is a subzone found in <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>.<span class="tttemplatelink coords-link noexcerpt" data-coords="/way 47 40"><a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna"><sup>[47,&#160;40]</sup></a></span><span style="display:none;"><span class="ZoneMap" style="position:relative;width:300px;height:200px;display:block"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/WorldMap-Azsuna.jpg/300px-WorldMap-Azsuna.jpg?4377a2" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="300" height="200" class="notpageimage" data-file-width="1002" data-file-height="668" /><span style="position:absolute;left:47%;top:40%;line-height:0;margin:-5.5px"><img alt="" src="/images/Blip.png?e1e66a" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="11" height="11" class="notpageimage" data-file-width="11" data-file-height="11" /></span></span></span> It is the home of <a href="/wiki/Prince_Farondis" title="Prince Farondis">Prince Farondis</a> and the main hub of the <a href="/wiki/Court_of_Farondis" title="Court of Farondis">Court of Farondis</a>.
</p><p>There is no flight path, but <a href="/wiki/Illidari_Stand" title="Illidari Stand">Illidari Stand</a> is <a href="/wiki/Patch_7.1.0_(undocumented_changes)#World_Environment" title="Patch 7.1.0 (undocumented changes)">right down the hill</a>. There are also skinning and archeology camps nearby.
</p>
