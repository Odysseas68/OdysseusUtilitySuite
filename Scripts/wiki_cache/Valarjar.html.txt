<p>The <b>Valarjar</b>, the "warriors of the storm",<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are an army of ascended <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a> who serve <a href="/wiki/Keeper" title="Keeper">Keeper</a> <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a>. Chosen by the All-Father, the strongest vrykul have their spirits lifted to the Halls by the <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr" title="Val&#39;kyr">Val'kyr</a> where they fight eternal, shiny and gold,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> protecting <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a> from all threats.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Valarjar can be given either mighty, golden Stormforged bodies, or become Val'kyr themselves with power over life and death. According to the <a href="/wiki/Bronzebeard" title="Bronzebeard">Bronzebeard</a> brothers, the All-Father follows the <a href="/wiki/Light" title="Light">Light</a>, with the Val'kyr <a href="/wiki/Shieldmaiden" title="Shieldmaiden">shieldmaidens</a> in his army being referred to as warriors of the Light.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>This army was originally created as an equivalent to the <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Aspects" title="Dragon Aspects">Dragon Aspects</a>, who Odyn judged unworthy for the task of protecting Azeroth, until the Val'kyr queen <a href="/wiki/Helya" title="Helya">Helya</a> cursed him and his army to be trapped within the Halls of Valor for ages. During the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion</a> of Azeroth, the spell that kept them imprisoned was lifted with Helya's recent death, freeing Odyn and his golden warriors for good. From a section of the Halls named <a href="/wiki/Skyhold" title="Skyhold">Skyhold</a>, which served as their <a href="/wiki/Order_Halls" class="mw-redirect" title="Order Halls">order hall</a>, the Valarjar fought alongside the other <a href="/wiki/Class_Order" title="Class Order">orders</a> under the lead of the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Battlelord</a>, the champion of the All-Father.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Helarjar" title="Helarjar">Helarjar</a> are the <a href="/wiki/Kvaldir" title="Kvaldir">kvaldir</a> equivalent to the Valarjar, loyal to Helya.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Creation">Creation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Creation"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/The_Legend_of_Odyn" title="The Legend of Odyn">The Legend of Odyn</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:262px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Odyn_Prime_Designate_by_Alex_Stone.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Odyn_Prime_Designate_by_Alex_Stone.jpg/260px-Odyn_Prime_Designate_by_Alex_Stone.jpg?7a571e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="260" height="334" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1200" data-file-height="1543" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Odyn_Prime_Designate_by_Alex_Stone.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Keeper" title="Keeper">Keeper</a> <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a>, the All-Father.</div></div></div>
<p>Serving as Prime Designate over a relatively young <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Titan_keeper" class="mw-redirect" title="Titan keeper">titan keeper</a> <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a> often walked the world disguised as a variety of beings, in order to observe his charge. In particular, Odyn was drawn to the mighty <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a>, whom he admired for their courage and tenacity, and would mingle among them.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>After witnessing their battle with the serpent <a href="/wiki/Ysildar" title="Ysildar">Ysildar</a>, Odyn became further sure of his view that they were a fearless race gifted with nobility and valor. When the other Keepers decided to ask the <a href="/wiki/Pantheon_of_Order" class="mw-redirect" title="Pantheon of Order">Pantheon of Order</a> to empower the <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Aspects" title="Dragon Aspects">Dragon Aspects</a> for their heroism against <a href="/wiki/Galakrond" title="Galakrond">Galakrond</a>, Odyn was vehemently against it, stating that only <a href="/wiki/Titan-forged" title="Titan-forged">titan-forged</a> could be trusted to defend Azeroth, as he did not trust the <a href="/wiki/Proto-dragon" title="Proto-dragon">proto-dragons</a>. Despite his protests, the Dragon Aspects were given their duty, and so Odyn relinquished his position of Prime Designate,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Ulduar&#39;s&#95;Oath&#95;6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ulduar&#39;s_Oath-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> seeking instead to create his own elite army, one of steadfast and valiant stature, and saw the vrykul to be the most desirable candidate for his army.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Under Odyn's command, the <a href="/wiki/Titanic_watcher" title="Titanic watcher">Titanic watcher</a> <a href="/wiki/Helya" title="Helya">Helya</a> raised a section of <a href="/wiki/Ulduar" title="Ulduar">Ulduar</a> into the skies, transforming it into a new seat of power known as the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a>, which he intended to populate with his new army. The All-Father then told the vrykul that those who died a worthy death in battle would have their spirits brought to the Halls and given new immortal bodies infused with the power of the storm to fight as his Valarjar, which Odyn thought would be better defenders than the <a href="/wiki/Dragon" title="Dragon">dragons</a>. In order to retrieve the spirits of the deceased, Odyn needed vrykul to volunteer to become <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr" title="Val&#39;kyr">Val'kyr</a>, inspired by the <a href="/wiki/Kyrian" title="Kyrian">kyrian</a> he saw in the <a href="/wiki/Shadowlands" title="Shadowlands">Shadowlands</a> after he had sacrificed his eye to <a href="/wiki/Mueh%27zala" title="Mueh&#39;zala">Mueh'zala</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:292px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Sunborne_Val%27kyr_art.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Sunborne_Val%27kyr_art.jpg/290px-Sunborne_Val%27kyr_art.jpg?413440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="290" height="328" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="973" data-file-height="1101" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Sunborne_Val%27kyr_art.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr" title="Val&#39;kyr">Val'kyr</a> of Odyn have a mission to retrieve the <a href="/wiki/Soul" title="Soul">souls</a> of the worthy dead to become Valarjar.</div></div></div>
<p>Since no one was willing to make this sacrifice, the All-Father decided he would need to create his servants by force. However, Helya protested against the turning of her fellow titan-forged into Val'kyr, threatening to bring the Halls of Valor back down on Azeroth. Instead, her disobedience was seen as a threat, and she was ultimately transformed into the first Val'kyr and ordered to begin ferrying vrykul souls from the Shadowlands, as well as transform other unwilling vrykul into Val'kyr.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Odyn would later lie to his followers that Helya's transformation had been due to her own arrogance: he claimed that during their argument, she had reached out for the power of the Shadowlands and subsequently gotten sucked into the realm, destroying her body and transforming her into a twisted wraith before he managed to pull her out and that she had subsequently apologized and begged him to make her a Val'kyr.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point, it seems that Odyn still saw fit to uplift some dragons to serve in his army. These <a href="/wiki/Storm_dragon" title="Storm dragon">storm dragons</a>, "born of the gods",<sup id="cite&#95;ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> are known as the <a href="/wiki/Thorignir" title="Thorignir">Thorignir</a>. Imbued with the power of the storms and led by <a href="/wiki/Thrymjaris" title="Thrymjaris">Thrymjaris</a>, they swore fealty to the titan keeper. They are of great importance, as protectors of <a href="/wiki/Stormheim" title="Stormheim">Stormheim</a>, presiding over the Trial of Will to determine the worth of those who seek the <a href="/wiki/Aegis_of_Aggramar" title="Aegis of Aggramar">Aegis of Aggramar</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and helping to prevent the unworthy from entering the Halls of Valor.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Though all had gone according to Odyn's design, Helya nursed resentment against him for thousands of years, until the day <a href="/wiki/Loken" title="Loken">Loken</a>, a Keeper manipulated by the <a href="/wiki/Old_God" title="Old God">Old God</a> <a href="/wiki/Yogg-Saron" title="Yogg-Saron">Yogg-Saron</a>, convinced her to turn against him. She used the same magic that had been used to lock the <a href="/wiki/Elemental_Plane" title="Elemental Plane">Elemental Plane</a> to seal Odyn and his Valarjar army inside the Halls of Valor, and then decided to take vrykul souls in her new realm <a href="/wiki/Helheim" title="Helheim">Helheim</a>. The imprisonment of the Valarjar would last for thousands of years, during which Helya would be replaced by <a href="/wiki/Eyir" title="Eyir">Eyir</a> as the leader of the Val'kyr.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Shortly after Helya sealed the Halls of Valor, she was found and attacked by <a href="/wiki/Ingvar" title="Ingvar">Ingvar</a>, one of the few Valarjar who had been outside the Halls at the time. Ingvar almost managed to kill Helya with his weapons, the <a href="/wiki/Warswords_of_the_Valarjar" title="Warswords of the Valarjar">Warswords of the Valarjar</a>, but Helya overpowered and destroyed him, subsequently keeping the warswords as a memento of her victory over Odyn.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Warswords&#95;16-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Warswords-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legion">Legion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Legion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<div class="noexcerpt" style="margin: calc(0.5em + 1vh) auto calc(0.5em + 1vh) min(24px, calc(15px + 1.25%)); max-width:calc(70% + 15em)">
<div style="font-style:italic">
<p>“In the clouds high above Stormheim, the titanic keeper Odyn has called the greatest vrykul warriors on Azeroth to be his Valarjar. Amidst feasting halls and hunting grounds, these ascended vrykul test themselves and prepare for the great war that is to come.”
</p>
</div>
<dl><dd>— <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor#Adventure_Guide" title="Halls of Valor">Adventure Guide entry for the Halls of Valor</a></dd></dl></div>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:352px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Halls_of_Valor.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Halls_of_Valor.jpg/350px-Halls_of_Valor.jpg?93e699" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="350" height="199" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1904" data-file-height="1080" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Halls_of_Valor.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div><a href="/wiki/Skyhold" title="Skyhold">Skyhold</a>, in the heavens of <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>Though Odyn and the Valarjar remained largely confined to the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a> for millennia, many heroic vrykul souls chosen by Odyn continued to be collected by his Val'kyr and taken to the floating fortress above <a href="/wiki/Stormheim" title="Stormheim">Stormheim</a>, which granted the region a special significance for the vrykul people. Odyn himself was also still able to roam the outside world as an observer, favoring a disguise as the sage <a href="/wiki/Havi" class="mw-redirect" title="Havi">Havi</a>. Among the vrykul of the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a> in particular, the legend of the Valarjar remained quite alive and widespread, and many sought a worthy death in combat to join the ranks of the formidable Valarjar. They worshiped Odyn as a deity, and though native to <a href="/wiki/Northrend" title="Northrend">Northrend</a>, many vrykul would make the voyage to the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a> over the years and gravitated towards Stormheim.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion</a> of <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>, Odyn took interest in the arrival of mortal forces to battle the <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a>, observing their initial <a href="/wiki/Battle_for_the_Broken_Shore" class="mw-redirect" title="Battle for the Broken Shore">setback</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Shore" title="Broken Shore">Broken Shore</a>. Later, he would pick a specific mortal <a href="/wiki/Warrior" title="Warrior">warrior</a> to act as his new champion, granting them overall command of the Valarjar themselves.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Odyn&#95;and&#95;the&#95;Valarjar&#95;18-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Odyn_and_the_Valarjar-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> This warrior, who became known as the Battlelord, was saved from death while fighting the Legion, and though taken to the Halls of Valor at Odyn's behest, was freely able to leave the complex at will.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:292px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Warswords_of_the_Valarjar.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Warswords_of_the_Valarjar.jpg/290px-Warswords_of_the_Valarjar.jpg?455143" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="290" height="259" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="560" data-file-height="500" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Warswords_of_the_Valarjar.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <a href="/wiki/Warswords_of_the_Valarjar" title="Warswords of the Valarjar">Warswords of the Valarjar</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>Eager to join in the war against the demonic invaders, Odyn used the Battlelord as his tool to direct the Valarjar. An area of the Halls known as <a href="/wiki/Skyhold" title="Skyhold">Skyhold</a> became an <a href="/wiki/Order_Halls" class="mw-redirect" title="Order Halls">order hall</a> for the many mortal warriors involved in the war, and the resources of the Valarjar were once more used against the enemies of Azeroth. The All-Father also helped the Battlelord in acquiring one of the most powerful <a href="/wiki/Artifact" title="Artifact">weapons</a> in existence to better fight the Legion, including the <a href="/wiki/Warswords_of_the_Valarjar" title="Warswords of the Valarjar">Warswords of the Valarjar</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Scale_of_the_Earth-Warder" title="Scale of the Earth-Warder">Scale of the Earth-Warder</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Strom%27kar,_the_Warbreaker" title="Strom&#39;kar, the Warbreaker">Strom'kar, the Warbreaker</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Using the <a href="/wiki/Scouting_Map" title="Scouting Map">Eye of Odyn</a> to see events across the Broken Isles and beyond, the Valarjar waged a limited war against the Legion, striking in key areas. At the same time, the Battlelord continued their own campaign.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In <a href="/wiki/Stormheim" title="Stormheim">Stormheim</a>, the Valarjar vrykul were found at <a href="/wiki/Valdisdall" title="Valdisdall">Valdisdall</a>, their headquarters for much of their operations against the Legion and the <a href="/wiki/Helarjar" title="Helarjar">Helarjar</a>.
</p><p>Though Odyn influenced events in <a href="/wiki/Stormheim" title="Stormheim">Stormheim</a> when it was attacked by the <a href="/wiki/Felskorn" class="mw-redirect" title="Felskorn">Felskorn</a> and demons, the Valarjar were unable to play an active role. However, mortal <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">heroes</a> would eventually travel into the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a> itself to pursue <a href="/wiki/God-King_Skovald" title="God-King Skovald">God-King Skovald</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Aegis_of_Aggramar" title="Aegis of Aggramar">Aegis of Aggramar</a>. There, Odyn determined to test these champions and set the Valarjar and other denizens of the Halls to besting them. The mortals eventually succeeded in facing down all the challenges the Valarjar could pose, confronting and defeating both the God-King and Odyn himself, and thus securing the Aegis.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Odyn would discover that Helya had developed a way of turning even the souls of Stormforged Valarjar into her twisted <a href="/wiki/Kvaldir" title="Kvaldir">kvaldir</a>. Outraged, he would rally a force of <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">champions</a> and, having tested them himself personally, he sent them into <a href="/wiki/Helheim" title="Helheim">Helheim</a> with the goal of vanquishing Helya. They would battle through waves of the Sea-Witch's minions before confronting her. After a desperate fight, they were successful in killing Helya, and in doing so freed Odyn and the Valarjar from their millennia-long containment.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After finally slaying Helya, the Valarjar recovered her <a href="/wiki/Helya%27s_Horn" title="Helya&#39;s Horn">horn</a>, and though unredeemable, Odyn had the Battlelord use it to put the kvaldir to good use against key Legion targets around the Broken Isles.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Helya&#39;s&#95;Horn&#95;24-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Helya&#39;s_Horn-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Warrior_Campaign">Warrior Campaign</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Warrior Campaign"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Valarjar_Campaign" title="Valarjar Campaign">Valarjar Campaign</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Valarjar.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Valarjar.jpg/320px-Valarjar.jpg?eb8197" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="181" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1360" data-file-height="768" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Valarjar.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Odyn and his Valarjar in the <a href="/wiki/Hall_of_Glory" title="Hall of Glory">Hall of Glory</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>Over time, more preparations were made for the coming war. The Battlelord recruited <a href="/wiki/Captain_Hjalmar_Stahlstrom" title="Captain Hjalmar Stahlstrom">Captain Hjalmar Stahlstrom</a> to oversee the training of new <a href="/wiki/Valarjar_Aspirants" title="Valarjar Aspirants">Aspirants</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Captain&#95;25-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Captain-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while <a href="/wiki/Einar_the_Runecaster" title="Einar the Runecaster">Einar the Runecaster</a> was brought on board to expand the powers of Skyhold.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Troops&#95;in&#95;the&#95;Field&#95;26-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Troops_in_the_Field-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With these advancements, the Valarjar became significantly more disciplined and organized, taking on the form of a truly formidable army.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>To further strengthen their forces, a plan was made to unite the <a href="/wiki/Titan-forged" title="Titan-forged">titan-forged</a> of <a href="/wiki/Ulduar" title="Ulduar">Ulduar</a> using the <a href="/wiki/Gjallarhorn" class="mw-redirect" title="Gjallarhorn">Gjallarhorn</a>, which the <a href="/wiki/Keeper" title="Keeper">Keepers</a> were oathbound to answer its call, as it would signal that the end times were upon <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With the aid of <a href="/wiki/Hymdall" title="Hymdall">Hymdall</a>, the Battlelord marched on <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Summit" title="Highmountain Summit">Highmountain Summit</a> seeking the great worm <a href="/wiki/Jorhuttam" title="Jorhuttam">Jorhuttam</a>, and would ultimately vanquish it in a fierce battle, claiming the artifact from its corpse.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Valarjar later gained the aid of the <a href="/wiki/Valkyra" title="Valkyra">Valkyra</a> in the form of <a href="/wiki/Savyn_Valorborn" title="Savyn Valorborn">Savyn Valorborn</a>, who rallied the most worthy <a href="/wiki/Shieldmaiden" title="Shieldmaiden">shieldmaidens</a> in Odyn's name.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Recruiting&#95;30-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Recruiting-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point, the Valkyra were under attack by <a href="/wiki/Runemaster_Skomjorn" title="Runemaster Skomjorn">Runemaster Skomjorn</a> and his <a href="/wiki/Felskorn" class="mw-redirect" title="Felskorn">Felskorn</a> vrykul, until they received help from the Valarjar to crush those who've betrayed Odyn.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Meanwhile, the Valarjar saved a group of mighty warriors loyal to Odyn who had been captured and forced by <a href="/wiki/Sea_giant" title="Sea giant">sea giants</a> to fight in their underground pit in <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When they learned that powerful armaments were lost when the great ship <a href="/wiki/Vaesska" title="Vaesska">Vaesska</a> sank at sea in <a href="/wiki/Vashj%27ir" title="Vashj&#39;ir">Vashj'ir</a>, the Battlelord sent his troops to retrieve them, fighting <a href="/wiki/Wavemistress_S%27revess" title="Wavemistress S&#39;revess">Wavemistress S'revess</a> and her <a href="/wiki/Naga" title="Naga">naga</a> in the process.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At the same time, Odyn turned his attention once more to <a href="/wiki/Helya" title="Helya">Helya</a> and her <a href="/wiki/Helarjar" title="Helarjar">Helarjar</a>, dispatching the Battlelord into the <a href="/wiki/Maw_of_Souls" title="Maw of Souls">Maw of Souls</a> to deliver a decisive victory against the Sea-Witch. Though she would survive the battle, Helya suffered a significant defeat at the hands of the Valarjar, leaving them enough room to fight off the Legion first.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the assault, the Battlelord managed to struck down <a href="/wiki/Ymiron" class="mw-redirect" title="Ymiron">Ymiron</a> and convinced the All-Father that forcing the fallen vrykul king into his service for the rest of time would be a perfectly suited punishment for his crimes.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When they finally used the Gjallarhorn, the Valarjar discovered that Ulduar had been overrun by the Legion. The Battlelord departed immediately with Valarjar in tow for the titanic fortress and cut their way through the demonic invaders. Both <a href="/wiki/Thorim" title="Thorim">Thorim</a> and <a href="/wiki/Hodir" title="Hodir">Hodir</a> were eventually rescued, though the latter ended up getting trapped through one of the invaders' portals to the world of <a href="/wiki/Niskara" title="Niskara">Niskara</a>. After Thorim pledged himself and his titan-forged to the Valarjar, they devised a plan to save Hodir.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Ulduar&#39;s&#95;Oath&#95;6-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Ulduar&#39;s_Oath-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After an attack on <a href="/wiki/Felblaze_Ingress" title="Felblaze Ingress">Felblaze Ingress</a>, Hymdall opened a portal to let their forces invade the demonic world.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the course of the battle, Thorim and the Battlelord located Hodir and slew his captor <a href="/wiki/Lady_Ran%27zara" title="Lady Ran&#39;zara">Lady Ran'zara</a>, freeing him from the <a href="/wiki/Fel" title="Fel">fel</a> corruption.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Having achieved victory on Niskara, Hodir pledged himself to the Valarjar's cause,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while Odyn rewarded his Battlelord with more power for their <a href="/wiki/Artifact" title="Artifact">artifact</a> weapon.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Battle_for_the_Exodar" title="Battle for the Exodar">Battle for the <i>Exodar</i></a>, <a href="/wiki/Finna_Bjornsdottir" title="Finna Bjornsdottir">Finna Bjornsdottir</a> and <a href="/wiki/Ragnvald_Drakeborn" title="Ragnvald Drakeborn">Ragnvald Drakeborn</a> helped the Battlelord in repelling <a href="/wiki/High_General_Rakeesh" title="High General Rakeesh">High General Rakeesh</a> and the Legion forces out of the <a href="/wiki/Draenei" title="Draenei">draenei</a> capital.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h4><span class="mw-headline" id="Legionfall">Legionfall</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Legionfall"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h4>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Valarjar_Deliverence_Point.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Valarjar_Deliverence_Point.jpg/320px-Valarjar_Deliverence_Point.jpg?288f60" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="233" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1049" data-file-height="764" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Valarjar_Deliverence_Point.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The Valarjar forces on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Shore" title="Broken Shore">Broken Shore</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>After <a href="/wiki/Gul%27dan_(alternate_universe)" title="Gul&#39;dan (alternate universe)">Gul'dan</a> was defeated in the <a href="/wiki/Nighthold" title="Nighthold">Nighthold</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Pillars_of_Creation" title="Pillars of Creation">Pillars of Creation</a> were reunited to stop the <a href="/wiki/Felstorm" title="Felstorm">Felstorm</a>, Odyn (as Havi) attended a meeting in <a href="/wiki/Dalaran" title="Dalaran">Dalaran</a> to represent the Valarjar with the other orders, where they planned their next move. All would watch as the <a href="/wiki/Demon_lord" title="Demon lord">demon lord</a> <a href="/wiki/Kil%27jaeden" title="Kil&#39;jaeden">Kil'jaeden</a> launched his new attack from the <a href="/wiki/Tomb_of_Sargeras" title="Tomb of Sargeras">Tomb of Sargeras</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-41" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-41"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>41<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> A new faction was subsequently formed, known as the <a href="/wiki/Armies_of_Legionfall" title="Armies of Legionfall">Armies of Legionfall</a>, in which all orders pooled their forces and waged war on the Broken Shore, based from <a href="/wiki/Deliverance_Point" title="Deliverance Point">Deliverance Point</a>. The Valarjar themselves would also establish an outpost at <a href="/wiki/Aalgen_Point" title="Aalgen Point">Aalgen Point</a>, closer to the Tomb of Sargeras. Odyn and the Valarjar personally dealt with a remnant <a href="/wiki/Felskorn" class="mw-redirect" title="Felskorn">Felskorn</a> presence at <a href="/wiki/Felrage_Strand" title="Felrage Strand">Felrage Strand</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-42" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-42"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>42<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Assault_on_Stormheim" title="Assault on Stormheim">Assault on Stormheim</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Gates_of_Valor" title="Gates of Valor">Gates of Valor</a> were targeted by <a href="/wiki/Lord_Commander_Alexius" title="Lord Commander Alexius">Lord Commander Alexius</a> following a successful feint at <a href="/wiki/Shield%27s_Rest" title="Shield&#39;s Rest">Shield's Rest</a>. The <a href="/wiki/Man%27ari" class="mw-redirect" title="Man&#39;ari">Man'ari</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Legion_Command_Ship" title="Legion Command Ship">command ship</a> heavily bombarded the gates, as mortal champions fought on to board the vessel and desperately drive back the attack. <h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Organization">Organization</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=10" title="Edit section: Organization"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Arena_of_Glory.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Arena_of_Glory.jpg/320px-Arena_of_Glory.jpg?e8a3aa" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="181" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1360" data-file-height="768" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Arena_of_Glory.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <a href="/wiki/Arena_of_Glory" title="Arena of Glory">Arena of Glory</a> in Skyhold.</div></div></div>
<p>The <a href="/wiki/Skyhold" title="Skyhold">Skyhold</a>, a portion of the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a> located high up in the clouds above <a href="/wiki/Stormheim" title="Stormheim">Stormheim</a>, serves as the headquarters of the Valarjar since the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion</a> of <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>. Among its wings, the <a href="/wiki/Forge_of_Odyn" title="Forge of Odyn">Forge of Odyn</a> is where the mightiest weapons of the valarjar are crafted by <a href="/wiki/Master_Smith_Helgar" title="Master Smith Helgar">Master Smith Helgar</a>, the greatest smith in the Halls,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Odyn&#95;and&#95;the&#95;Valarjar&#95;18-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Odyn_and_the_Valarjar-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> who had been operating for longer than many races have existed.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-54" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-54"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>54<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The <a href="/wiki/Arena_of_Glory" title="Arena of Glory">Arena of Glory</a> is a combat ring where warriors vie for supremacy in epic combat and test their skills against one another, presided over by the <a href="/wiki/Val%27kyr" title="Val&#39;kyr">Val'kyr</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Odyn&#95;and&#95;the&#95;Valarjar&#95;18-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Odyn_and_the_Valarjar-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Finally, the <a href="/wiki/Great_Mead_Hall" title="Great Mead Hall">Great Mead Hall</a> is the legendary home of the Valarjar, it is a placed used as a living quarters for their armies, where important ceremonies are often conducted, as well as the seat of power for <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a> the All-Father himself.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Odyn&#95;and&#95;the&#95;Valarjar&#95;18-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Odyn_and_the_Valarjar-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When asked what the Valarjar meant to them, the Battlelord replied among the following:
</p>
<ul><li>Strength - Skyhold is more than just the seat of Odyn, or the feasting hall of the honored dead. It is the home of the Valarjar, the warriors of the storm. It is where they tend to their fury and gather their strength, waiting for the call to strike down the enemies of Azeroth from above.</li>
<li>Glory - Skyhold is a place of learning, where the lessons of the glorious dead can be taught to others, skills can be honed, and weaponry mastered. When the Valarjar answer the call to battle, they do not see enemies-- only more glorious dead to join their ranks and share their deeds in Odyn's Hall.</li>
<li>Leadership - The feasting hall of the Valarjar is where those warriors who gave everything can remember what they died protecting. They draw our strength in remembering what they fight for, until they once again answer the call to lead the living in defense of that which is worth dying for.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-55" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-55"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>55<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Leadership">Leadership</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=11" title="Edit section: Leadership"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Valarjar is under the leadership of both the <a href="/wiki/Keeper" title="Keeper">Keeper</a> <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Battlelord</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Warrior" title="Warrior">warrior</a> <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">champion</a> chosen by the All-Father himself, who was then unable to leave the <a href="/wiki/Halls_of_Valor" title="Halls of Valor">Halls of Valor</a>. Once they proved their worth and ability to lead, they were named the Battlelord and put in command of one of the greatest armies in existence.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-56" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-56"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>56<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes_and_trivia">Notes and trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=18" title="Edit section: Notes and trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The <a href="/wiki/Ymirjar" title="Ymirjar">Ymirjar</a>, chosen ascendant vrykul of the <a href="/wiki/Lich_King" title="Lich King">Lich King</a>, are very similar to the Valarjar, implying that the Lich King created a darker version of the vrykul religion to suit his own ends.</li>
<li>The title of the order leader was originally going to be "Skylord". It was changed to "Battlelord" during alpha.</li>
<li>During development, their placeholder reputation faction name was <b>Vry'kul of Stormheim</b>. An instance of them being referred to as such was left behind.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-67" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-67"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>67<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li><a href="/wiki/Cairne_Bloodhoof" title="Cairne Bloodhoof">Cairne Bloodhoof</a> might have been originally intended to be a Valarjar's sub-leader, according to <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Inv_misc_groupneedmore.png/16px-Inv_misc_groupneedmore.png?ab3389" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/New_Recruit_(warrior)" title="New Recruit (warrior)"><span class="itemlink ajaxttlink qc-common" title="New Recruit is a common quality item."><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>New Recruit<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>, which had similar variants given by the other class halls' sub-leaders.</li></ul>
