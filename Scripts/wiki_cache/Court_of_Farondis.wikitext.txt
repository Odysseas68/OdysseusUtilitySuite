The '''Court of Farondis''' are a [[Organization|group]] of [[Night elf|night elven]] [[ghost]]s cursed by the destruction of the [[:Tidestone of Golganneth]] in [[Azsuna]].

They are one of the factions [[Adventurer|heroes]] can acquire reputation for in order to purchase certain toys and gear in ''[[World of Warcraft: Legion|Legion]]''.

==Background==
{{Stub/Lore-section}} <!-- +summary of their Legion questlines -->
===War of the Ancients===
When [[Prince Farondis]] turned against [[Queen Azshara]] during the [[War of the Ancients]], he tried to use the [[:Tidestone of Golganneth]] to destroy the [[Well of Eternity]] in [[Zin-Azshari]]. Doing so would close the [[Burning Legion]]'s portal and stop the invasion. Unfortunately, there was a loyal noble to Azshara in his court, [[Vandros]], who gave word to her. In a frightening display of [[arcane]] power Azshara remotely destroyed the Tidestone, releasing a wave of dark energy over all of [[Azsuna]]<ref>[[Ancient Highborne Tome]]</ref> that shattered the region and cursed its citizens to live on as [[ghost]]s.

===Legion===
{{Legion-section}}
In time, the Court have come to hate [[Prince Farondis]] for incurring the wrath of [[Queen Azshara]]. By the time of the [[third invasion of the Burning Legion]] in 33 [[ADP]], however, upon learning that the [[naga]] invasion led by [[Tidemistress Athissa]] was sent by Queen Azshara herself, they forgave him for trying to do the very thing that he did before a second time. The Court, aided by the [[adventurer]]s, fought against the [[Hatecoil]] naga and led the defense of Azsuna during the Legion's [[Assault on Azsuna|assault]].

[[Magister Garuhod]] eventually managed to break free of the curse through a special brew. After this, the members of the Court continued trying to replicate his methods in the hopes of finding a cure for themselves.<ref>[[A Cleansing Cocktail]]</ref><ref>[[Mixology Mix-up]]</ref>

===Battle for Azeroth===
{{Bfa-section}}
Around the time of the [[Fourth War]] in 33 ADP, several [[Nar'thalas Mage|Nar'thalas magi]] traveled to [[Stormwind City]] to provid portal services to the [[Crumbled Palace]] in the [[Wizard's Sanctum]]. Stormwind guards refer to them as their allies.

===Exploring Azeroth===
{{Novel-section}}
In Year 42 ADP, [[Thalyssra]] reported that the ghosts were happier with Farondis redeemed. Several [[nightborne]] [[scholar]]s began working on finding a way to break the curse laid by Azshara.<ref>''[[Exploring Azeroth: Islands and Isles]]'', pg. 18</ref>
