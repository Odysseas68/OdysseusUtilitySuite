<p>The <b>Vault of the Wardens</b> is the <a href="/wiki/Warden" title="Warden">Warden</a> maximum security facility holding among the greatest threats to <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>, which once included the body of <a href="/wiki/Illidan_Stormrage" title="Illidan Stormrage">Illidan Stormrage</a>, his <a href="/wiki/Illidari" title="Illidari">Illidari</a> <a href="/wiki/Demon_hunter" title="Demon hunter">demon hunters</a>, and other terrifying monsters.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The door to the Vault is opened by the <a href="/wiki/Darnassian" title="Darnassian">Darnassian</a> phrase "Anu'dorini Talah",<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> which translates to "Let my will be known."<sup id="cite&#95;ref-loretweet1&#95;4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-loretweet1-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Vault is replete with magical safeguards,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Startup&#95;5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Startup-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and <a href="/wiki/Containment_Crystal" title="Containment Crystal">powerful containment crystals</a> are used to encase each prisoner in their cell.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Aside from the demon hunters once imprisoned in the <a href="/wiki/Illidari_Ward" title="Illidari Ward">Illidari Ward</a>, the most powerful prisoners of the Vault are sealed in the vaults of the <a href="/wiki/Demon_Ward" title="Demon Ward">Demon Ward</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Being posted there was once considered prestigious.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Adventure_Guide">Adventure Guide</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Adventure Guide"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><i>This secret Warden facility was carved into the mountainside deep in <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>, and sealed with magical wards both to keep intruders out, and to ensure that the terrors locked within could never escape. Many of the most dangerous foes the Wardens have encountered was locked away within the Vault, but in the aftermath of Cordana's betrayal and the Legion's attack, those creatures now roam the halls freely.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Background"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="After_the_Sundering">After the Sundering</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: After the Sundering"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Vault_of_the_Wardens.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Vault_of_the_Wardens.jpg/320px-Vault_of_the_Wardens.jpg?d162a4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="199" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1554" data-file-height="967" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Vault_of_the_Wardens.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The Vault of the Wardens in <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</div></div></div>
<p>Ten thousand years ago, right after the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ancients" title="War of the Ancients">War of the Ancients</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the secret Vault of the Wardens facility was carved into the mountainside deep on the <a href="/wiki/Isle_of_the_Watchers" title="Isle of the Watchers">Isle of the Watchers</a> in <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>, and sealed with magical wards both to keep intruders out, and to ensure that the terrors locked within could never escape. Over the following millennia, most of the most dangerous foes the <a href="/wiki/Watchers" title="Watchers">Watchers</a> have encountered was locked away within the Vault.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Among their prisoners, <a href="/wiki/Ash%27Golm" title="Ash&#39;Golm">Ash'Golm</a> was one of <a href="/wiki/Ragnaros" title="Ragnaros">Ragnaros</a>' lieutenants who burned a violent scar across the forests of <a href="/wiki/Kalimdor" title="Kalimdor">Kalimdor</a> before the <a href="/wiki/Warden" title="Warden">Wardens</a> finally managed to bind him. Unable to fully destroy him, they locked him in a prison of ice.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When the Wardens discovered that the <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demonic</a> <a href="/wiki/Beholder" title="Beholder">beholder</a>'s magical emissions could be reflected back to their source, to debilitating effect, they used this knowledge to ensnare <a href="/wiki/Glazer" title="Glazer">Glazer</a>, and crafted a special prison of mirrors to contain its power.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>At some point, the <a href="/wiki/Overfiend" title="Overfiend">overfiend</a> <a href="/wiki/Immolanth" title="Immolanth">Immolanth</a> led a <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Legion</a> army that conquered and eventually ruled a distant nexus world, until the Wardens somehow managed to imprison him in the <a href="/wiki/Vault_of_Law" title="Vault of Law">Vault of Law</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Fall_of_the_Betrayer">Fall of the Betrayer</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Fall of the Betrayer"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>Following <a href="/wiki/Illidan_Stormrage" title="Illidan Stormrage">Illidan Stormrage</a>'s defeat at the <a href="/wiki/Black_Temple" title="Black Temple">Black Temple</a> in 27 <a href="/wiki/ADP" title="ADP">ADP</a>, <a href="/wiki/Maiev_Shadowsong" title="Maiev Shadowsong">Maiev Shadowsong</a> returned to the summit right after, now accompanied by several wardens, to encase the Betrayer's corpse in the Vault of the Wardens so that his dark, lingering soul could suffer the rest of eternal sentence, as justice to be forever served. She also imprisoned his surviving <a href="/wiki/Illidari" title="Illidari">Illidari</a>, vowing never to allow the <a href="/wiki/Fel" title="Fel">fel</a>-touched <a href="/wiki/Demon_hunter" title="Demon hunter">demon hunters</a> to roam free ever again,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and secured the <a href="/wiki/Sargerite_Keystone" title="Sargerite Keystone">Sargerite Keystone</a> within the Vault.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="The_Tomb_of_Sargeras">The Tomb of Sargeras</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: The Tomb of Sargeras"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels"><img alt="WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png" src="/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="62" height="16" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="62" data-file-height="16" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to the <i>Warcraft</i> <a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels">novels</a>, <a href="/wiki/Novellas" title="Novellas">novellas</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Short_stories" title="Short stories">short stories</a>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>Anticipating that <a href="/wiki/Gul%27dan_(alternate_universe)" title="Gul&#39;dan (alternate universe)">Gul'dan</a> would come for <a href="/wiki/Illidan_Stormrage" title="Illidan Stormrage">Illidan Stormrage</a>'s body, Maiev ordered her <a href="/wiki/Watchers" title="Watchers">Watchers</a> to rally at the <a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Betrayer" title="Vault of the Betrayer">Vault of the Betrayer</a>, ready to relocate all that is inside. After the <a href="/wiki/Orc" title="Orc">orc</a> <a href="/wiki/Warlock" title="Warlock">warlock</a> managed to open the way for the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Tomb_of_Sargeras" title="Tomb of Sargeras">Tomb of Sargeras</a>, Maiev veered north to fortify the Vault of the Wardens, while <a href="/wiki/Archmage" title="Archmage">Archmage</a> <a href="/wiki/Khadgar" title="Khadgar">Khadgar</a> attempted to draw the <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a> away to buy her time.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legion">Legion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Legion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:DemonWard1.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/DemonWard1.jpg/320px-DemonWard1.jpg?f65784" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="180" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1600" data-file-height="900" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:DemonWard1.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <a href="/wiki/Demon_Ward" title="Demon Ward">Demon Ward</a> invaded by the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>At the outset of the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion of the Burning Legion</a> in 32 <a href="/wiki/ADP" title="ADP">ADP</a>, <a href="/wiki/Gul%27dan_(alternate_universe)" title="Gul&#39;dan (alternate universe)">Gul'dan</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> targeted the Vault with the aid of the traitor <a href="/wiki/Cordana_Felsong" title="Cordana Felsong">Cordana Felsong</a>. With no other choice, Maiev freed the <a href="/wiki/Illidari" title="Illidari">Illidari</a> <a href="/wiki/Demon_hunter" title="Demon hunter">demon hunters</a> who had languished in her prison for years. Together, they fought off the <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a> led by <a href="/wiki/Bastillax" title="Bastillax">Bastillax</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> from the prison complex, but failed to prevent Gul'dan and Cordana from retrieving the corpse of <a href="/wiki/Illidan_Stormrage" title="Illidan Stormrage">Illidan Stormrage</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the assault, the Legion killed most of the wardens,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and their leader Maiev Shadowsong was imprisoned in <a href="/wiki/Black_Rook_Hold" title="Black Rook Hold">Black Rook Hold</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In the aftermath, the survivors were determined to rebuild their prison and hunt down the terrifying beasts that were let loose when it fell.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Since their ranks have been decimated by the Legion,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Grim&#95;23-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grim-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the <a href="/wiki/Watchers" title="Watchers">Watchers</a> set up <a href="/wiki/Watchers%27_Aerie" title="Watchers&#39; Aerie">Watchers' Aerie</a> and <a href="/wiki/Wardens%27_Redoubt" title="Wardens&#39; Redoubt">Wardens' Redoubt</a> outside the prison, and then tasked <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a> to hunt down prisoners who have escaped across the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> In spite of their shared hatred, the Illidari also agreed to cooperate with the Wardens, tracking down some of the more powerful creatures that escaped from the Vault, in exchange for information to locate the keystone.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Inquisitor_Tormentorum" title="Inquisitor Tormentorum">Inquisitor Tormentorum</a> arrived in the aftermath of the initial assault, drifting through the Vault's halls, with his gaze piercing the shadows in search of some vital secret.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When the <a href="/wiki/Class_order" class="mw-redirect" title="Class order">class order</a> leaders entered the Vault,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Sira_Moonwarden" title="Sira Moonwarden">Sira Moonwarden</a> tasked them to recover the <a href="/wiki/Moonblade" title="Moonblade">moonglaives</a> of their fallen sisters, as they needed all the arms they could gather.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Grim&#95;23-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Grim-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She also asked them to complete the start-up sequence to restore power in the <a href="/wiki/Vault_of_Mirrors" title="Vault of Mirrors">Vault of Mirrors</a>, in order to keep the prison complex's containment functioning until they could retake it.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Startup&#95;5-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Startup-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Dark Warden Cordana was eventually slain in the <a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Betrayer" title="Vault of the Betrayer">Vault of the Betrayer</a> to retrieve <a href="/wiki/Sargerite_Keystone" title="Sargerite Keystone">Sargerite Keystone</a> from her corpse.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Following her death, the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Deathlord</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Knights_of_the_Ebon_Blade" title="Knights of the Ebon Blade">Knights of the Ebon Blade</a> came here to claim her <a href="/wiki/Eye_of_the_Warden" title="Eye of the Warden">eye</a> and use several prisoners' parts to help create <a href="/wiki/Rottgut" title="Rottgut">Rottgut</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> while the First of the <a href="/wiki/Black_Harvest" class="mw-redirect" title="Black Harvest">Black Harvest</a> was sent by <a href="/wiki/Kira_Iresoul" title="Kira Iresoul">Kira Iresoul</a> to collect <a href="/wiki/Felsworn_Heart" title="Felsworn Heart">Cordana's heart</a> to empower the <a href="/wiki/Bloodstone_(quest_item)" title="Bloodstone (quest item)">Bloodstone</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Exploring_Azeroth">Exploring Azeroth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens?action=edit&amp;section=7" title="Edit section: Exploring Azeroth"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels"><img alt="WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png" src="/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="62" height="16" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="62" data-file-height="16" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to the <i>Warcraft</i> <a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels">novels</a>, <a href="/wiki/Novellas" title="Novellas">novellas</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Short_stories" title="Short stories">short stories</a>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>By the Year 42 <a href="/wiki/ADP" title="ADP">ADP</a>, the threats within the Vault of the Wardens had been contained by the Watchers, and the wards were holding at full power. During her travel across the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>, <a href="/wiki/Thalyssra" title="Thalyssra">First Arcanist Thalyssra</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Suramar_(kingdom)" title="Suramar (kingdom)">kingdom of Suramar</a> managed to convince the wardens that she had a reasonable interest in knowing what was happening in a place filled with such dangerous prisoners.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
