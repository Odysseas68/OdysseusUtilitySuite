<p><b>The Wardens</b> are <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elven</a> <a href="/wiki/Warden" title="Warden">wardens</a> loyal to <a href="/wiki/Maiev_Shadowsong" title="Maiev Shadowsong">Maiev Shadowsong</a> operating in the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>. They have worked from their base of operations, the <a href="/wiki/Isle_of_the_Watchers" title="Isle of the Watchers">Isle of the Watchers</a> in <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>, to hunt down and neutralize major threats to <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>'s inhabitants, through imprisonment or summary execution. Captured foes were interned in the <a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens" title="Vault of the Wardens">Vault of the Wardens</a>. 
</p><p>They reward the heroes who help them deal with dangerous threats on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>. The faction is known to possess <a href="/wiki/Warden_Towers" title="Warden Towers">Warden Towers</a> across the Broken Isles, although in recent times they have been forced to abandon many of them. There are two in most <i>Legion</i> zones that serve as PvP targets.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Wardens_(faction)?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Watchers" title="Watchers">Watchers</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<p>At the outset of the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion" class="mw-redirect" title="Third invasion">third invasion</a> of Azeroth by the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a> targeted the Vault and, with the aid of the traitor warden <a href="/wiki/Cordana_Felsong" title="Cordana Felsong">Cordana Felsong</a>, they succeeded in overrunning the prison complex and retrieving the corpse of <a href="/wiki/Illidan_Stormrage" title="Illidan Stormrage">Illidan Stormrage</a>. <a href="/wiki/Maiev_Shadowsong" title="Maiev Shadowsong">Maiev Shadowsong</a> was captured during the conflict and taken to <a href="/wiki/Black_Rook_Hold" title="Black Rook Hold">Black Rook Hold</a>. The Wardens worked with the <a href="/wiki/Illidari" title="Illidari">Illidari</a> to fight back the invading demons.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Working&#95;With&#95;the&#95;Wardens&#95;2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Working_With_the_Wardens-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>In Shadowsong's absence, <span class="raceicon"></span><a href="/wiki/Sira_Moonwarden" title="Sira Moonwarden"><span style="color:">Sira Moonwarden</span></a> took up the mantle of leadership.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-DealWithIt&#95;1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DealWithIt-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Wardens continued their duties of upholding justice and safeguarding the world's inhabitants, directing <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">order leaders</a> to eliminate various threats on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>. It is implied that the vast majority of the Wardens' ranks have been decimated by recent events,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> making it necessary for them to <a href="/wiki/Warden_Trainee" title="Warden Trainee">train new recruits</a>, and to directly ask <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a> to hunt down prisoners who have escaped from the <a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens" title="Vault of the Wardens">Vault of the Wardens</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Following the creation of the <a href="/wiki/Armies_of_Legionfall" title="Armies of Legionfall">Armies of Legionfall</a>, members of the Wardens, including the now freed Maiev and several <a href="/wiki/Warden_Vigilant" title="Warden Vigilant">Warden Vigilants</a>, participated in the <a href="/wiki/Assault_on_Broken_Shore" title="Assault on Broken Shore">Assault on Broken Shore</a>.
</p>
