'''Stormforged Valarjar''' are [[vrykul]] who have been transformed for their valor and heroism by the [[keeper]] [[Odyn]], granting them new metallic forms and increased strength. These [[warrior]]s make [[Skyhold]] their home, and are loyal to Odyn himself, and his chosen [[Adventurer|Battlelord]].

Apart from celebrating in the [[Great Mead Hall]] in Skyhold, the Stormforged undertake missions in Odyn's name. With the [[Third invasion of the Burning Legion|third invasion]] of [[Azeroth]] by the [[Burning Legion]], the Odyn has committed the Valarjar to fighting the [[demon]]s alongside the other [[Class Order]]s. Since then, the Stormforged have taken part in numerous engagements, including a raid on [[Niskara]], the [[Assault on Broken Shore]], and the [[Argus Campaign]].

==History==
Only [[vrykul]] who display immense valor in battle are chosen by [[Odyn]] to become Stormforged. For millennia, these [[warrior]]s were trapped in [[Skyhold]] with Odyn himself, and have only regained their freedom with the [[Burning Legion]]'s [[Third invasion of the Burning Legion|third invasion]] of [[Azeroth]]. The actions of the [[Adventurer|Battlelord]] would secure the support of the [[Valarjar]] and the Stormforged in the subsequent war against the [[demon]]s.

When the [[keeper]] [[Hodir]] was captured by the Legion and taken to the prison world of [[Niskara]], the Valarjar would launch a massive assault on to liberate him. Many Stormforged would take part in this attack, which ultimately succeeded in freeing Hodir and returning him to Skyhold. When the [[Armies of Legionfall]] was founded to battle the Legion on the [[Broken Shore]], the Valarjar would send forces to the area to aid in the campaign (mainly based from [[Deliverance Point]]). 

The Stormforged also took a small part in the [[Argus Campaign]], with forces stationed on board the [[draenei]] ship known as the ''[[Vindicaar]]''.
