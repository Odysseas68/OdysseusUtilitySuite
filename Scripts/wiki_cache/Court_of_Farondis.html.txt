<p>The <b>Court of Farondis</b> are a <a href="/wiki/Organization" title="Organization">group</a> of <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elven</a> <a href="/wiki/Ghost" title="Ghost">ghosts</a> cursed by the destruction of the <a href="/wiki/Tidestone_of_Golganneth" title="Tidestone of Golganneth">Tidestone of Golganneth</a> in <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>.
</p><p>They are one of the factions <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">heroes</a> can acquire reputation for in order to purchase certain toys and gear in <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="War_of_the_Ancients">War of the Ancients</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Court_of_Farondis?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: War of the Ancients"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When <a href="/wiki/Prince_Farondis" title="Prince Farondis">Prince Farondis</a> turned against <a href="/wiki/Queen_Azshara" title="Queen Azshara">Queen Azshara</a> during the <a href="/wiki/War_of_the_Ancients" title="War of the Ancients">War of the Ancients</a>, he tried to use the <a href="/wiki/Tidestone_of_Golganneth" title="Tidestone of Golganneth">Tidestone of Golganneth</a> to destroy the <a href="/wiki/Well_of_Eternity" title="Well of Eternity">Well of Eternity</a> in <a href="/wiki/Zin-Azshari" title="Zin-Azshari">Zin-Azshari</a>. Doing so would close the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>'s portal and stop the invasion. Unfortunately, there was a loyal noble to Azshara in his court, <a href="/wiki/Vandros" class="mw-redirect" title="Vandros">Vandros</a>, who gave word to her. In a frightening display of <a href="/wiki/Arcane" title="Arcane">arcane</a> power Azshara remotely destroyed the Tidestone, releasing a wave of dark energy over all of <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> that shattered the region and cursed its citizens to live on as <a href="/wiki/Ghost" title="Ghost">ghosts</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legion">Legion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Court_of_Farondis?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Legion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>In time, the Court have come to hate <a href="/wiki/Prince_Farondis" title="Prince Farondis">Prince Farondis</a> for incurring the wrath of <a href="/wiki/Queen_Azshara" title="Queen Azshara">Queen Azshara</a>. By the time of the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion of the Burning Legion</a> in 33 <a href="/wiki/ADP" title="ADP">ADP</a>, however, upon learning that the <a href="/wiki/Naga" title="Naga">naga</a> invasion led by <a href="/wiki/Tidemistress_Athissa" class="mw-redirect" title="Tidemistress Athissa">Tidemistress Athissa</a> was sent by Queen Azshara herself, they forgave him for trying to do the very thing that he did before a second time. The Court, aided by the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a>, fought against the <a href="/wiki/Hatecoil" title="Hatecoil">Hatecoil</a> naga and led the defense of Azsuna during the Legion's <a href="/wiki/Assault_on_Azsuna" title="Assault on Azsuna">assault</a>.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Magister_Garuhod" title="Magister Garuhod">Magister Garuhod</a> eventually managed to break free of the curse through a special brew. After this, the members of the Court continued trying to replicate his methods in the hopes of finding a cure for themselves.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Battle_for_Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Court_of_Farondis?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Battle for Azeroth"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="Battle for Azeroth"><img alt="Battle for Azeroth" src="/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>Around the time of the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a> in 33 ADP, several <a href="/wiki/Nar%27thalas_Mage" title="Nar&#39;thalas Mage">Nar'thalas magi</a> traveled to <a href="/wiki/Stormwind_City" title="Stormwind City">Stormwind City</a> to provid portal services to the <a href="/wiki/Crumbled_Palace" title="Crumbled Palace">Crumbled Palace</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Wizard%27s_Sanctum" title="Wizard&#39;s Sanctum">Wizard's Sanctum</a>. Stormwind guards refer to them as their allies.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Exploring_Azeroth">Exploring Azeroth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Court_of_Farondis?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Exploring Azeroth"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels"><img alt="WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png" src="/images/WoW-novel-logo-16x62.png?6916a4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="62" height="16" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="62" data-file-height="16" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to the <i>Warcraft</i> <a href="/wiki/Novels" title="Novels">novels</a>, <a href="/wiki/Novellas" title="Novellas">novellas</a>, or <a href="/wiki/Short_stories" title="Short stories">short stories</a>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>In Year 42 ADP, <a href="/wiki/Thalyssra" title="Thalyssra">Thalyssra</a> reported that the ghosts were happier with Farondis redeemed. Several <a href="/wiki/Nightborne" title="Nightborne">nightborne</a> <a href="/wiki/Scholar" title="Scholar">scholars</a> began working on finding a way to break the curse laid by Azshara.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
