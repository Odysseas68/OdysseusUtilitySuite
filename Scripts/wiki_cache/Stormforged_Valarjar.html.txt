<p><b>Stormforged Valarjar</b> are <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a> who have been transformed for their valor and heroism by the <a href="/wiki/Keeper" title="Keeper">keeper</a> <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a>, granting them new metallic forms and increased strength. These <a href="/wiki/Warrior" title="Warrior">warriors</a> make <a href="/wiki/Skyhold" title="Skyhold">Skyhold</a> their home, and are loyal to Odyn himself, and his chosen <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Battlelord</a>.
</p><p>Apart from celebrating in the <a href="/wiki/Great_Mead_Hall" title="Great Mead Hall">Great Mead Hall</a> in Skyhold, the Stormforged undertake missions in Odyn's name. With the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion</a> of <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a> by the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>, the Odyn has committed the Valarjar to fighting the <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a> alongside the other <a href="/wiki/Class_Order" title="Class Order">Class Orders</a>. Since then, the Stormforged have taken part in numerous engagements, including a raid on <a href="/wiki/Niskara" title="Niskara">Niskara</a>, the <a href="/wiki/Assault_on_Broken_Shore" title="Assault on Broken Shore">Assault on Broken Shore</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Argus_Campaign" title="Argus Campaign">Argus Campaign</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Stormforged_Valarjar?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Only <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a> who display immense valor in battle are chosen by <a href="/wiki/Odyn" title="Odyn">Odyn</a> to become Stormforged. For millennia, these <a href="/wiki/Warrior" title="Warrior">warriors</a> were trapped in <a href="/wiki/Skyhold" title="Skyhold">Skyhold</a> with Odyn himself, and have only regained their freedom with the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion</a> of <a href="/wiki/Azeroth" title="Azeroth">Azeroth</a>. The actions of the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Battlelord</a> would secure the support of the <a href="/wiki/Valarjar" title="Valarjar">Valarjar</a> and the Stormforged in the subsequent war against the <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a>.
</p><p>When the <a href="/wiki/Keeper" title="Keeper">keeper</a> <a href="/wiki/Hodir" title="Hodir">Hodir</a> was captured by the Legion and taken to the prison world of <a href="/wiki/Niskara" title="Niskara">Niskara</a>, the Valarjar would launch a massive assault on to liberate him. Many Stormforged would take part in this attack, which ultimately succeeded in freeing Hodir and returning him to Skyhold. When the <a href="/wiki/Armies_of_Legionfall" title="Armies of Legionfall">Armies of Legionfall</a> was founded to battle the Legion on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Shore" title="Broken Shore">Broken Shore</a>, the Valarjar would send forces to the area to aid in the campaign (mainly based from <a href="/wiki/Deliverance_Point" title="Deliverance Point">Deliverance Point</a>). 
</p><p>The Stormforged also took a small part in the <a href="/wiki/Argus_Campaign" title="Argus Campaign">Argus Campaign</a>, with forces stationed on board the <a href="/wiki/Draenei" title="Draenei">draenei</a> ship known as the <i><a href="/wiki/Vindicaar" title="Vindicaar">Vindicaar</a></i>. 
</p>
