<p><b>The Honorbound</b> is a faction <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> based on the <a href="/wiki/The_Banshee%27s_Wail" class="mw-redirect" title="The Banshee&#39;s Wail">The <i>Banshee's Wail</i></a>, in the <a href="/wiki/Port_of_Zandalar" title="Port of Zandalar">Port of Zandalar</a>. Reputation is gained by completing quests of the <a href="/wiki/Horde_War_Campaign" title="Horde War Campaign">Horde War Campaign</a> quests, world quests on <a href="/wiki/Kul_Tiras" title="Kul Tiras">Kul Tiras</a> and <a href="/wiki/Zandalar" title="Zandalar">Zandalar</a>, dungeon world quests, in <a href="/wiki/Warfront" title="Warfront">warfront</a> zones, and during <a href="/wiki/Faction_Assault" class="mw-redirect" title="Faction Assault">Faction Assault</a> quests.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Faction_description">Faction description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Honorbound?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Faction description"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><i>A special delegation of the <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> army, focused on disrupting <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a> operations in <a href="/wiki/Kul_Tiras" title="Kul Tiras">Kul Tiras</a>.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Honorbound?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Background"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="Battle for Azeroth"><img alt="Battle for Azeroth" src="/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<div class="thumb tleft"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:The_Banshee%27s_Wail.png" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/The_Banshee%27s_Wail.png/320px-The_Banshee%27s_Wail.png?b6a674" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="209" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1581" data-file-height="1031" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:The_Banshee%27s_Wail.png" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The <i><a href="/wiki/Banshee%27s_Wail" title="Banshee&#39;s Wail">Banshee's Wail</a></i> docked in the <a href="/wiki/Port_of_Zandalar" title="Port of Zandalar">Port of Zandalar</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>At Nathanos's command, the <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">Speaker of the Horde</a> gathered <a href="/wiki/War_Resources" title="War Resources">resources</a> for the Honorbound in order to lead operations into <a href="/wiki/Kul_Tiras" title="Kul Tiras">Kul Tiras</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> At the Speaker's direction, the Honorbound carried out missions of subterfuge, reconnaissance, assassination, and other focused strikes against the <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Garona_Halforcen" title="Garona Halforcen">Garona Halforcen</a> recruited reinforcements for the war effort.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As apart of their mission, the Honorbound sought to established footholds within Kul Tiras, though the exact order of their formation is unknown.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> <a href="/wiki/Trade_Prince" class="mw-redirect" title="Trade Prince">Trade Prince</a> <a href="/wiki/Jastor_Gallywix" title="Jastor Gallywix">Gallywix</a>, <a href="/wiki/Eitrigg" title="Eitrigg">Eitrigg</a>, and the Speaker established the <a href="/wiki/Krazzlefrazz_Outpost" title="Krazzlefrazz Outpost">Krazzlefrazz Outpost</a> within the <a href="/wiki/Drustvar" title="Drustvar">Drustvar</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Meanwhile, <a href="/wiki/Shadow_Hunter_Ty%27jin" title="Shadow Hunter Ty&#39;jin">Shadow Hunter Ty'jin</a>, seeking a base within <a href="/wiki/Tiragarde_Sound" title="Tiragarde Sound">Tiragarde Sound</a>, struck a bargain with <a href="/wiki/Owings" title="Owings">First Mate Owings</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Fogsail_Freebooters" title="Fogsail Freebooters">Fogsail Freebooters</a>, in which she would allow the Horde the use of <a href="/wiki/Plunder_Harbor" title="Plunder Harbor">Plunder Harbor</a> in exchange for their aid with her coup against <a href="/wiki/Captain_Rhenik" title="Captain Rhenik">Captain Rhenik</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Thus when she launched her coup the Honorbound aided the mutineers, enabling the death of Rhenik, where soon after Plunder Harbor was officially made a foothold of the Horde.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After the Honorbound established and fortified <a href="/wiki/Warfang_Hold" title="Warfang Hold">Warfang Hold</a> in <a href="/wiki/Stormsong_Valley" title="Stormsong Valley">Stormsong Valley</a>, they came under attack by the Kul Tiran forces.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The attack forced them to the shore and sank their ships, leaving the Honorbound forces with nowhere to run and nowhere to hide. However, they were saved by the timely intervention of <a href="/wiki/High_Warlord_Cromush" title="High Warlord Cromush">High Warlord Cromush</a> and the Speaker of the Horde, who manned a cannon against the Kul Tirans on the coast which gave the troops some breathing room.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Speaker then united with <a href="/wiki/Rexxar" title="Rexxar">Rexxar</a> and <a href="/wiki/Centurion_Kaga_Warmstone" title="Centurion Kaga Warmstone">Centurion Kaga Warmstone</a> in putting out the flames within the hold and fighting against the Kul Tiran forces within, culminating in the death of <a href="/wiki/Captain_Ara" title="Captain Ara">Captain Ara</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With their enemies dead the Speaker sounded the <a href="/wiki/Warhorn_of_the_Hold?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Warhorn of the Hold (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Warhorn of the Hold</a>, which rallied the troops and allowed the Horde to secure Warfang Hold.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-13" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>With the footholds secured, <a href="/wiki/Nathanos_Blightcaller" title="Nathanos Blightcaller">Nathanos Blightcaller</a>, leader of the Honorbound forces, turned to combating the Alliance, while the Speaker directed them to claim <a href="/wiki/Swiftwind_Post" title="Swiftwind Post">Swiftwind Post</a>, <a href="/wiki/Wolf%27s_Den" title="Wolf&#39;s Den">Wolf's Den</a>, <a href="/wiki/Windfall_Cavern" title="Windfall Cavern">Windfall Cavern</a>, <a href="/wiki/Stonefist_Watch" title="Stonefist Watch">Stonefist Watch</a>, <a href="/wiki/Hillcrest_Pasture" title="Hillcrest Pasture">Hillcrest Pasture</a>, <a href="/wiki/Stonetusk_Watch" title="Stonetusk Watch">Stonetusk Watch</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Mudfisher_Cove" title="Mudfisher Cove">Mudfisher Cove</a> as Horde outposts.
</p><p>Seeking the corpse of <a href="/wiki/Marshal_M._Valentine" title="Marshal M. Valentine">Marshal M. Valentine</a>, Nathanos directed the Honorbound to sail towards the <a href="/wiki/Great_Sea" title="Great Sea">Great Sea</a>. As the Speaker searched the sea for Valentine's corpse, the Honorbound came under attack by the <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a>. However, Nathanos and his forces were able to beat back the Alliance and the Speaker returned to the <i><a href="/wiki/Banshee%27s_Wail" title="Banshee&#39;s Wail">Banshee's Wail</a></i> with the corpses of Valentine and <a href="/wiki/Derek_Proudmoore" title="Derek Proudmoore">Derek Proudmoore</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Derek's corpse was soon used as bait to lure away Kul Tiran forces from <a href="/wiki/Boralus" title="Boralus">Boralus</a> so that the Speaker and <a href="/wiki/Thomas_Zelling" title="Thomas Zelling">Thomas Zelling</a> could claim the <a href="/wiki/Abyssal_Scepter" title="Abyssal Scepter">Abyssal Scepter</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As they carried this mission, the Honorbound clashed with the Kul Tirans attacking Plunder Harbor. After the Abyssal Scepter was claimed by the Horde, Nathanos used it to destroy the Kul Tiran fleet, securing victory for the Honorbound.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Tides_of_Vengeance">Tides of Vengeance</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Honorbound?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Tides of Vengeance"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:A_Path_of_Blood.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/A_Path_of_Blood.jpg/320px-A_Path_of_Blood.jpg?fed740" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="164" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="2760" data-file-height="1417" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:A_Path_of_Blood.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The Honorbound and <a href="/wiki/Zandalari_army" title="Zandalari army">Zandalari army</a> fighting the <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a> forces at the <a href="/wiki/Blood_Gate" title="Blood Gate">Blood Gate</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>As part of the renewed war effort, the Honorbound launched an assault against the <a href="/wiki/Azerite" title="Azerite">Azerite</a> supply that was in the possession of Kul Tiras and the Alliance at <a href="/wiki/Norwington_Estate" title="Norwington Estate">Norwington Estate</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After claiming the Azerite, they withdrew from the manor.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> As the war against the Alliance <a href="/wiki/Faction_Assaults" title="Faction Assaults">intensified</a>, the Honorbound under the command of High Warlord Cromush defended <a href="/wiki/Faction_Assault_on_Vol%27dun" title="Faction Assault on Vol&#39;dun">Vol'dun</a>, <a href="/wiki/Faction_Assault_on_Nazmir" title="Faction Assault on Nazmir">Nazmir</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Faction_Assault_on_Zuldazar" title="Faction Assault on Zuldazar">Zuldazar</a> and struck out against Alliance forces within <a href="/wiki/Faction_Assault_on_Drustvar" title="Faction Assault on Drustvar">Drustvar</a>, <a href="/wiki/Faction_Assault_on_Tiragarde_Sound" title="Faction Assault on Tiragarde Sound">Tiragarde Sound</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Faction_Assault_on_Stormsong_Valley" title="Faction Assault on Stormsong Valley">Stormsong Valley</a>.
</p><p>After receiving word that the Alliance had mounted an attack through <a href="/wiki/Nazmir" title="Nazmir">Nazmir</a>, the Honorbound joined the <a href="/wiki/Zandalari_Empire" title="Zandalari Empire">Zandalari Empire</a> in battle against their enemies in the region. Following the death of <a href="/wiki/Blademaster_Telaamon" title="Blademaster Telaamon">Blademaster Telaamon</a> and the destruction of the Abyssal Scepter, the Honorbound discovered that Telaamon and his forces were a bait to lure them away from <a href="/wiki/Dazar%27alor" title="Dazar&#39;alor">Dazar'alor</a> in order to weaken the city's defenses.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Realizing that the city was in danger, the combined Zandalari and Horde army rushed back to defend the city.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>After being informed that the <i><a href="/wiki/The_Swift_Vengeance" title="The Swift Vengeance">the Swift Vengeance</a></i> had been taken by <a href="/wiki/Irontide_Raiders" title="Irontide Raiders">Irontide</a> <a href="/wiki/Pirate" title="Pirate">pirates</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Chandlery_Wharf" title="Chandlery Wharf">Chandlery Wharf</a>, Nathanos declared they would be sent to search the area for the stolen ship.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, in reality <a href="/wiki/Baine_Bloodhoof" title="Baine Bloodhoof">Baine Bloodhoof</a> and Thomas Zelling had rescued the raised Derek Proudmoore from being turned into a weapon for Sylvanas and had set up the pirates to hide their actions from her. Unbeknownst to them, Sylvanas had discovered their actions and called for the leaders of the Horde to gather at Warfang Hold, which Baine suspected was a show of force.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The Honorbound served as a guard for the subsequent meeting that ended with Zelling's death and Baine's arrest at Slyvanas's command.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-23" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-23"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>23<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Rise_of_Azshara">Rise of Azshara</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Honorbound?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Rise of Azshara"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The Honorbound were among the Horde forces that joined the fleet under the command of <a href="/wiki/Nathanos_Blightcaller" title="Nathanos Blightcaller">Nathanos Blightcaller</a>, who used the <a href="/wiki/Xal%27atath" title="Xal&#39;atath">black blade</a> to guide the Horde fleet and the pursuing Alliance fleet towards <a href="/wiki/Nazjatar" title="Nazjatar">Nazjatar</a> where both would be attacked by the naga under <a href="/wiki/Queen_Azshara" title="Queen Azshara">Queen Azshara</a>'s command after the seas opened.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-24" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-24"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>24<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> While <a href="/wiki/Captain_Holgresh" title="Captain Holgresh">Captain Holgresh</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Veteran_Crew" title="Veteran Crew">Veteran Crews</a> lost their lives as a result, others members of the Honorbound like <a href="/wiki/Captain_Dawnsail" title="Captain Dawnsail">Captain Dawnsail</a> and <a href="/wiki/Captain_Jalazko" title="Captain Jalazko">Captain Jalazko</a> were discovered alive.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> It is presumed that other possible survivors rallied to <a href="/wiki/Lor%27themar_Theron" title="Lor&#39;themar Theron">Lor'themar Theron</a>, <a href="/wiki/First_Arcanist_Thalyssra" class="mw-redirect" title="First Arcanist Thalyssra">First Arcanist Thalyssra</a>, and the <a href="/wiki/Unshackled" title="Unshackled">Unshackled</a> at <a href="/wiki/Newhome" title="Newhome">Newhome</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Aftermath">Aftermath</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Honorbound?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Aftermath"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>When the Horde split between <a href="/wiki/Varok_Saurfang" title="Varok Saurfang">Varok Saurfang</a> revolutionaries and the loyalists of Sylvanas Windrunner, Nathanos rallied to Sylvanas's side.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> However, the Honorbound as an organization stood out from the conflict, which ended with Saurfang's death and Sylvanas's abandonment of the Horde.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With Nathanos remaining <a href="/wiki/Banshee_loyalists" title="Banshee loyalists">loyal</a> to the Banshee Queen,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the Honorbound became leaderless.
</p><p>In the aftermath of <a href="/wiki/N%27Zoth" title="N&#39;Zoth">N'Zoth</a>'s death and the end of the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>, the Horde and Alliance entered into an armistice with one another.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The status of the Honorbound is unknown, with it possibly being reassigned or disbanded.
</p>
