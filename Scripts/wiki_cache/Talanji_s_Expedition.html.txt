<p><b>Talanji's Expedition</b> is an expedition of <a href="/wiki/Zandalari_troll" title="Zandalari troll">Zandalari trolls</a> from <a href="/wiki/Zuldazar" title="Zuldazar">Zuldazar</a>, backed by the <a href="/wiki/Darkspear_tribe" title="Darkspear tribe">Darkspear tribe</a>. It is personally led by <a href="/wiki/Talanji" title="Talanji">Talanji</a>, Princess of the <a href="/wiki/Zandalari_Empire" title="Zandalari Empire">Zandalari Empire</a>. The expedition will face off against the <a href="/wiki/Blood_troll" title="Blood troll">blood troll</a> forces.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Faction_description">Faction description</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Talanji%27s_Expedition?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Faction description"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><i>Princess Talanji and her allies fight to protect Zandalar from the onslaught of the Blood Trolls.</i>
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Talanji%27s_Expedition?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Talanji_and_her_allies_in_Nazmir.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Talanji_and_her_allies_in_Nazmir.jpg/320px-Talanji_and_her_allies_in_Nazmir.jpg?bef4fe" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="158" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1894" data-file-height="938" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Talanji_and_her_allies_in_Nazmir.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>The expedition and their allies before the assault on <a href="/wiki/Zul%27Nazman" title="Zul&#39;Nazman">Zul'Nazman</a></div></div></div>
<p>Even though her <a href="/wiki/King_Rastakhan" class="mw-redirect" title="King Rastakhan">father</a> dismissed the threat of the <a href="/wiki/Blood_troll" title="Blood troll">blood trolls</a>, Talanji sought to take the fight to them. Aided by her fellow Zandalari and backed by <a href="/wiki/Rokhan" title="Rokhan">Rokhan</a>, a <a href="/wiki/Shadow_hunter" title="Shadow hunter">shadow hunter</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Darkspear_tribe" title="Darkspear tribe">Darkspear tribe</a>, as well as a contingent of his scouts and warriors, Talanji's newly created expedition ventured into the swamps of <a href="/wiki/Nazmir" title="Nazmir">Nazmir</a>. At Rokhan's direction, <a href="/wiki/Zul%27jan_Ruins" title="Zul&#39;jan Ruins">Zul'jan Ruins</a> was converted into the expedition's main outpost.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After discovering that the blood trolls sought to unleash <a href="/wiki/Taloc" title="Taloc">Taloc</a> on Zuldazar, Talanji and Rokhan sought to enlist the aid of the loa to aid them in the battle against the blood trolls, unaware that <a href="/wiki/Torga" title="Torga">Torga</a> had been killed, <a href="/wiki/Krag%27wa" class="mw-redirect" title="Krag&#39;wa">Krag'wa</a> was under assault by the blood trolls, and <a href="/wiki/Hir%27eek" title="Hir&#39;eek">Hir'eek</a> corrupted into serving <a href="/wiki/G%27huun" title="G&#39;huun">G'huun</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>As they sought the aid of <a href="/wiki/Bwonsamdi" title="Bwonsamdi">Bwonsamdi</a>, <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a> would encounter his worshiper, <a href="/wiki/Hanzabu" title="Hanzabu">Hanzabu</a>, who brought them to the <a href="/wiki/Necropolis_(Nazmir)" title="Necropolis (Nazmir)">Necropolis</a> to meet with him. After tasking the adventurer with cleansing his temple of the <a href="/wiki/Ghost" title="Ghost">spirits</a> who had abandoned Bwonsamdi for G'huun, Bwonsamdi agreed to pledge his powers to the fight against the blood trolls in exchange for one million <a href="/wiki/Soul" title="Soul">souls</a>. The ritual to finalize their contract was interrupted by the blood trolls and their matriarch who were successfully fought off. For being attacked in his own temple, Bwonsamdi declared that the blood trolls would be mighty sorry.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Thus the expedition gained the loa of death as their ally. The expedition also recruited numerous <a href="/wiki/Tortollan" title="Tortollan">tortollans</a> to their cause after saving Torga's corpse from being corrupted by G'huun's <a href="/wiki/Herald" title="Herald">herald</a>, <a href="/wiki/Jungo" class="mw-redirect" title="Jungo">Jungo</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While Hir'eek was killed,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Krag'wa would be liberated from the blood troll assault, and the loa unleash his fury on them.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> With their new allies and Horde <a href="/wiki/Gob_Squad" title="Gob Squad">reinforcements</a>, Talanji's expedition launched an assault on the <a href="/wiki/Zul%27Nazman" title="Zul&#39;Nazman">blood troll capital</a> that saw to the death of <a href="/wiki/Grand_Ma%27da_Ateena" title="Grand Ma&#39;da Ateena">Grand Ma'da Ateena</a>, the matriarch of the blood trolls, and Taloc being blasted into <a href="/wiki/Uldir" title="Uldir">Uldir</a> itself.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
