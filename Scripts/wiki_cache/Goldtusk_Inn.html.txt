<p>The <b>Goldtusk Inn</b> is an <a href="/wiki/Inn" title="Inn">inn</a> owned by <a href="/wiki/Rhan%27ka" title="Rhan&#39;ka">Rhan'ka</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Gozda&#39;kun&#95;1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gozda&#39;kun-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and staffed by the <a href="/wiki/Goldtusk_Gang" title="Goldtusk Gang">Goldtusk Gang</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Gozda&#39;kun&#95;1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gozda&#39;kun-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Meet&#95;2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meet-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> inside a cave on the northeast side of <a href="/wiki/Whistlebloom_Oasis" title="Whistlebloom Oasis">Whistlebloom Oasis</a> in <a href="/wiki/Vol%27dun" title="Vol&#39;dun">Vol'dun</a>. According to Rhan'ka, "All exiles are welcome to rest at de Goldtusk Inn, de only luxury lodge in dese harsh dunes".<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Meet&#95;2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meet-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Guests at the inn need to earn their keep. The inn offers several excursion activities and packages, such as the "Warlord Package" (which is required for access to excursions to <a href="/wiki/Dead_Man%27s_Pass" title="Dead Man&#39;s Pass">Dead Man's Pass</a>), the "Scorpid King Package" (unlimited all-day access to Rhan'ka's famous <a href="/wiki/Scorpid" title="Scorpid">scorpid</a> blood drinks),<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and the "Giant Deadly Unstoppable Killer Cobra Experience" (an activity which challenges guests to travel to the dunes north of the inn and try not to be killed by <a href="/wiki/Sevriss" title="Sevriss">Sevriss</a>).<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a> first arrive at the inn, it offers no commodities and the visitors are only able to see the seemingly insane Rhan'ka talking to skulls.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Meet&#95;2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Meet-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After Rhan'ka sends the adventurers to <a href="/wiki/Dead_Man%27s_Pass" title="Dead Man&#39;s Pass">Dead Man's Pass</a>, <a href="/wiki/Farraki_tribe" title="Farraki tribe">Sandfury</a> raiders led by <a href="/wiki/Gozda%27kun_the_Slaver" title="Gozda&#39;kun the Slaver">Gozda'kun the Slaver</a> thrash the inn and kidnap the Goldtusk Gang (leading Rhan'ka to officially ban the tribe from the inn).<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Gozda&#39;kun&#95;1-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Gozda&#39;kun-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After adventurers rescue the skulls, Rhan'ka conducts a ritual to initiate them into the Goldtusk Gang and allow them to see what the gang sees, revealing that most of the Goldtusk Gang members are actually ghosts that were previously invisible.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After adventurers help the various gang members with tasks around the inn — specifically by helping <a href="/wiki/Man%27zul" title="Man&#39;zul">Man'zul</a> create and plant a "gold tusk" above the inn's entrance,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> clearing the oasis of predators for <a href="/wiki/Volni" title="Volni">Volni</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and gathering fruit for <a href="/wiki/Zulsan" title="Zulsan">Zulsan</a>'s all-you-can-eat breakfast buffet<sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> — the place receives its first guest in the form of the injured <a href="/wiki/Bladeguard_Tarkaj" title="Bladeguard Tarkaj">Bladeguard Tarkaj</a>, who is offered food, drink, bandages, and bedding by the adventurer. Afterward, Rhan'ka declares that the inn is officially open.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Notes">Notes</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Goldtusk_Inn?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Notes"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>The inn contains an interactable <a href="/wiki/Drum_(Vol%27dun)?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Drum (Vol&#39;dun) (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Drum</a> and a Cooking Cauldron in which Rhan'ka hides during part of the questline.</li>
<li>The commodities at the inn are not initially available and instead have to be unlocked through quests. Volni's flight path unlocks after <span class="questlink"><span class="questlong-prefix linkicon"><img alt="H" src="/images/Horde_15.png?c852a8" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="15" height="15" data-file-width="15" data-file-height="15" />&#160;[30-60]&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/The_Best_Kill_is_Overkill" title="The Best Kill is Overkill"><span class="ajaxttlink questlink">The Best Kill is Overkill</span></a></span>, and Grenja, Man'zul, and Rhan'ka only offer their services after completion of <span class="questlink"><span class="questlong-prefix linkicon"><img alt="H" src="/images/Horde_15.png?c852a8" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="15" height="15" data-file-width="15" data-file-height="15" />&#160;[30-60]&#160;</span><a href="/wiki/A_Goldtusk_Greeting" title="A Goldtusk Greeting"><span class="ajaxttlink questlink">A Goldtusk Greeting</span></a></span>. A Goldtusk Greeting also adds an <a href="/wiki/Anvil" title="Anvil">anvil</a>, <a href="/wiki/Forge" title="Forge">forge</a>, and <a href="/wiki/Mailbox" class="mw-redirect" title="Mailbox">Goldtusk Inn Mailbox</a> outside the inn's entrance.</li></ul>
