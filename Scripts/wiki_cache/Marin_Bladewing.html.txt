<p><b>Marin Bladewing</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Night_elf" title="Night elf">night elf</a> <a href="/wiki/Warden" title="Warden">warden</a> serving under <a href="/wiki/Maiev_Shadowsong" title="Maiev Shadowsong">Maiev Shadowsong</a>. She took part in the <a href="/wiki/Firelands_Invasion" title="Firelands Invasion">Firelands Invasion</a> as part of the <a href="/wiki/Shadow_Wardens" title="Shadow Wardens">Shadow Wardens</a>. She later joined the <a href="/wiki/The_Wardens" class="mw-redirect" title="The Wardens">Wardens</a> in resisting the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> from <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>, under the command of <a href="/wiki/Sira_Moonwarden" title="Sira Moonwarden">Sira Moonwarden</a> and Maiev. At the time of the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>, Marin and her fellow wardens committed themselves to the <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a> cause, and fought alongside their leader in <a href="/wiki/Battle_for_Darkshore" title="Battle for Darkshore">the campaign</a> to drive the <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> from <a href="/wiki/Darkshore" title="Darkshore">Darkshore</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Marin_Bladewing?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Cataclysm">Cataclysm</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Marin_Bladewing?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Cataclysm"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm" title="Cataclysm"><img alt="Cataclysm" src="/images/Cata-Logo-Small.png?cd16d8" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="39" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="39" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm" title="World of Warcraft: Cataclysm">Cataclysm</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:242px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Marin_Bladewing.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Marin_Bladewing.jpg/240px-Marin_Bladewing.jpg?2ed0d2" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="397" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="648" data-file-height="1071" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Marin_Bladewing.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Marin on the <a href="/wiki/Molten_Front" title="Molten Front">Molten Front</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>Following the <a href="/wiki/Cataclysm_(event)" title="Cataclysm (event)">Cataclysm</a>, Marin originally appeared as a <a href="/wiki/Shadow_Wardens" title="Shadow Wardens">Shadow Warden</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Forlorn_Spire" title="Forlorn Spire">Forlorn Spire</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Molten_Front" title="Molten Front">Molten Front</a>. There, she took part in the <a href="/wiki/Firelands_Invasion" title="Firelands Invasion">campaign</a> against the Fire Lord, <a href="/wiki/Ragnaros" title="Ragnaros">Ragnaros</a>, and his minions, in order to save all of <a href="/wiki/Mount_Hyjal" title="Mount Hyjal">Mount Hyjal</a> from destruction. She was accompanied in this quest by a number of other wardens and <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a>, and appeared to be in command of her fellow sisters.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-The&#95;Wardens&#95;are&#95;Watching&#95;1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-The_Wardens_are_Watching-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> After slaying <a href="/wiki/Fire_elemental" title="Fire elemental">fire elementals</a>, they took the <a href="/wiki/Forlorn_Spire" title="Forlorn Spire">Forlorn Spire</a>, after which she sensed <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Flame" title="Druids of the Flame">Druids of the Flame</a> nearby.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Therefore, she requested to strike at and destroy the druids' <a href="/wiki/Solar_Core?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Solar Core (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">Solar Core</a> that had the potential of causing massive damage to Azeroth<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and ordered the capture of one of the druids for questioning in order to learn of their plans, and sent a <a href="/wiki/Shadow_Warden" title="Shadow Warden">warden</a> and the adventurer to do it.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-The&#95;Wardens&#95;are&#95;Watching&#95;1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-The_Wardens_are_Watching-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Afterward, she asked the adventurer with assisting <a href="/wiki/Shalis_Darkhunter" title="Shalis Darkhunter">Shalis Darkhunter</a> and <a href="/wiki/Theresa_Barkskin" title="Theresa Barkskin">Theresa Barkskin</a> in their mission in the <a href="/wiki/Igneous_Depths" title="Igneous Depths">Igneous Depths</a> and specifically tasked them to disrupt the <a href="/wiki/Druids_of_the_Flame" title="Druids of the Flame">Druids of the Flame</a>'s ritual by destroying their <a href="/wiki/Flame_Protection_Rune?action=edit&amp;redlink=1" class="new" title="Flame Protection Rune (page does not exist)" rel="nofollow">summoning runes</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Warlords_of_Draenor">Warlords of Draenor</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Marin_Bladewing?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Warlords of Draenor"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Warlords_of_Draenor" title="Warlords of Draenor"><img alt="Warlords of Draenor" src="/images/WoD-Logo-Small.png?be5b60" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="46" height="14" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="46" data-file-height="14" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Warlords_of_Draenor" title="World of Warcraft: Warlords of Draenor">Warlords of Draenor</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/War_in_Draenor" title="War in Draenor">war in Draenor</a>, Marin was part of a contingent of <a href="/wiki/Warden" title="Warden">wardens</a> sent by <a href="/wiki/Maiev_Shadowsong" title="Maiev Shadowsong">Maiev Shadowsong</a> to protect <a href="/wiki/Archmage_Khadgar" class="mw-redirect" title="Archmage Khadgar">Archmage Khadgar</a> as he worked against the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> on <a href="/wiki/Alternate_Draenor" class="mw-redirect" title="Alternate Draenor">alternate Draenor</a>. Led by <a href="/wiki/Cordana_Felsong" title="Cordana Felsong">Cordana Felsong</a>, the group was stationed across the continent, with Marin herself being found in <a href="/wiki/Starfall_Outpost" title="Starfall Outpost">Starfall Outpost</a> in <a href="/wiki/Shadowmoon_Valley_(alternate_universe)" title="Shadowmoon Valley (alternate universe)">Shadowmoon Valley</a> and <a href="/wiki/Yrel%27s_Watch" title="Yrel&#39;s Watch">Yrel's Watch</a> in <a href="/wiki/Nagrand_(alternate_universe)" title="Nagrand (alternate universe)">Nagrand</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legion">Legion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Marin_Bladewing?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Legion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>By the time of the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion</a> of the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>, Marin and her <a href="/wiki/The_Wardens" class="mw-redirect" title="The Wardens">fellow Wardens</a> had reestablished themselves on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a>, where they guarded the <a href="/wiki/Vault_of_the_Wardens" title="Vault of the Wardens">Vault of the Wardens</a> against all threats. The <a href="/wiki/Demon" title="Demon">demons</a>' attack on the facility, aided by <a href="/wiki/Cordana_Felsong" title="Cordana Felsong">Cordana Felsong</a> herself, succeeded in breaching the Vault. Marin would act as <a href="/wiki/Quartermaster" title="Quartermaster">quartermaster</a> for the organization from <a href="/wiki/Wardens%27_Redoubt" title="Wardens&#39; Redoubt">Wardens' Redoubt</a> in <a href="/wiki/Azsuna" title="Azsuna">Azsuna</a>, from which she and her sisters fought desperately to drive back the Legion's forces.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Battle_for_Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Marin_Bladewing?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Battle for Azeroth"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="Battle for Azeroth"><img alt="Battle for Azeroth" src="/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Wardenlineup_alternate.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Wardenlineup_alternate.jpg/320px-Wardenlineup_alternate.jpg?d8c29a" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="205" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="961" data-file-height="615" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Wardenlineup_alternate.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Marin Bladewing and the Wardens in <a href="/wiki/Darkshore" title="Darkshore">Darkshore</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>Warden Bladewing and her sisters would answer the call of <a href="/wiki/Maiev_Shadowsong" title="Maiev Shadowsong">Maiev Shadowsong</a> and <a href="/wiki/Tyrande_Whisperwind" title="Tyrande Whisperwind">Tyrande Whisperwind</a>, as the <a href="/wiki/Kaldorei" class="mw-redirect" title="Kaldorei">kaldorei</a> prepared to retake <a href="/wiki/Darkshore" title="Darkshore">Darkshore</a> from its <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> occupiers during the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>. They would venture with Maiev to <a href="/wiki/Bashal%27Aran" title="Bashal&#39;Aran">Bashal'Aran</a>, where they defended High Priestess Tyrande during her ritual to become the <a href="/wiki/Night_Warrior" title="Night Warrior">Night Warrior</a>, and subsequently witnessed her transformation.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, Marin and her sisters took part in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_for_Darkshore" title="Battle for Darkshore">Battle for Darkshore</a> alongside other <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a> forces.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Dragonflight">Dragonflight</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Marin_Bladewing?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Dragonflight"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><span class="icon df-inline"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight" title="Dragonflight"><img alt="Dragonflight" src="/images/thumb/Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png?be4c0e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="48" height="17" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight" title="World of Warcraft: Dragonflight">Dragonflight</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>Years later, Marin and her fellow wardens attended the gathering at <a href="/wiki/Amirdrassil" title="Amirdrassil">Amirdrassil</a> when it bloomed on the <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Isles" title="Dragon Isles">Dragon Isles</a> after <a href="/wiki/Fyrakk" title="Fyrakk">Fyrakk</a>'s death. Afterward, she can be seen speaking with <a href="/wiki/Arko%27narin_Starshade" title="Arko&#39;narin Starshade">Arko'narin Starshade</a> in <a href="/wiki/Arlithrien_Lodge" title="Arlithrien Lodge">Arlithrien Lodge</a>.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Marin_Bladewing?action=edit&amp;section=12" title="Edit section: Trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>

<ul><li>As of <i>Legion</i>, Marin has also appeared accompanied by a <a href="/wiki/Sentinel_Owl_(NPC)" title="Sentinel Owl (NPC)">Sentinel Owl</a>.</li></ul></li></ul>
