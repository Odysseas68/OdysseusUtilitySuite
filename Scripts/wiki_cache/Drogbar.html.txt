<p>The <b>drogbar</b> (<a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Phonetic_Alphabet" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:International Phonetic Alphabet"><span title="Representation in the International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA)" class="IPA">/ˈdɹɔɡ.bɑɹ/</span></a> <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Pronunciation_respelling_key" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Help:Pronunciation respelling key"><span title="English Pronunciation Respelling" class="IPA">DRAWG-bar</span></a></i>) are a <a href="/wiki/Shaman" title="Shaman">shamanistic</a> <a href="/wiki/Humanoid" title="Humanoid">humanoid</a> <a href="/wiki/Race" title="Race">species</a> that dwell underground, mainly found in the infinite caverns deep within <a href="/wiki/Highmountain" title="Highmountain">Highmountain</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Broken_Isles" title="Broken Isles">Broken Isles</a><sup id="cite&#95;ref-Zone&#95;preview&#95;2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Zone_preview-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and in <a href="/wiki/Zaralek_Cavern" title="Zaralek Cavern">Zaralek Cavern</a> on the <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Isles" title="Dragon Isles">Dragon Isles</a>. Their brutish appearance can hide their cleverness.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Having once been the <a href="/wiki/Slave" class="mw-redirect" title="Slave">slaves</a> of <a href="/wiki/Deathwing" title="Deathwing">Deathwing</a> himself, the drogbar have long lived in relative peace with the <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_tauren" title="Highmountain tauren">Highmountain tauren</a>, but shortly after the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>'s return the drogbar leader, <a href="/wiki/Dargrul" title="Dargrul">Dargrul</a>, stole the ancient <a href="/wiki/Hammer_of_Khaz%27goroth" title="Hammer of Khaz&#39;goroth">Hammer of Khaz'goroth</a>, initiating a war between the two races.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Background">Background</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Drogbar?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: Background"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p><a href="/wiki/Xal%27atath" title="Xal&#39;atath">Xal'atath</a> describes the drogbar as failed <a href="/wiki/Titan" title="Titan">titan</a> experiments.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The drogbar were once slaves to the <a href="/wiki/Black_dragonflight" title="Black dragonflight">black</a> <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Aspects" title="Dragon Aspects">Dragon Aspect</a> himself, <a href="/wiki/Deathwing" title="Deathwing">Neltharion</a> the Earth-Warder, and they were the ones who placed the <a href="/wiki/Goblin" title="Goblin">goblin</a>-forged <a href="/wiki/Adamantine" title="Adamantine">adamantium</a> plates on the Aspect when his body began to fall apart.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Neltharion also had the drogbar experiment with ways to merge the elements into a <a href="/wiki/Elementium" title="Elementium">binding ore</a>. He believed it would be the ultimate tool in his rise to power. There were at least fifty attempts.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Tablets&#95;1-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Tablets-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> One revolt occurred before the arrival of <a href="/wiki/Huln_Highmountain" title="Huln Highmountain">Huln Highmountain</a> but was swiftly ended by Neltharion. The revolt's leader, <a href="/wiki/The_Grey_One" title="The Grey One">The Grey One</a>, was petrified into a statue as a constant reminder of who the drogbar truly serve.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> When Huln finally arrived and used the <a href="/wiki/Hammer_of_Khaz%27goroth" title="Hammer of Khaz&#39;goroth">Hammer of Khaz'goroth</a> to banish Neltharion from the region, the drogbar were freed.
</p><p>The <a href="/wiki/Rivermane_tribe" title="Rivermane tribe">Rivermane tribe</a> were the ones to first establish a fragile peace pact between the tauren and the drogbar as a means to survive in relative peace. As part of this agreement, they mutually benefit from one another's resources through trade at designated areas outside drogbar caves. The drogbar provide spell components from inedible plants, rare minerals, gems,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Zone&#95;preview&#95;2-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Zone_preview-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> potent alchemy<sup id="cite&#95;ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and other rarities as a trade offering to the tauren, and in return, the tauren bring the drogbar grain, fish, leather goods, and lumber. They make a mutual exchange at these ritual sites, then go back to their respective homelands. It's a symbiotic relationship — they don't like each other, but they need each other.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Zone&#95;preview&#95;2-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Zone_preview-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The drogbar at one point warred against the followers of the <a href="/wiki/Vrykul" title="Vrykul">vrykul</a> warlord <a href="/wiki/Warlord_Volund" title="Warlord Volund">Volund</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>However, soon after the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a>'s arrival, a drogbar leader named <a href="/wiki/Dargrul" title="Dargrul">Dargrul the Underking</a> destroyed the unity of the two races and stole the Hammer of Khaz'goroth, a powerful artifact protected by the Highmountain tauren. Using the terrible power of the Hammer, the Underking intends to take control of Highmountain.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Not all drogbar work for Dargrul, however. The <a href="/wiki/Stonedark_clan" title="Stonedark clan">Stonedark clan</a> led by <a href="/wiki/Navarrogg" title="Navarrogg">Navarrogg</a>, in gratitude to an <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurer</a> who saved them from the <a href="/wiki/Bloodtotem_tribe" title="Bloodtotem tribe">Bloodtotem tauren</a>, joined the <a href="/wiki/Highmountain_Tribe" title="Highmountain Tribe">Highmountain Tribe</a> instead.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-10" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-10"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>10<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>There is also at least one group of neutral drogbar that had a settlement in what is now known as the <a href="/wiki/Feltotem_Caverns" title="Feltotem Caverns">Feltotem Caverns</a>, but after the Bloodtotem became the Feltotem, these drogbar were pushed out by concerted demon and Feltotem attacks.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-11" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-11"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>11<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>  Their current location is unknown.
</p><p>Drogbar that are part of the <a href="/wiki/Twilight%27s_Hammer" title="Twilight&#39;s Hammer">Twilight's Hammer</a> can be found in Highmountain's <a href="/wiki/Deep_Roads" title="Deep Roads">Deep Roads</a>. Another Old God-affiliated group of drogbar is the <a href="/wiki/Necrodark" title="Necrodark">Necrodark</a>.
</p><p>The Drogbar from Zaralek trade with the <a href="/wiki/Nerubian" title="Nerubian">nerubians</a> of <a href="/wiki/Azj-Kahet" title="Azj-Kahet">Azj-Kahet</a> far to the southwest in <a href="/wiki/Khaz_Algar" title="Khaz Algar">Khaz Algar</a>.
</p>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Culture">Culture</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Drogbar?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Culture"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<p>Drogbar are not violent by nature, despite their size.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-12" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-12"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>12<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Living in caves, the drogbar make the most of what they have. At least one particular species of <a href="/wiki/Fungus" title="Fungus">mushroom</a> is known to be gathered and prepared for consumption. Rock is used for furniture; tables are an obvious application, while "resting stones" are chairs,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Margul&#95;13-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Margul-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> and long, rectangular slabs are "sleeping stones". Stones that make different sounds when struck can used for drums, such as "<a href="/wiki/Tinkrul" title="Tinkrul">tink rocks</a>", "<a href="/wiki/Boprul" title="Boprul">bop rocks</a>" and "<a href="/wiki/Clomprul" title="Clomprul">clomp rocks</a>". The drogbar have always been masters at <a href="/wiki/Jewelcrafting" title="Jewelcrafting">jewelcrafting</a>. In particular, tribal <a href="/wiki/Shaman" title="Shaman">shaman</a> would wear heavy, three-stoned necklaces. These necklaces were passed down from master to student.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-14" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-14"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>14<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>With their powers over the earth,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-15" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-15"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>15<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> the drogbar know how to carve stone into containers such as cups and shallow bowls, to ones with lids. With a channel carved around the outside for a leather strap, lidded containers can be small ones with a plug of wood, to large, stout ones with a matching stone lid fitted into the top. Entire waterwheels made of stone are used as a power source for simple engineering, fed by subterranean waterways that flow into the caves.  Stone conduits suspended from sturdy wooden supports embedded in the walls also partially redirect subterranean water for the peoples' convenient use.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-16" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-16"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>16<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Many of these items are most likely crafted by drogbar Stoneshapers, who are also known to calcify the dead bodies of important drogbar to be commemorated as statues.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-17" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-17"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>17<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>While most drogbar appreciate the rough edges of a raw gemstone, others prefer a smoother, rounder jewel. Rather than using standard gemcutting tools, they use a hand-cranked stone device, which tumbles the gems over the course of several weeks. These are typically operated by servants, slaves, or disobedient youngsters.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-18" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-18"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>18<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>For some, snail racing (including a racing circuit) is a popular pastime.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-19" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-19"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>19<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Snails are also used as a culinary ingredient at least by the Stonedark (whether they are the same species as the ones used for racing is unknown).<sup id="cite&#95;ref-20" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-20"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>20<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>There are rumors that the ancient drogbar collected powder from the residue of dragon's breath.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-21" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-21"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>21<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The Basilisk Bazaar is an event held by the drogbar where they trade, fight and skin their lizard pets.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-22" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-22"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>22<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Worms">Worms</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Drogbar?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Worms"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<p>The drogbar have a close kinship with Highmountain's giant <a href="/wiki/Worm" title="Worm">worms</a>. They use caves such as the <a href="/wiki/Rockcrawler_Chasm" title="Rockcrawler Chasm">Rockcrawler Chasm</a> to train and breed fledgling rockcrawlers that in time grow in size and strength, morphing into monstrous siege worms that the drogbar ride into battle.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-25" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-25"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>25<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-26" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-26"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>26<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The drogbar feed their fledgling worms <a href="/wiki/Eagle" title="Eagle">eagle</a> eggs so they'll grow strong enough to serve as mounts.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-27" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-27"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>27<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>An enormous worm called <a href="/wiki/Naraxas" title="Naraxas">Naraxas</a>, mother of monsters, has been worshipped by the drogbar since the age of Neltharion. Constant sacrifices are made to the creature so she can nurture the generations of horror the drogbar use against their enemies.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-28" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-28"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>28<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> During the battle of <a href="/wiki/Snowblind_Mesa" title="Snowblind Mesa">Snowblind Mesa</a>, the drogbar used spawn of Naraxas to burrow their way to the surface and deploy their troops.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-29" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-29"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>29<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The great worm <a href="/wiki/Jorhuttam" title="Jorhuttam">Jorhuttam</a> is worshipped by the <a href="/wiki/Frostcrag_tribe" title="Frostcrag tribe">Frostcrag tribe</a> of drogbar.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-30" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-30"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>30<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>

<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Language">Language</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Drogbar?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Language"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<dl><dd><span class="noexcerpt" data-nosnippet=""><i>Main article: <a href="/wiki/Drogbar_(language)" title="Drogbar (language)">Drogbar (language)</a></i></span></dd></dl>
<p>Not much is known about the drogbar language. One of the more common terms is "brul", apparently an honorific.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-Margul&#95;13-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-Margul-13"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>13<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-31" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-31"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>31<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-32" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-32"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>32<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-33" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-33"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>33<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>The drogbar language shares one term with <a href="/wiki/Draconic" title="Draconic">Draconic</a>.

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Drogbar?action=edit&amp;section=8" title="Edit section: Trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>Drogbar seem to be inspired by <i><a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeons_%26_Dragons" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Dungeons &amp; Dragons">Dungeons &amp; Dragons</a>'</i>s <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bugbear_(Dungeons_%26_Dragons)" class="extiw" title="wikipedia:Bugbear (Dungeons &amp; Dragons)">bugbears</a>. Early drogbar concept art depicted them as bear-like creatures, before evolving into something more akin to D&amp;D's bugbears, and some drogbar image files are labeled as "bugbears".<sup id="cite&#95;ref-34" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-34"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>34<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-35" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-35"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>35<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-36" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-36"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>36<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-37" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-37"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>37<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li>
<li>Drogbar use the same model skeleton and animations as post-<a href="/wiki/Patch_5.2.0" title="Patch 5.2.0">5.2</a> <a href="/wiki/Dire_troll" title="Dire troll">dire trolls</a> and <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Warlords_of_Draenor" title="World of Warcraft: Warlords of Draenor">Warlords of Draenor</a></i> <a href="/wiki/Dire_orc" title="Dire orc">dire orcs</a>.</li></ul>
<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Speculation">Speculation</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Drogbar?action=edit&amp;section=9" title="Edit section: Speculation"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<table style="background: transparent;">

<tbody><tr>
<td><a href="/wiki/File:Questionmark-medium.png" class="image"><img alt="Questionmark-medium.png" src="/images/Questionmark-medium.png?4f580e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="41" height="55" data-file-width="41" data-file-height="55" /></a>
</td>
<td valign="top"><div style="border-top: 1px solid #808080; border-bottom: 1px solid #808080; padding: 1ex 1em; margin-top: 0.3ex; max-width: 34em;"><small style="line-height: 120%;">This article or section includes speculation, observations or opinions possibly supported by lore or by Blizzard officials. <b>It should not be taken as representing official lore.</b></small></div>
</td></tr></tbody></table>
<ul><li>Considering the <a href="/wiki/Twilight%27s_Hammer" title="Twilight&#39;s Hammer">Twilight's Hammer</a> have access to subterranean tunnels under the <a href="/wiki/Great_Sea" title="Great Sea">Great Sea</a>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-38" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-38"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>38<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> it is possible that the cult met the drogbar of <a href="/wiki/Highmountain" title="Highmountain">Highmountain</a> that way and enrolled some of them.</li>
<li>Since <a href="/wiki/Xal%27atath" title="Xal&#39;atath">Xal'atath</a> has implied that the drogbar are a failed titan experiment, they could have evolved from the <a href="/wiki/Troll" title="Troll">trolls</a>.
<ul><li>In the canceled <i><a href="/wiki/Warcraft_Adventures:_Lord_of_the_Clans" title="Warcraft Adventures: Lord of the Clans">Warcraft Adventures</a></i>, <a href="/wiki/Deathwing" title="Deathwing">Deathwing</a> was served by trolls which had mottled; grey skin, a concept that was reprised in <i>The Sundering</i>,<sup id="cite&#95;ref-39" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-39"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>39<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> a description that might have inspired the existence of the drogbar.</li>
<li>One quest calls them "rock trolls".<sup id="cite&#95;ref-40" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-40"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>40<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup></li></ul></li></ul>
