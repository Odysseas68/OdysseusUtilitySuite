<p>The <b>Isle of Fangs</b> is a small island off the southern coast of <a href="/wiki/Zuldazar" title="Zuldazar">Zuldazar</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Bay_of_Kings" title="Bay of Kings">Bay of Kings</a>. It's the southernmost point of <a href="/wiki/Zandalar" title="Zandalar">Zandalar</a>. Like <a href="/wiki/Tusk_Isle" title="Tusk Isle">Tusk Isle</a> just to the north, the Isle of Fangs is used as a <a href="/wiki/Port" title="Port">port</a> and outpost by the <a href="/wiki/Zandalari_Empire" title="Zandalari Empire">Zandalari Empire</a>, with part of the <a href="/wiki/Golden_Fleet" title="Golden Fleet">Golden Fleet</a> being moored here. The island also has its own <a href="/wiki/Watch_tower" title="Watch tower">watch tower</a> which can be used to send signals across the bay.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Isle_of_Fangs?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="Battle for Azeroth"><img alt="Battle for Azeroth" src="/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>The Isle of Fangs is the farthest out of the various ports in the <a href="/wiki/Bay_of_Kings" title="Bay of Kings">Bay of Kings</a> that have been the historic home of the <a href="/wiki/Zandalari" class="mw-redirect" title="Zandalari">Zandalari</a> <a href="/wiki/Golden_Fleet" title="Golden Fleet">Golden Fleet</a>. A large portion of the fleeting, including one of its most powerful <a href="/wiki/Ship" class="mw-redirect" title="Ship">ships</a> known as the <i><a href="/wiki/Breath_of_Pa%27ku" title="Breath of Pa&#39;ku">Breath of Pa'ku</a></i>, was stationed here at the time of the <a href="/wiki/War_in_Zandalar" title="War in Zandalar">war in Zandalar</a>. Eventually, mutineers loyal to <a href="/wiki/Prophet_Zul" class="mw-redirect" title="Prophet Zul">Prophet Zul</a> would seize control of the fleet as it sat in harbor, though the <a href="/wiki/Battleship" title="Battleship">battleships</a> were soon reclaimed by Zandalari and <a href="/wiki/Horde" title="Horde">Horde</a> forces. After this victory against the traitors and their <a href="/wiki/Mogu" title="Mogu">mogu</a> allies was complete, the final <a href="/wiki/Loa" title="Loa">loa</a>-blessed signal flame was lit on the Isle of Fangs' watchtower, decisively marking the success as complete.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>Later, as part of their <a href="/wiki/Alliance_War_Campaign" title="Alliance War Campaign">war campaign</a> on Zandalar, <a href="/wiki/Alliance" title="Alliance">Alliance</a> forces would infiltrate the Isle of Fangs and the ships docked there. These agents recovered important military information from the <i>Breath of Pa'ku</i> before secretly sabotaging the hull of each ship with special <a href="/wiki/Explosive" title="Explosive">explosives</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> The act of mining these battleships was then repeated on <a href="/wiki/Tusk_Isle" title="Tusk Isle">Tusk Isle</a> and at <a href="/wiki/Warport_Rastari" title="Warport Rastari">Warport Rastari</a>, in preparation for an <a href="/wiki/Battle_of_Dazar%27alor" title="Battle of Dazar&#39;alor">impending attack</a> on <a href="/wiki/Dazar%27alor" title="Dazar&#39;alor">Dazar'alor</a> itself.
</p>
