<p>The term <b>quartermaster</b> has a number of meanings in <i>World of Warcraft</i>, mainly associated with <i>faction quartermasters</i>.  These are <a href="/wiki/NPC" class="mw-redirect" title="NPC">NPCs</a> who will sell items to players that have accumulated a sufficient <a href="/wiki/Reputation" title="Reputation">reputation</a> level with a particular <a href="/wiki/Faction" title="Faction">faction</a>.  They also sell <a href="/wiki/Championing" title="Championing">championing</a> tabards, which allow the player to gain reputation with a faction in level-relevant dungeons. Many vendors added in <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm" title="World of Warcraft: Cataclysm">World of Warcraft: Cataclysm</a></i>, and accepting <a href="/wiki/Currencies" class="mw-redirect" title="Currencies">currencies</a> other than gold, are also called quartermasters.
</p><p>The term quartermaster has been increasingly used since <i><a href="/wiki/The_Burning_Crusade" class="mw-redirect" title="The Burning Crusade">The Burning Crusade</a></i> expansion when each of the dungeon reputation factions had an NPC identified by name as the quartermaster.  The term is commonly applied retroactively to classic vendors who served a similar purpose.
</p><p>Typically the items available at <a href="/wiki/Exalted" class="mw-redirect" title="Exalted"><span class="rep-exalted" title="Exalted">Exalted</span></a> are of a high quality, and may include rare cosmetic rewards such as <a href="/wiki/Mounts" class="mw-redirect" title="Mounts">Mounts</a> or <a href="/wiki/Companions" class="mw-redirect" title="Companions">Companions</a>, or unique <a href="/wiki/Profession" title="Profession">profession</a> recipes.
</p>
