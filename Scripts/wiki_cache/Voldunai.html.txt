<p>The <b>Voldunai</b> is a <a href="/wiki/Faction" title="Faction">faction</a> of <a href="/wiki/Vulpera" title="Vulpera">vulpera</a> and <a href="/wiki/Zandalari_troll" title="Zandalari troll">Zandalari</a> exiles found in <a href="/wiki/Vol%27dun" title="Vol&#39;dun">Vol'dun</a>. "Voldunai" seems to be a general name for most non-hostile, non-<a href="/wiki/Sethrak" title="Sethrak">sethrak</a> inhabitants of Vol'dun, encompassing everything from vulpera <a href="/wiki/Caravan" title="Caravan">caravans</a> and the worshippers of <a href="/wiki/Akunda" title="Akunda">Akunda</a>, to individual groups of Zandalari like those at the <a href="/wiki/Goldtusk_Inn" title="Goldtusk Inn">Goldtusk Inn</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Scorched_Sands_Outpost" title="Scorched Sands Outpost">Scorched Sands Outpost</a>.
</p><p><a href="/wiki/Reins_of_the_Alabaster_Hyena" title="Reins of the Alabaster Hyena">Alabaster hyenas</a> are used by the Voldunai to quickly move unseen throughout the sands.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
