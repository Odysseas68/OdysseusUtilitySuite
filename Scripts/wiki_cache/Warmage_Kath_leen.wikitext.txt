'''Warmage Kath'leen''' is a [[human]] located near the [[Violet Citadel]] in [[Dalaran]]. She can provide directions for various locations in the [[Dalaran]].

It is currently unknown if she survived Dalaran's destruction.

{{legion-section}}
With the invasion of the [[Burning Legion]], she joined the effort of the [[Armies of Legionfall]] as one of their artificers.
