{{QuoteWiki|There was once a place on Azeroth where nature was in perfect balance, a place so peaceful it might have been mistaken for the Emerald Dream. Druids sought enlightenment among the vibrant forests of Val'sharah for millennia, but now the region has fallen under the gaze of evil. A nightmare is spreading unchecked, and none are immune to its corruption.|[[The Art of World of Warcraft: Legion]]'', pg. 67}}

'''Val'sharah''' ({{IPA|/ˈvæl.ʃəˌɹɑː/}} {{respell|VAL-shə-rah}}), sometimes spelled as '''Val'Sharah''',<ref>[[Feline Frantic]]</ref><ref>[[The Dreamweavers (quest)]]</ref> is a [[zone|region]] in the northwestern area of the [[Broken Isles]]. It is covered by a thick forest, and is the cradle of [[night elf|night elven]] [[druid|druidic]] culture. It is home to the [[World Tree]] [[Shaladrassil]]. The zone is currently contested by [[Xavius]] and his [[satyrs]] attempting to spread the corruption of the [[Emerald Nightmare]].<ref name="GamesCon">2015-08-08, [http://warcraft.blizzplanet.com/blog/comments/gamescom-2015-world-of-warcraft-legion-story-and-setting-transcript GamesCom 2015 World of Warcraft: Legion Story and Setting Transcript]. ''Blizzplanet'', retrieved on 2015-08-22</ref>

==History==
===Early history===
Originally, this ancient forest lay within the great and undivided realm of [[Kalimdor (landmass)|Kalimdor]]. It was best known as the home to the [[demigod]] [[Cenarius]], the Lord of the Forest. It was within this region that Cenarius taught the [[night elf|night elves]] the way of [[druid]]ism, with [[Malfurion Stormrage]], who was born in [[Lorlathil]], becoming the first druid under his tutelage.<ref name="GamesCon"/> Val'sharah can thus be regarded as the birthplace of druidism.<ref name="Tranquil">[[The Tranquil Forest]]</ref>

Soon, the region became the pinnacle of druidism on [[Azeroth]].<ref name="GamesCon"/> Its forests were specifically molded by druids to be a reflection of the [[Emerald Dream]] – no more than an echo, but as close to the Dream as the real world could ever be.<ref name="Cenarius">[[Cenarius, Keeper of the Grove]]</ref> The forest was considered to be sacred by the druids,<ref name="Tranquil"/> and was populated by night elves, the children of Cenarius ([[dryad]]s and [[keeper of the grove|keepers of the grove]]), as well as [[furbolg]]s, [[moonkin]] and others. The elves considered these races their allies.<ref>[[Grell in a Handbasket]]</ref>

At some point thousands of years ago, the [[World Tree]] [[Shaladrassil]] was planted here. Over the ages, it grew, towering over the area and pushing its roots deep into the land. It became the protector of the region.<ref name="Die">[[The Die is Cast]]</ref> The druid base of [[Shala'nir]] was established at the base of the tree.<ref name="PPV">[[Powering the Portal: Val'sharah]]</ref>

Apart from civilian settlements, the region also became the home to one of the most famous night elven fortresses – [[Black Rook Hold]]. This mighty but somber keep was chiseled into the solid rock by the [[House of Ravencrest]] over multiple generations. It was a well-guarded and mostly impenetrable fortress. Relying on their military might, the Ravencrests rose steadily within the elven society, eventually being considered second to the royal house only. <ref>''[[The Well of Eternity]]'', chapter 18</ref><ref>''[[The Demon Soul]]'', chapter 2</ref> The region was also home to the elven martial city of [[Kal'delar]].<ref>[[Scroll of Town Portal: Kal'delar]]</ref>

===War of the Ancients===
The region saw heavy fighting during the [[War of the Ancients]]. It was within the forests of Val'sharah that the [[Wild God]] [[Ashamane]] fought a delaying battle against the [[Burning Legion]] forces bound for [[Suramar]]. Although she was able to kill the [[annihilan]] general [[Ronokon]], the being's self-destruction immolated Ashamane and burned a crater into the forest. The Wild God's actions brought a precious time for the [[Suramar City|city of Suramar]], allowing them to raise a protective shield. She thus saved countless lives. The elves eventually erected a [[Ashamane's Fall|great shrine]] at the place of Ashamane's sacrifice.<ref name="Tome">[[Tome of the Ancients]]</ref><ref name="Reveal">{{ref web|url=http://us.battle.net/wow/en/blog/19950403/|title=Legion: Druid Artifact Reveal|accessdate=2015-11-08|author=Blizzard Entertainment|date=2015-10-23|publisher=Blizzard Entertainment}}</ref>

The War of the Ancients also saw a demonic attack on Black Rook Hold. Lord [[Kur'talos Ravencrest]] and [[Illidan Stormrage]] led the [[Moon Guard]] in the defense of the fortress. They eventually succeeded, albeit only at the price of Illidan sacrificing the lives of the other Moon Guard members to absorb their energy. Famous combatants in the battle included [[Broxigar the Red]], [[Jarod Shadowsong]], and [[Rhonin]].<ref>[[Ravencrest's Legacy (quest)]]</ref>

The Legion also tried, and failed, to destroy the [[Temple of Elune (Val'sharah)|Temple of Elune]] located within the [[Crescent Vale]].<ref name="Lyanis">[[Lyanis Moonfall]]</ref> The war ended with the [[Great Sundering]], which blasted Kalimdor into pieces. Val'sharah broke off from the mainland and mostly survived as part of the isle group known as the [[Broken Isles]], along with [[Azsuna]], Suramar, and others. Suramar City cut itself off from the surrounding land behind a protective barrier, further isolating Val'sharah from the outside world. In essence, the ancient forest was forgotten by the outside world with the exception of druids.<ref name="GamesCon"/> After the war, the surviving [[satyr]]s of the forest were assembled together and placed into a deep hibernation under the roots of [[Shaladrassil]].<ref name="Thaon">[[Thaon Moonclaw]]</ref>

===Druid refuge===
Even after its isolation, Val'sharah has remained central to druidic culture. The main druidic organization is the order of the [[Dreamweavers]] (also called the Druids of Val'sharah), who have protected the Emerald Dream since time immemorial.<ref>[[Dreamweavers]] faction description</ref> Many of its members are ancient enough to remember [[Shaladrassil]] taking root.<ref>[[Rensar Greathoof]]</ref> After the Great Sundering, they trained under Cenarius's tutelage, and acquired unparalleled knowledge in the ways of the druids.<ref name="TTD">[[To The Dreamgrove]]</ref> Their main base of operation is the [[Dreamgrove]], which is directly linked to the [[Emerald Dreamway]], a portion of the Dream that serves as a travel hub to key locations across Azeroth. The location is protected by ancient magic, creating a sanctuary here. The leader of the Dreamweavers is archdruid [[Rensar Greathoof]].<ref name="TTD"/>

Val'sharah is also the home of several of the strongest druidic individuals alive. The demi-god Cenarius, the Lord of the Forest, lives within the Grove of Cenarius.<ref name="Cenarius"/> [[Elothir]], [[ancient of lore]], resides with his acolytes in the ancient grove of [[Thas'talah]].<ref>[[Archdruid of Lore]]</ref> A group of druids live within the [[Moonclaw Vale]], charged with the protection of Val'sharah.<ref>[[Archdruid of the Vale]]</ref> The [[Druids of the Claw]] make their home in [[Steelclaw Vale]], dedicating themselves to observing the Emerald Dream.<ref>[[Archdruid of the Claw]]</ref> The [[Druids of the Fin]] patrol [[Emerald Bay]].<ref>[[Seal Clubbing]]</ref> The [[Ashen]] druids guard the legacy of Ashamane in the [[Mistvale]].<ref>[[The Shrine of Ashamane]]</ref><ref>[[Fangs of Ashamane]]</ref> There are several [[barrow den]]s located here, including the [[Sleeper's Barrow]] in Steelclaw Vale and the [[Tel'Andu Barrow Den]] within the Dreamgrove.

The druids had several key artifacts in their possession. The Ashen guarded the [[Fangs of Ashamane]] within their shrine.<ref name="Reveal"/><ref name="Tome"/> Meanwhile a branch of [[G'Hanir]], the ancient Mother Tree, was kept in a shrine beneath the roots of Shaladrassil called the [[Circle of Spirits]].<ref>[[G'Hanir, the Mother Tree]]</ref><ref>[[In Deep Slumber]]</ref> [[Malorne's Idol]], a gift by the Wild God herself, was held at the shrine called [[Malorne's Refuge]].<ref>[[Malorne's Refuge (quest)]]</ref><ref>[[Grip of Nightmare]]</ref>

The [[Sisterhood of Elune]] is also represented within the region. Their main base of operation is the [[Temple of Elune (Val'sharah)|Temple of Elune]] within the [[Crescent Vale]], which predates the War of the Ancients.<ref name="Lyanis"/> This location also served as the hiding place for the [[Tears of Elune]], an artifact that is part of the group of relics known as the [[Pillars of Creation]].<ref name="Tears">[[Tears for Fears]]</ref>

The night elves have several villages scattered throughout the area. These include the travel hub of [[Lorlathil]], [[Ash'theran]], [[Lightsong]], [[Shadowfen]], the enclave of [[Shala'nir]]<ref>[[Close Enough to Touch]]</ref><ref>[[Scroll of Town Portal: Shala'nir]]</ref> and others. Several other sentient races also inhabit the forest, including the [[Cenarius's children]], [[furbolg]], [[moonkin]], [[harpy|harpies]] and [[grell]]. Recently a new village, [[Bradensbrook]], was founded on the west coast by uncorrupted humans from [[Gilneas (kingdom)|Gilneas]].<ref>[[Bastion of Bradensbrook]]</ref> Val'sharah has a very vivid fauna and flora.

===Legion===
{{Legion-section}}
During the Burning Legion's [[Third invasion of the Burning Legion|third invasion]] of Azeroth, Val'sharah was assaulted by the satyr lord [[Xavius]] and the [[Emerald Nightmare]]. He was able to wake up the [[satyr]]s slumbering below [[Shaladrassil]],<ref name="Thaon"/> and corrupt the World Tree itself. With its roots deep within the ground, the great tree then began to corrupt the forest.<ref>[[The Emerald Queen]]</ref><ref>[[Root Cause]]</ref> The satyrs broke through and overran [[Shala'nir]].<ref name="PPV"/>

Following this, the [[Emerald Nightmare]] began to manifest itself throughout Val'sharah. Cenarius was overtaken by an affliction that caused him to lie unwaking in his grove; he was eventually successfully corrupted and yanked into the Nightmare itself. [[Malfurion Stormrage]] was captured by the satyrs while trying to free his erstwhile mentor.<ref name="Die"/> The Wild God Malorne was also lost in the Nightmare.<ref>[[Communing With Malorne]]</ref> Archdruid [[Thaon Moonclaw]] was also corrupted by Xavius, and eventually slain.<ref>[[The Nightmare Lord]]</ref> Furbolgs,<ref>[[Frenzied Furbolgs]]</ref> moonkin,<ref>[[Where the Wildkin Are]]</ref> keepers of the grove, dryads,<ref>[[Twisted Ash]]</ref><ref>[[Powering the Portal: Andu'talah]]</ref> ancients<ref>[[Darkheart Thicket: Fallen Power]]</ref> and [[sprite]]s<ref>[[Thorny Dancing]]</ref> succumbed to madness en masse.

At some point, the [[Knights of the Silver Hand|Order of the Silver Hand]] sent their troops in the region to destroy a rampaging satyr warband, led by [[Illbringer Zizzifrax]], in order to help the [[druid]]s of the [[Cenarion Circle]] who have their hands full with the [[satyr]] and the [[Emerald Nightmare|Nightmare]].<ref>[[Blood of Our Enemy: Val'sharah]]</ref> They also attacked [[naga]]s in southern Val'sharah, in order to collect their weapons crafted from [[Lumenstone]],<ref>[[Lumenstone: Val'sharah]]</ref> and later investigated about the [[furbolg]]s of the region who appear to have come under the [[demon]]ic influence of a [[man'ari eredar]], named [[Summoner Djobi]], who was taken down by the paladins.<ref>[[The Highlord's Command: Legion Defense]]</ref>

The [[Dragon Aspect]] [[Ysera]] arrived at Val'sharah to intervene, seeking to recover the [[Tears of Elune]] from the Temple of Elune to cleanse Cenarius. However, Xavius got to the artifact first,<ref name="Tears"/> and managed to corrupt Ysera herself with it.<ref>[[The Demon's Trail]]</ref> [[Tyrande Whisperwind]] tracked down the Nightmare Lord, although not before losing the archdruid Elothir to the corrupting influence.<ref>[[Reading the Leaves]]</ref> In the climactic battle at the Temple of Elune, Tyrande and her companions were forced to kill Ysera, reclaiming and cleansing the Tears of Elune in the process.<ref>[[The Fate of Val'sharah]]</ref>

These dramatic events caused heavy damage to the forest and its inhabitants. Shala'nir was completely lost to the Nightmare, its luckier inhabitants evacuating the village.<ref name="FF">[[Feline Frantic]]</ref> A similar fate befell Shadowfen, which was hastily abandoned.<ref>[[Lost in Retreat]]</ref> Whole sections of the forest, such as the area of Dreadwood and Moonrest, were lost to the affliction.<ref name ="Purge">[[Purge of the Nightmare]]</ref><ref>[[To Old Friends]]</ref> Crazed creatures began appearing throughout the land, and brazen satyrs and their allies attacked several locations. Many elves escaped to places such as [[Starsong Refuge]], or even crossed the border into Suramar. The corrupted tendrils of Shaladrassil followed them even there.<ref name="FF"/>

The Burning Legion also assaulted the region directly. The heroes of Azeroth helped the Dreamweavers and other locals repel the assault.<ref>[[Assault on Val'sharah]]</ref> [[Demon]]s attacked several key locations, including the [[Ashamane's Fall|Shrine of Ashamane]]<ref>[[Aid for the Ashen]]</ref> and [[Sundersong Glade]].<ref>[[A Glade Defense]]</ref>

A wholly different crisis emerged around [[Black Rook Hold]]. The [[warlock]] {{Au|Gul'dan}} reanimated the spirits of Lord [[Kur'talos Ravencrest]] and his soldiers,<ref>[[Jarod's Mission]]</ref><ref>[[Lieutenant of the Tower]]</ref> who started assaulting [[Bradensbrook]]. With the help of [[Jarod Shadowsong]], the villagers were eventually able to repulse this threat. Shadowsong and adventurers then pushed into Black Rook Hold itself. After heavy fighting, they were able to slay the [[dreadlord]] [[Dantalionax]], and finally put the spirits of Ravencrest's soldiers to rest.

Adventurers eventually followed Xavius into the corrupted [[Darkheart Thicket]], managing to free Malfurion Stormrage.<ref>[[Darkheart Thicket: Enter the Nightmare]]</ref> Eventually, the adventurers entered the Emerald Nightmare itself, freeing Cenarius and slaying Xavius. With the immediate danger gone, the druids began the work to cleanse the Nightmare from the land, hoping to reclaim Shala'nir and safeguard the roots of the World Tree.<ref>[[What a Nightmare]]</ref> However, it remains to be seen if the corruption can ever be fully cleansed.<ref name="Purge"/>

===Battle for Azeroth===
{{bfa-section}}
As of the [[Fourth War]], and after the [[Burning of Teldrassil]], Val'sharah and its [[Temple of Elune (Val'sharah)|Temple of Elune]] are among the few remaining night elf communities on Azeroth.

An Alliance adventurer was sent to [[Mevris Ghostdancer]] for assistance in seeking out raw materials to create the [[Drums of Primal Might]] by his wife [[Daniss Ghostdancer]], who sought a means of vengeance against the Horde for the lives they destroyed.<ref>[[The Other Ghostdancer]]</ref> The night elves of the Temple have established active trade ties with the neighboring [[Gilnean]] village of [[Bradensbrook]], sending and receiving supplies to and from the villagers via ships.<ref>[[Mevris' Menace]]</ref>

The Nightmare is starting to take hold and the satyr [[Xavius]] has designs on the Tears and [[Ysera]]. Can adventurers sort everything out in time?
