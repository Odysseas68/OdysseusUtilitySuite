<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="History">History</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Celestine_of_the_Harvest?action=edit&amp;section=1" title="Edit section: History"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:242px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Celestine.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Celestine.jpg/240px-Celestine.jpg?7ed622" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="240" height="340" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="519" data-file-height="736" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Celestine.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Celestine's human form.</div></div></div>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Cataclysm">Cataclysm</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Celestine_of_the_Harvest?action=edit&amp;section=2" title="Edit section: Cataclysm"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm" title="Cataclysm"><img alt="Cataclysm" src="/images/Cata-Logo-Small.png?cd16d8" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="39" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="39" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Cataclysm" title="World of Warcraft: Cataclysm">Cataclysm</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>At the time of the <a href="/wiki/Cataclysm_(event)" title="Cataclysm (event)">Cataclysm</a>, <a href="/wiki/Worgen" title="Worgen">worgen</a> <a href="/wiki/Druid" title="Druid">druid</a> <a href="/wiki/Adventurer" title="Adventurer">adventurers</a> first met her in <a href="/wiki/Gilneas_City_(starting_zone)" class="mw-redirect" title="Gilneas City (starting zone)">Gilneas City</a>, where she instructed them on how to use the spell <span class="nobreak"><span class="linkicon"><img alt="" src="/images/thumb/Spell_nature_rejuvenation.png/16px-Spell_nature_rejuvenation.png?8810f4" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="16" height="16" data-file-width="64" data-file-height="64" />&#32;</span><a href="/wiki/Rejuvenation" title="Rejuvenation"><span class="abilitylink ajaxttlink"><span class="bracket">&#91;</span>Rejuvenation<span class="bracket">&#93;</span></span></a></span>. At some point, she was afflicted with the <a href="/wiki/Worgen_curse" title="Worgen curse">worgen curse</a> and later moved to <a href="/wiki/Stormwind_City" title="Stormwind City">Stormwind City</a>, residing in a house next to <a href="/wiki/Olivia%27s_Pond" title="Olivia&#39;s Pond">Olivia's Pond</a>. Druid adventurers in Stormwind can visit her to train their skills.
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Firelands_Invasion" title="Firelands Invasion">Firelands Invasion</a>, Celestine helped patrol the <a href="/wiki/Regrowth_(subzone)" title="Regrowth (subzone)">Regrowth</a> in <a href="/wiki/Mount_Hyjal" title="Mount Hyjal">Mount Hyjal</a>.
</p><p>During <a href="/wiki/Hallow%27s_End" title="Hallow&#39;s End">Hallow's End</a>, Celestine is standing outside the gates of Stormwind, where she explains the significance of burning the <a href="/wiki/Wickerman" title="Wickerman">Wickerman</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Legion">Legion</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Celestine_of_the_Harvest?action=edit&amp;section=3" title="Edit section: Legion"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="Legion"><img alt="Legion" src="/images/Legion-Logo-Small.png?7a5440" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="40" data-file-height="15" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Third_invasion_of_the_Burning_Legion" title="Third invasion of the Burning Legion">third invasion of the Burning Legion</a>, Celestine worked with <a href="/wiki/Hamuul_Runetotem" title="Hamuul Runetotem">Hamuul Runetotem</a> and <a href="/wiki/Zen%27tabra" title="Zen&#39;tabra">Zen'tabra</a> to battle <a href="/wiki/Destromath" title="Destromath">Destromath</a> at the nearly-destroyed <a href="/wiki/Well_of_Eternity_(Hyjal)" title="Well of Eternity (Hyjal)">Well of Eternity</a> on Hyjal.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-1" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-1"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p><p>When <a href="/wiki/Satyr" title="Satyr">satyr</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Burning_Legion" title="Burning Legion">Burning Legion</a> attacked <a href="/wiki/Val%27sharah" title="Val&#39;sharah">Val'sharah</a>'s <a href="/wiki/Temple_of_Elune_(Val%27sharah)" title="Temple of Elune (Val&#39;sharah)">Temple of Elune</a>, Celestine went there and aided in the defense. She was then recalled by the <a href="/wiki/Dreamgrove" title="Dreamgrove">Dreamgrove</a> to assist with a ritual to commune with <a href="/wiki/Malorne" title="Malorne">Malorne</a> in <a href="/wiki/Nordrassil" title="Nordrassil">Nordrassil</a> on Hyjal.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-2" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-2"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup><sup id="cite&#95;ref-3" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-3"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Afterward, she was among the druids that entered <a href="/wiki/Malorne%27s_Nightmare" title="Malorne&#39;s Nightmare">Malorne's Nightmare</a>.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Battle_for_Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Celestine_of_the_Harvest?action=edit&amp;section=4" title="Edit section: Battle for Azeroth"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="Battle for Azeroth"><img alt="Battle for Azeroth" src="/images/thumb/BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png/40px-BattleForAzeroth-Logo-Small.png?25e36c" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="40" height="15" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Battle_for_Azeroth" title="World of Warcraft: Battle for Azeroth">Battle for Azeroth</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>During the <a href="/wiki/Fourth_War" title="Fourth War">Fourth War</a>, Celestine participated in the <a href="/wiki/Battle_for_Darkshore" title="Battle for Darkshore">Battle for Darkshore</a>. In <a href="/wiki/Bashal%27Aran" title="Bashal&#39;Aran">Bashal'Aran</a>, she is seen channeling a spell to <a href="/wiki/Shadebough_(ancient)" title="Shadebough (ancient)">Shadebough</a>. In Stormwind City, she moved out of her house and is standing next to a tree.
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Shadowlands">Shadowlands</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Celestine_of_the_Harvest?action=edit&amp;section=5" title="Edit section: Shadowlands"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><span class="icon sl-inline"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands" title="Shadowlands"><img alt="Shadowlands" src="/images/thumb/Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Shadowlands-Icon-Inline.png?d84a63" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="48" height="17" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Shadowlands" title="World of Warcraft: Shadowlands">Shadowlands</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<p>Celestine was present in <a href="/wiki/Bashal%27Aran" title="Bashal&#39;Aran">Bashal'Aran</a> and witnessed Tyrande's speech about the Horde's defeat in <a href="/wiki/Darkshore" title="Darkshore">Darkshore</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-4" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-4"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup>
</p>
<h3><span class="mw-headline" id="Dragonflight">Dragonflight</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Celestine_of_the_Harvest?action=edit&amp;section=6" title="Edit section: Dragonflight"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h3>
<div style="clear:left;"></div><p><span style="display:inline-block; min-width: 32px; text-align:center"><span class="icon df-inline"><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight" title="Dragonflight"><img alt="Dragonflight" src="/images/thumb/Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png/48px-Dragonflight-Icon-Inline.png?be4c0e" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="48" height="17" style="vertical-align: middle" data-file-width="80" data-file-height="29" /></a></span></span> <span class="greybar" style="font-size: 0.85em; padding: 0 0.2em;"><b>This section concerns content related to <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Dragonflight" title="World of Warcraft: Dragonflight">Dragonflight</a></i>.</b></span></p><div style="clear:left;"></div>
<div class="thumb tright"><div class="thumbinner" style="width:322px;"><a href="/wiki/File:Celestine_and_Vassandra_-_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg" class="image"><img src="/images/thumb/Celestine_and_Vassandra_-_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg/320px-Celestine_and_Vassandra_-_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg?c19c9d" decoding="async" loading="lazy" width="320" height="213" class="thumbimage" data-file-width="1207" data-file-height="804" /></a>  <div class="thumbcaption"><div class="magnify"><a href="/wiki/File:Celestine_and_Vassandra_-_Reclamation_of_Gilneas.jpg" class="internal" title="Enlarge"></a></div>Celestine fighting the <a href="/wiki/Scarlet_Crusade" title="Scarlet Crusade">Scarlet Crusade</a> alongside <a href="/wiki/Vassandra_Stormclaw" title="Vassandra Stormclaw">Vassandra Stormclaw</a>.</div></div></div>
<p>Following the reappearance of the <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Isles" title="Dragon Isles">Dragon Isles</a>, she traveled to the <a href="/wiki/Shady_Sanctuary" title="Shady Sanctuary">Shady Sanctuary</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Ohn%27ahran_Plains" title="Ohn&#39;ahran Plains">Ohn'ahran Plains</a> and was seen speaking with <a href="/wiki/Lyessa_Bloomwatcher" title="Lyessa Bloomwatcher">Lyessa Bloomwatcher</a>.
</p><p>She was later amongst those who came to <a href="/wiki/Alexstrasza" title="Alexstrasza">Alexstrasza</a>'s aid when she was attacked by <a href="/wiki/Fyrakk" title="Fyrakk">Fyrakk the Blazing</a>. In the battle that followed, she fought side by side with <a href="/wiki/Moira_Thaurissan" title="Moira Thaurissan">Moira Thaurissan</a> and <a href="/wiki/Genn_Greymane" title="Genn Greymane">Genn Greymane</a> at the <a href="/wiki/Field_of_Flames" title="Field of Flames">Field of Flames</a>.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-5" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-5"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> Celestine later attended the gathering in <a href="/wiki/Amirdrassil" title="Amirdrassil">Amirdrassil</a> when it bloomed on the <a href="/wiki/Dragon_Isles" title="Dragon Isles">Dragon Isles</a> after Fyrakk's death.
</p><p>During the <a href="/wiki/Reclamation_of_Gilneas" title="Reclamation of Gilneas">Reclamation of Gilneas</a>, Celestine went with <a href="/wiki/Genn_Greymane" title="Genn Greymane">King Genn Greymane</a> to <a href="/wiki/Aderic%27s_Repose" title="Aderic&#39;s Repose">Aderic's Repose</a> and was sent to tell the <a href="/wiki/Forsaken" title="Forsaken">Forsaken</a> outside the city to wait for their signal.<sup id="cite&#95;ref-6" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-6"><span class="cite-bracket">&#91;</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">&#93;</span></a></sup> She was later seen fighting the <a href="/wiki/Scarlet_Crusade" title="Scarlet Crusade">Scarlet Crusade</a> soldiers at <a href="/wiki/Cathedral_Square" title="Cathedral Square">Cathedral Square</a> alongside <a href="/wiki/Vassandra_Stormclaw" title="Vassandra Stormclaw">Vassandra Stormclaw</a>. Afterward, she can be found at <a href="/wiki/Tal%27doren" title="Tal&#39;doren">Tal'doren</a> in the <a href="/wiki/Blackwald" title="Blackwald">Blackwald</a>.
</p><p>Celestine can also be seen in <a href="/wiki/Belanaar" title="Belanaar">Belanaar</a> speaking with Vassandra.
</p>

<h2><span class="mw-headline" id="Trivia">Trivia</span><span class="mw-editsection"><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">[</span><a href="/wiki/Celestine_of_the_Harvest?action=edit&amp;section=19" title="Edit section: Trivia"><span>edit</span></a><span class="mw-editsection-bracket">]</span></span></h2>
<ul><li>With the release of <a href="/wiki/Patch_7.3.5" title="Patch 7.3.5">patch 7.3.5</a> and the <a href="/wiki/Stormwind_Embassy" title="Stormwind Embassy">Stormwind Embassy</a>, she was moved underground, rendering her unavailable but still targetable via /tar. Guards still refer to her at her old location.
<ul><li>She was later moved back overground, although guards still refer to her cottage despite her now being outdoors.</li></ul></li>
<li>Prior to patch 4.0.6, her worgen form was bugged and had the wrong female worgen texture applied to it.</li>
<li>In Stormwind, she was in human form by day and in worgen form by night, like the worgen in the pre-<i>Cataclysm</i> <a href="/wiki/Pyrewood_Village" title="Pyrewood Village">Pyrewood Village</a>.</li>
<li>Celestine is voiced by <a href="/wiki/Lauren_Tom" title="Lauren Tom">Lauren Tom</a> in <i><a href="/wiki/World_of_Warcraft:_Legion" title="World of Warcraft: Legion">Legion</a></i>.</li></ul>
